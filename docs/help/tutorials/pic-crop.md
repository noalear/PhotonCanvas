# Crop a picture
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Cut a picture down to part of it, free or to a set shape. -->
<!-- page: pictures.md#crop -->
<!-- order: 130 -->
Cropping keeps only part of a picture, freely or to a shape like square or 4:3. The part kept stays
exactly where it was on the work area.

## Select a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click a picture on the work area. No picture yet? **Import → Picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Open Crop
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Crop -->
<!-- wait: window:Crop picture -->
Click **Crop**. An orange box shows what's kept.

## Choose a shape
<!-- target: field:Shape -->
**Free**, or a shape such as **Square 1:1** or **4:3**: the other side then follows.

## Set the part to keep
<!-- target: field:Width % -->
**Left %** and **Top %** set where it starts, **Width %** and **Height %** how much is kept.

## Crop
<!-- target: button:Crop -->
<!-- wait: closed:Crop picture -->
Click **Crop**. The picture gets smaller by the part cut away.
