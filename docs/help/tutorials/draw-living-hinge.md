# Make a living hinge
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Draw rows of staggered slits that let flat plywood or MDF bend. -->
<!-- page: drawing.md#living-hinge -->
<!-- order: 64 -->
A living hinge is a pattern of staggered slits that lets a flat sheet bend. Use it for curved box
sides, covers and round corners in plywood or MDF.

## Open the generators
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Generators -->
<!-- wait: window:Generators -->
Click **Generators** on the **Home** tab.

## Choose Living hinge
<!-- target: tabitem:Living hinge -->
Click the **Living hinge** tab.

## Size it
<!-- target: field:Cut length -->
Set the area's **Width** and **Height**, the **Cut length** of each slit and the uncut **Gap** between slits.

## Set the spacing
<!-- target: field:Between rows -->
**Between rows** is the distance between lines of slits. Closer bends more easily but the strips get thinner; 1.5 mm suits 3 mm plywood.

## Make it
<!-- target: button:Make the hinge -->
<!-- wait: closed:Generators -->
Click **Make the hinge**. The slits run up and down, so the panel bends around an up-and-down line.
