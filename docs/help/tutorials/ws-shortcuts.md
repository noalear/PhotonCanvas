# Change a keyboard shortcut
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Give a command a key, change its keys, or put them back. -->
<!-- page: keyboard-shortcuts.md#changing-shortcuts -->
<!-- order: 130 -->
Most commands have a key, and the ones you use often can get one too. As an example, you'll give
**Frame** a key.

## Open the shortcuts
<!-- target: header:File -->
<!-- wait: window:Keyboard shortcuts -->
Click **File**, then **Keyboard shortcuts…**.

## Find the command
<!-- target: text:Frame -->
**Frame** is in the JOB group at the bottom. It has no key yet: its button shows "—".

## Press the new keys
<!-- target: text:Frame -->
Click the "—" beside **Frame**, then press the keys you want, such as `F5`. If another command
had them, it loses them and a note says which. `Esc` cancels.

## Put keys back
<!-- target: button:All back to the defaults -->
**Default** on a row puts that command's keys back; **All back to the defaults** puts back every
one.

## Close
<!-- target: button:Close -->
<!-- wait: closed:Keyboard shortcuts -->
Click **Close**. The new keys work straight away.
