# Combine and break apart
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Make one shape with holes from several outlines, or split a shape into its pieces. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#combine-and-break-apart -->
<!-- order: 20 -->
Combine makes several outlines one shape, and outlines inside others become its holes: a washer
from two circles. Break apart does the opposite, for drawings that arrive as one big shape.

## Draw a ring
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Ellipse -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+2 -->
Choose **Ellipse**, hold `Shift` and draw a circle. **Ellipse** goes back to **Select** after
each shape, so press `E` (or click **Ellipse**) again and draw a smaller circle inside it.

## Select both
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection:2 -->
Press `V` for **Select** and drag a box round both circles.

## Combine them
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Combine -->
Click **Combine** (`Ctrl+K`). They're one shape now, and the small circle is its hole.

## Break it apart
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Break apart -->
With the combined shape selected, click **Break apart** (`Ctrl+Shift+K`): each circle is a shape of
its own again.
