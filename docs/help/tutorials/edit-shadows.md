# Drop shadow and 3D shadow
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Add a shadow shape beside text or a shape, flat or extruded. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#drop-shadow-and-3d-shadow -->
<!-- order: 180 -->
A shadow makes lettering stand out, engraved lighter than the letters or in another colour. Drop
shadow adds the sliver beside each shape; 3D shadow a solid, extruded look.

## Select the shapes
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select a text or closed shapes.

## Choose a shadow
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/More… -->
Click **More…** and choose **Drop shadow…** or **3D shadow…**.

## Set the offset
<!-- target: text:Offset X, Y (mm) -->
Type how far the shadow sits, in mm: right, then down, such as `1.5, 1.5`. Use a point for
decimals.

## Apply
<!-- target: button:OK -->
Click **OK**. The shadow is a new shape on the same layer, selected.

## Give it its own settings
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Right-click the shadow → **Move to layer** to put it on another layer and engrave it differently.
