# See the bed under your design
<!-- category: Camera -->
<!-- summary: Lay the camera picture under the design, update it, and watch it live. -->
<!-- page: camera.md#the-camera-picture-under-your-design -->
<!-- order: 20 -->
With the camera picture under your design you can place a design on the material by eye, with the
design drawn at true size over the real bed.

## Connect and calibrate
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
This needs the machine connected and the camera calibrated (the
[Calibrate the camera](tutorial:cam-calibrate) tutorial). Put your material on the bed.

## Focus on the material
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
Click **Auto-focus** at the right end of the ribbon. The picture lines up for the height the
machine is focused at.

## Show the picture
<!-- target: ribbon:Camera/Show picture -->
Click **Show picture** on the **Camera** tab. A photo is taken and laid under the design.

## Take a new photo
<!-- target: ribbon:Camera/Update photo -->
Move the material, then click **Update photo** to see it where it is now.

## Watch it live
<!-- target: ribbon:Camera/Live view -->
Click **Live view**: the picture keeps updating, 3 to 4 photos a second. Click it again to stop.

## Make it fainter
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
Right-click **Show** at the right end of the ribbon and drag **Camera picture opacity** until the
design stands out.
