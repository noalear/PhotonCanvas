# Text on a path
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Make a line of text follow a curve or a shape's outline. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#text-on-path -->
<!-- order: 60 -->
Text can run along a curve or round a circle: a name round a badge, words along a wave. It stays
editable text.

## Write a text
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Text -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click **Text**, click on the work area and type your words in the Selection panel.

## Draw the path
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Ellipse -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Draw the circle or curve the words should follow, with **Ellipse** or **Pen**.

## Select both
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection:2 -->
With **Select**, click the text, then `Shift`+click the circle.

## Put the text on it
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Text on path -->
Click **Text on path**. The letters sit along the outline, each turned to follow it.

## Slide it along
<!-- target: panel:Selection -->
Select the text alone and change its **Align**: **Left** starts at the path's start, **Centre**
centres it, **Right** ends at the path's end.

## Don't mark the path
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
The circle would be marked too. Delete it (the text keeps its shape), or move it to a layer whose
output is off.
