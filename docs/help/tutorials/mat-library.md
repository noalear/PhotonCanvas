# Use the material library
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Keep the settings that worked for each material, and put them on a layer. -->
<!-- page: material-library.md -->
<!-- order: 10 -->
The library remembers the settings that worked on each material, with stars, notes and photos.
Start each job from settings you trust instead of guessing them again.

## Open the library
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Library -->
<!-- wait: window:Material library -->
Click **Library** on the **Laser** tab.

## Add a material
<!-- target: button:New material -->
Click **New material**. It's added to the list, ready to name.

## Describe it
<!-- target: field:Name -->
Type its name, then pick its **Category** and type its **Thickness mm**. Changes are saved as you go.

## Shiny metal?
<!-- target: button:Highly reflective -->
Tick **Highly reflective** for bare copper, brass, aluminium or mirror finishes: layers on it keep
the MOPA away from the frequencies that bounce back into the fiber.

## Put a recipe on a layer
<!-- target: button:Apply to layer -->
Select a layer in the Layers panel, then click **Apply to layer** on a recipe. Recipes come from the
tests and from **Save to library…** in a layer's settings.

## Rate it
<!-- target: button:Tested -->
Click the stars to rate a recipe, and tick **Tested** once you've burned it and checked the result.
