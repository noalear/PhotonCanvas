# See pictures as they'll burn
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Show pictures on the work area in the material's own colours, corrected by its tone test. -->
<!-- page: pictures.md#pictures-as-burned -->
<!-- order: 160 -->
A photo rarely burns the way it looks on screen. With a tone test on the material, the work area
can show each picture as it will come out, in the material's own colours.

## Run a tone test first
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Tone test -->
If the picture's layer has no tone correction yet, run **Tone test** for its material and match the
layer to it.

## Turn it on
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Pictures as burned -->
Click **Pictures as burned** on the **View** tab. Pictures on layers with a tone test now show as
they'll burn.

## Try the tone settings
<!-- target: field:Brightness -->
Select a picture and change **Brightness** or **Contrast**: the work area follows.
