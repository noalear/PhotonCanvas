# Line the MOPA up with the blue laser
<!-- category: Camera -->
<!-- summary: Both lasers burn a grid of dots, the close-up camera measures how far apart they land, and MOPA work is moved to match. -->
<!-- page: camera.md#align-lasers -->
<!-- order: 100 -->
When one design uses both lasers, the MOPA's work should land exactly where the blue laser's does.
This measures the difference with the close-up camera and corrects every job from then on.

## Connect and set up the close-up camera
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
This needs the machine connected, focused on scrap both lasers mark (anodised aluminium, black card,
coated metal or wood), and the close-up position known at that height (the
[Close-up position](tutorial:cam-close-up-position) tutorial).

## Open Align MOPA
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Align lasers -->
<!-- wait: window:Align MOPA to blue -->
Click **Align lasers** on the **Machine** tab.

## Add the dots
<!-- target: button:Add the dots to the design -->
<!-- wait: closed:Align MOPA to blue -->
Click **Add the dots to the design**: two grids of nine dots, on the "Align · blue" and
"Align · MOPA" layers.

## Set the powers
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Check each layer's power marks your scrap dark (see the Layer settings panel).

## Burn them
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Ship It -->
Click **Ship It** and press the machine's start button. Don't move the scrap afterwards.

## Measure
<!-- target: button:Take a close-up photo -->
<!-- wait: window:Align MOPA to blue -->
When the job finishes the window opens again on **Measure**. Click **Take a close-up photo**: it
says how far the MOPA lands from the blue laser.

## Use it
<!-- target: button:Use this alignment -->
Click **Use this alignment**. From now on MOPA work is moved to match. Burn the dots again to check.

## Tidy up
<!-- target: button:Remove the dots from the design -->
Click **Remove the dots from the design**.
