# Turn an add-on off and on
<!-- category: Getting started -->
<!-- summary: See which add-ons you have, and turn one off and back on without restarting. -->
<!-- page: addons.md -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
Add-ons are features you can turn on or off while the program runs. This shows where they are.

## Open the Add-ons window
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Add-ons -->
<!-- wait: window:Add-ons -->
On the **View** tab, click **Add-ons** in the **Show** group.

## Look through the list
<!-- target: window:Add-ons -->
Each add-on says what it does and whether it came with PhotonCanvas. The switch beside it turns it
**On** or **Off** at once: its buttons appear or disappear straight away.

## Close it
<!-- target: window:Add-ons -->
<!-- wait: closed:Add-ons -->
Click **Close**. Your choices are remembered next time PhotonCanvas starts.
