# Measure kerf and slot fit
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Cut a square and a slotted comb at your cut setting, to size parts and slots exactly. -->
<!-- page: materials-and-tests.md#kerf-and-slot-fit-comb -->
<!-- order: 31 -->
The beam takes a little material with it, so parts come out smaller than drawn. A 20 mm square
measured with calipers gives that kerf, and a comb of slots shows how tight slots should be. Do the
cut test first, keep it in the design, and have calipers ready.

## Open the cut test
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Cut test -->
<!-- wait: window:Cut test -->
With the burned cut test still in the design, click **Cut test**: it opens on **Read the results**.

## Pick the setting
<!-- target: field:Use -->
The squares you clicked before are still marked, and **Use** shows the setting you chose. Check
it's the one to test (if the program was closed in between, click the squares again).

## Add the kerf test
<!-- target: button:Kerf and fit test at this setting -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
A square and a comb of slots are added below the grid, and the window closes.

## Cut it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
<!-- wait: window:Cut test -->
Frame it, **Ship It** and press the machine's button. When it's done, the window opens again.

## Measure the square
<!-- target: field:Square is (mm) -->
Measure across the square in a couple of places and type the average. The kerf is what it's short of
20 mm.

## Try the slots
<!-- target: field:Snuggest slot -->
Slide the square into each slot and pick the one it goes into with a firm push and stays.

## Save it
<!-- target: button:Save to the library -->
Pick the material and save: the kerf and slot fit go with the cut setting.
