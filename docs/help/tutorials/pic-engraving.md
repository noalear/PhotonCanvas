# Engraving lines
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Redraw a photo as banknote-style lines that swell in the shadows. -->
<!-- page: pictures.md#engraving-lines -->
<!-- order: 40 -->
Engraving lines turn a portrait or photo into lines that swell where it's dark and vanish where it's
light, like a banknote. They're filled shapes, so they engrave crisply with ordinary fill settings.

## Select a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click a picture on the work area. No picture yet? **Picture → Import picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Open Engraving
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Engraving -->
<!-- wait: window:Engraving lines -->
Click **Engraving**. The preview shows the lines, dark on a light ground.

## Choose the lines
<!-- target: field:Lines -->
**Straight lines**, **Lines following the forms**, **Rings** or **Spiral**.

## Set the spacing
<!-- target: field:Apart (mm) -->
Closer lines show more detail. Keep them at least twice the beam's width apart.

## Set the darkest width
<!-- target: field:Darkest width (%) -->
How wide a line gets at black, as a share of the spacing.

## Add them
<!-- target: button:Add to the design -->
<!-- wait: closed:Engraving lines -->
Click **Add to the design**. The lines go on a new Engrave layer. With the box ticked, the
picture's layer is turned off, so the picture itself isn't engraved, but only when the picture is
alone on that layer; otherwise turn its output off yourself.
