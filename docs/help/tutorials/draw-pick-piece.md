# Outline a piece with the camera
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Click a real piece on the camera picture to get its outline as a shape. -->
<!-- page: drawing.md#pick-piece -->
<!-- order: 70 -->
**Pick piece** outlines a real piece lying on the bed with one click on the camera picture. Use the
outline to fit a design inside it, cut round it or mark it as your material.

## Show the camera picture
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
This needs the machine connected and the camera calibrated. Put the piece on the bed and click **Show** in the machine panel at the right end of the ribbon (see [See the bed under your design](tutorial:cam-see-the-bed)).

## Choose Pick piece
<!-- target: ribbon:Placement/Pick piece -->
<!-- wait: tool:Wand -->
Click the wand icon, **Pick piece**, on the **Placement** tab (or press `W`).

## Click inside the piece
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Hold the mouse over the piece: its outline shows. Click to add it as a shape.

## Refine it
<!-- target: field:Tolerance -->
If the outline stops short, raise **Tolerance**; if it spills onto the bed, lower it. The last pick follows the slider.

## Smooth it
<!-- target: field:Smoothness -->
**Smoothness** gives a simpler outline. **Ignore under** leaves out specks and small holes.
