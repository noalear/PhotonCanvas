# Lock shapes in place
<!-- category: Arranging -->
<!-- summary: Stop shapes being picked or moved by accident, and unlock them again. -->
<!-- page: arrange.md#lock-and-unlock -->
<!-- order: 110 -->
A locked shape can't be clicked or dragged on the work area, so you can work round it without
knocking it. It's still marked when the job runs.

## Select the shapes
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select what should stay put.

## Lock them
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Lock -->
Click **Lock** (`Ctrl+Alt+L`). Clicking them on the work area does nothing now.

## Unlock one
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
In the Layers panel a locked shape has a lock button: click it to unlock just that one.

## Unlock all
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Unlock all -->
**Unlock all** (`Ctrl+Alt+Shift+L`) unlocks every locked shape and selects them.
