# MOPA pulse width and frequency
<!-- category: Layers and settings -->
<!-- summary: Set the fiber laser's pulse width and frequency, and see how much power it really gives. -->
<!-- page: layer-settings.md#mopa-pulse-width-and-frequency -->
<!-- order: 80 -->
The MOPA fiber laser fires in pulses. How long each pulse lasts and how many come each second
change the mark as much as power and speed do: crisp or soft, dark or coloured. Below a certain
frequency the source gives less than its full power, and the panel tells you how much.

## Use the MOPA
<!-- target: button:MOPA -->
Click **MOPA**. **Pulse width** and **Frequency** appear under power and speed.

## Pulse width
<!-- target: field:Pulse width -->
Pick a pulse width, 2 to 500 ns. Short pulses mark crisply; long pulses put in more heat.

## Frequency
<!-- target: field:Frequency -->
Type a frequency in kHz. The line under it says where full power is at this pulse width.

## Watch the warning
<!-- target: field:Frequency -->
Below the full-power frequency the line turns orange and says what share of the 60 W is left: 100%
power then gives only that share. Fine for colour and gentle marks.

## Shiny metal
<!-- target: button:Highly reflective material -->
For bare copper, brass, aluminium, chrome or mirror finishes, tick **Highly reflective material**.
On long pulses the MOPA then keeps out of the frequencies that could reflect back and damage it.
