# Edit a shape's points
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Move points, reshape curves, add and delete points, and make corners sharp or smooth. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#edit-points -->
<!-- order: 70 -->
When a shape is nearly right, editing its points is quicker than drawing it again. You need an
ordinary path: draw one with the **Pen**, or select a rectangle and press `Ctrl+Shift+C` to turn
it into one.

## Select the shape
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Click the shape you want to change.

## Edit points
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Edit points -->
<!-- wait: tool:Nodes -->
Click **Edit points** (or press `N`, or double-click the shape). Its points appear: squares are
corners, circles are smooth points.

## Move a point
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag a point. Click one to select it and show its curve handles.

## Shape a curve
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag a handle. On a smooth point the other handle follows; hold `Alt` as you start to move one
on its own.

## Add and remove points
<!-- target: canvas -->
Double-click an edge to add a point there. `Delete` removes the selected point.

## Sharp or smooth
<!-- target: canvas -->
`S` switches the selected point between sharp and smooth, `Y` makes its handles symmetric, `B`
breaks the outline there.

## Finish
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: tool:Select -->
Press `Esc` to go back to the Select tool.
