# Burn a colour test on metal
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: A grid of MOPA settings on stainless or titanium, to see which colours your plate gives. -->
<!-- page: materials-and-tests.md#colour-test -->
<!-- order: 60 -->
The colours stainless steel and titanium give depend on the plate, its finish and the focus, so a
test on your own plate is the way to know them. You need a clean, flat plate with the same finish as
the real piece, the focus set on it, and the machine connected.

## Show the colour tools
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
If there's no **Colour on metal** group on the Laser tab, tick **Colour on metal** in **Settings →
Equipment**.

## Open the colour test
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Colour test -->
<!-- wait: window:Colour test -->
Click **Colour test** on the **Laser** tab.

## Choose the kind
<!-- target: field:Test -->
Oxide colours, or diffraction gratings on polished stainless.

## Choose what changes
<!-- target: field:Across -->
One setting across, another down. Frequency does most of the work, power fine-tunes it.

## Set the rest
<!-- target: text:THE REST -->
Speed, lines per cm and pulse width for every square, then the square size and gap.

## Add it
<!-- target: button:Add to design -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
The grid goes at the top-left of the work area; the squares run from least energy to most.

## Burn it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
Keep the same focus for everything you'll mark with these colours.

## Measure it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Measure colours -->
Next, **Measure colours** reads each square and saves a palette.
