# Line shapes up
<!-- category: Arranging -->
<!-- summary: Align the edges or the centres of several shapes. -->
<!-- page: arrange.md#align-edges-and-centres -->
<!-- order: 10 -->
The six align buttons line shapes up by their edges or centres, against the box round everything
selected. Each click is one undo step, so try them one after another.

## Select the shapes
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection:2 -->
Select two or more shapes: drag a box round them, or `Shift`+click each.

## Left edges
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Align left edges -->
Click **Align left edges**: every shape's left edge moves to the leftmost one's.

## Right edges
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Align right edges -->
**Align right edges** does the same on the right.

## Centres, one above the other
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Align centres horizontally -->
**Align centres horizontally** puts their centres on one up-and-down line.

## Top edges
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Align top edges -->
**Align top edges** lines up their tops.

## Bottom edges
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Align bottom edges -->
**Align bottom edges** lines up their bottoms.

## Centres, side by side
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Align centres vertically -->
**Align centres vertically** puts their centres on one left-to-right line.
