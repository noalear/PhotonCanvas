# Burn a test grid
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Find the power and speed (or focus, or line spacing) that suit a material, with a grid of squares. -->
<!-- page: materials-and-tests.md#test-grid -->
<!-- order: 20 -->
Not sure what settings to use on a new material? A test grid burns a labelled square for each
combination of two settings on a scrap of it. You pick the square you like and use its numbers.

## Open the test grid
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Test grid -->
<!-- wait: window:Test grid -->
Put a scrap of the material on the bed, then click **Test grid** on the **Laser** tab.

## Choose the test
<!-- target: field:Test -->
**Power × speed** to start; **Focus** and **Line spacing** answer those questions. Each fills in
sensible values.

## Choose the laser
<!-- target: field:Laser -->
The blue diode for wood, leather, acrylic and paper; the MOPA for metals and some plastics.

## Choose the squares
<!-- target: field:Squares -->
**Engrave** fills them; **Score** and **Cut** draw outlines.

## Set the ranges
<!-- target: field:Across -->
For each direction, the setting and its **from**, **to** and **steps**. The line at the bottom says
how many squares and how big.

## Add it
<!-- target: button:Add to design -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click **Add to design**. The grid goes at the top-left of the work area, each square on its own
layer named after its settings.

## Burn it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
Move the grid onto the scrap, frame it and **Ship It**, then press the machine's button.

## Keep the best
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Record results -->
**Record results** saves the squares you like to the library, with a photo of each.
