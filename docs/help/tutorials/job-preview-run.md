# Preview the run
<!-- category: Running jobs -->
<!-- summary: Play the job back on the work area, sped up, to see what's marked when. -->
<!-- page: running-jobs.md#preview-run -->
<!-- order: 50 -->
**Preview run** plays the job back on the work area before you run it: what's marked when, sped
up. It's handy for long jobs, to check the order or see when a part will be done. Have something in
the design.

## Open the preview
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Preview run -->
<!-- wait: window:Run preview -->
Click **Preview run** on the **Laser** tab.

## Play it
<!-- target: button:Play -->
Click **Play**. Marked paths turn green, the one being marked amber, and a dot shows where the
laser is.

## Speed and time
<!-- target: window:Run preview -->
Pick **×1**, **×5**, **×20** or **×100**, or drag the slider to any moment of the job.

## Close it
<!-- target: window:Run preview -->
Close the window to clear the marks. It plays the job as it was when opened: after changing the
design, open it again.
