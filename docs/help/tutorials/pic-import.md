# Import a picture and set its tone
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Bring a photo into the design, size it, and adjust how it engraves. -->
<!-- page: pictures.md#import-a-picture -->
<!-- order: 10 -->
Photos and artwork are engraved dot by dot from a Picture layer. This brings one in, puts it where
you want it and adjusts its tones before it's engraved.

## Import a picture
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/Picture -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click **Picture** on the **Import** tab (`Ctrl+J`) and choose a photo. It lands in the middle of the work area,
selected, on a Picture layer.

## The Picture tab lights up
<!-- target: tab:Picture -->
With one picture selected the tab's name turns blue and its buttons can be used.

## Set its size
<!-- target: panel:Selection -->
Type a width in **W**; with **Keep proportions** ticked the height follows. **X** and **Y** move it.

## Adjust its tones
<!-- target: field:Brightness -->
**Brightness**, **Contrast**, **Gamma** and **Sharpen** change how it engraves, not the file.
**Reset** puts them back.

## Check the layer
<!-- target: panel:Layer settings -->
The Picture layer's resolution, dithering, power and speed decide how the dots are burned.
