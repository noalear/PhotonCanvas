# Tangent circles
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Draw every circle that touches three circles you select. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#tangent-circles -->
<!-- order: 160 -->
Given three circles, this draws every circle (up to eight) that touches all three, from inside or
outside: for geometric patterns of touching circles.

## Draw three circles
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Ellipse -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+3 -->
Choose **Ellipse**, hold `Shift` and draw three circles apart from each other.

## Select them
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection:3 -->
With **Select**, drag a box round all three.

## Find the circles that touch
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/More… -->
Click **More…** and choose **Tangent circles (select three circles)**. The new circles are
selected.

## Keep the ones you want
<!-- target: canvas -->
Click each circle you don't need and press `Delete`.
