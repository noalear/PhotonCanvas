# Round corners
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Turn the sharp corners of shapes into arcs of a radius you choose. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#round-corners -->
<!-- order: 50 -->
Rounded corners are kinder to fingers and to thin material, and look finished. This rounds every
sharp corner between straight edges at once.

## Select shapes with corners
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select one or more shapes with straight edges, such as a star or an imported outline.

## Open Round corners
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Round corners -->
<!-- wait: window:Round corners -->
Click **Round corners**.

## Type the radius
<!-- target: text:Corner radius (mm) -->
Type the radius in mm. A corner whose edges are too short gets the biggest radius that fits.

## Apply
<!-- target: button:OK -->
<!-- wait: closed:Round corners -->
Click **OK**. The status bar says how many corners were rounded.
