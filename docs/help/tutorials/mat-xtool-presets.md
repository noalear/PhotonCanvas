# Bring in xTool Studio's settings
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Read xTool's tested F2 Ultra settings, and your own, from xTool Studio on this computer. -->
<!-- page: material-library.md#importing-from-xtool-studio -->
<!-- order: 12 -->
If xTool Studio is installed here, its material settings can start your library: xTool's tested
settings for the F2 Ultra and the ones you made yourself. They're only read: nothing in xTool Studio
changes and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Close xTool Studio
Close xTool Studio if it's open, so its folder can be read.

## Open the library
<!-- target: ribbon:Materials & Tests/Library -->
<!-- wait: window:Material library -->
Click **Library** on the **Materials & Tests** tab.

## Import
<!-- target: button:Import from xTool Studio -->
Click **Import from xTool Studio…** and confirm with **Import**. A report says how many settings came
in and how many were left out.

## Find them
<!-- target: text:Search materials -->
Type a material's name in **Search materials**. xTool's settings end in *(xTool)*; yours from xTool
Studio come in untested.

## Try one on scrap
<!-- target: button:Apply to layer -->
Select a layer, click **Apply to layer** on a setting and burn it on scrap first: xTool's settings
are for their own materials.
