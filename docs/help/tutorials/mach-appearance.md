# Colours and units
<!-- category: Machine and settings -->
<!-- summary: Dark or light colours, and lengths in millimetres or inches. -->
<!-- page: machine.md#appearance -->
<!-- order: 80 -->
Choose dark or light colours for the program, and whether lengths show in millimetres or inches.
Everything is kept in millimetres; only what's shown changes.

## Open Settings
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
Click the gear at the top right.

## Open the Appearance page
<!-- target: text:Appearance -->
Click **Appearance** in the list on the left.

## Colours
<!-- target: field:Colours -->
**Dark** or **Light**. It takes effect the next time the program starts.

## Lengths
<!-- target: field:Lengths in -->
**Millimetres** or **Inches**, for the rulers, sizes, measuring and the selection's position. Layer
settings stay in millimetres.
