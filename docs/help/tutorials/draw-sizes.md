# Make several sizes
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Copy a design in several widths at once, side by side, each in proportion. -->
<!-- page: drawing.md#sizes -->
<!-- order: 68 -->
Make copies of a design in several sizes at once, each in proportion: for example a sign or
ornament in small, medium and large.

## Select the design
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select what you want in several sizes on the work area.

## Open the generators
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Generators -->
<!-- wait: window:Generators -->
Click **Generators** on the **Home** tab.

## Choose Sizes
<!-- target: tabitem:Sizes -->
Click the **Sizes** tab.

## Type the widths
<!-- target: field:Widths (mm) -->
Type the widths you want in mm, separated by commas: for example 40, 60, 80.

## Make them
<!-- target: button:Make the sizes -->
<!-- wait: closed:Generators -->
Click **Make the sizes**: the copies appear in a row to the right of the original.
