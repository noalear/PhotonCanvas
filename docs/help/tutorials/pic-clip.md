# Clip a picture to a shape
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Show and engrave only the part of a picture inside a closed shape. -->
<!-- page: pictures.md#clip-to-shape -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
Clipping keeps a picture inside a shape, such as a photo in a circle or a heart, without changing
the picture itself. The clip can be taken off again at any time.

## Put a shape over a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
Draw a closed shape over a picture (Home tab). No picture yet? **Picture → Import picture**
(`Ctrl+J`).

## Select both
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection:2 -->
Click the picture, then `Shift`+click the shape.

## Clip it
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Clip to shape -->
Click **Clip to shape**. Only the part inside is shown and engraved; the shape stays as a guide.

## Take the clip off
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Remove clip -->
With the picture selected, **Remove clip** brings back the whole picture.
