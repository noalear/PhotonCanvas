# Mask a picture with a shape
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: An image mask: show and engrave only the part of a picture inside a closed shape. -->
<!-- page: pictures.md#image-mask -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
An image mask keeps a picture inside a shape, such as a photo in a circle or a heart, without
changing the picture itself. The mask can be taken off again at any time.

## Put a shape over a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
Draw a closed shape over a picture (Home tab). No picture yet? **Picture → Import picture**
(`Ctrl+J`). The shape's outline shows on top of the picture, so you can see where it goes.

## Select both
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection:2 -->
Click the picture, then `Shift`+click the shape.

## Mask it
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Image mask -->
Click **Image mask**. Only the part inside is shown and engraved; the shape stays as a guide. The
rest of the picture may hang outside the work area: only the masked part has to fit.

## Take the mask off
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Remove mask -->
With the picture selected, **Remove mask** brings back the whole picture.
