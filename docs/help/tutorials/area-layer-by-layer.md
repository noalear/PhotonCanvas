# Mark one layer at a time
<!-- category: Work area -->
<!-- summary: Ship one layer, change the screen, colour or material, then ship the next. -->
<!-- page: work-area.md#layer-by-layer -->
<!-- order: 40 -->
For screen printing (one screen per colour), or anything where the material or its colour changes
between layers: each layer is marked as its own job, with a stop in between.

## Check your layers
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Put each screen or colour on its own layer.

## Flip for a screen (optional)
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Flip across -->
For screen printing, select the design and click **Flip across** on the **Arrange** tab.

## Open Layer by layer
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Layer by layer -->
<!-- wait: window:Layer by layer -->
Click **Layer by layer** on the **Laser** tab. The first layer is picked.

## Pick a layer
<!-- target: window:Layer by layer -->
Click the layer to mark: only it (and its sub-layers) goes in the job.

## Ship it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
Click **Ship It** and press the machine's start button.

## Change and carry on
<!-- target: window:Layer by layer -->
Change the screen, colour or material, click the next layer and ship again.

## Finish
<!-- target: button:Done -->
<!-- wait: closed:Layer by layer -->
Click **Done**. Jobs mark the whole design again.
