# Place tabs by hand
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Put tabs exactly where you want them on a cut outline. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#place-tabs -->
<!-- order: 120 -->
Tabs are short uncut bridges that keep a part in the sheet until you snap it out. A Cut layer can
space them evenly; this puts them exactly where you click, on a straight edge that's easy to sand.
Not yet tried on the machine.

## Use a Cut layer
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
The shape must be a closed outline on a Cut layer: tabs do nothing on other layers.

## Choose Place tabs
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Place tabs -->
<!-- wait: tool:Tabs -->
Click **Place tabs**.

## Click the outline
<!-- target: canvas -->
Click the outline where a tab should go: a small square marks it. Click again for more.

## Remove one
<!-- target: canvas -->
`Shift`+click a tab to remove it.

## Set the tab width
<!-- target: panel:Layer settings -->
The gap is the layer's **Tab width (mm)** wide. **Tab power %** above 0 cuts it thinly instead of
leaving it.

## Done
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Select -->
<!-- wait: tool:Select -->
Press `Esc` or click **Select**. The tabs move with the shape.
