# Cut overlapping shapes into pieces
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Split overlapping shapes into separate pieces where they cross. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#cut-shapes -->
<!-- order: 100 -->
Where shapes overlap, Cut shapes makes each overlap a piece of its own: for inlays, puzzles and
colour work, where every region is cut or engraved on its own.

## Draw two overlapping shapes
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+2 -->
Draw two closed shapes that overlap, such as two circles.

## Select both
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection:2 -->
With **Select**, drag a box round them.

## Cut them
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Cut shapes -->
Click **Cut shapes**. Two overlapping circles become three pieces: each side and the middle.

## Look at the pieces
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
The pieces are called Piece 1, Piece 2… in the Layers panel. Drag one aside to see it.
