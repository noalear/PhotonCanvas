# Trace a picture into outlines
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Turn a logo or drawing into outlines to cut, score or fill. -->
<!-- page: pictures.md#trace -->
<!-- order: 20 -->
Tracing turns a picture's dark areas, or its colours, into vector outlines. Use it for logos,
silhouettes and scanned drawings you want to cut or engrave as clean shapes.

## Select a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click a picture on the work area. No picture yet? **Picture → Import picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Open Trace
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Trace -->
<!-- wait: window:Trace picture -->
Click **Trace** (`Ctrl+T`). The outlines show in red over the picture.

## Choose a mode
<!-- target: button:Black & white -->
**Black & white** traces what's darker than a threshold. **Colours** puts each colour on its own
layer; **Pick colour** traces what you click.

## Set the threshold
<!-- target: field:Threshold -->
Drag **Threshold** until the red outlines hug what you want.

## Smooth the outlines
<!-- target: field:Smoothness -->
More **Smoothness** gives simpler, smoother curves. **Ignore specks** drops tiny blobs.

## Choose the layer
<!-- target: field:Put on layer -->
Pick a layer or **New layer**. **New layers** sets whether a new layer cuts, scores or engraves.

## Add it
<!-- target: button:Add to design -->
<!-- wait: closed:Trace picture -->
Click **Add to design**. The outlines land exactly over the picture.
