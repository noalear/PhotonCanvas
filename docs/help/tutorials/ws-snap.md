# Grid and snapping
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Show the grid, snap to shapes or the grid, and set the spacing and snap distance. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#snapping-the-grid-and-guides -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
Snapping lines shapes up with each other, the middle of the work area or the grid as you drag, so
things end up straight and evenly placed without measuring.

## Snap to shapes
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Snap to shapes -->
**Snap to shapes** (on to start with): dragged shapes line up with other shapes' edges and
centres, guides and the work area's edges and middle. A pink line shows it.

## Object snaps
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Object snaps -->
**Object snaps** (on to start with, `F3`): points you pick land on other shapes' ends, middles,
centres and crossings, with a yellow marker saying which. The tutorial on precise lines shows them
in use.

## Choose which
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Snap points… -->
**Snap points…** ticks each kind of object snap on or off, and **Tracking**.

## Show the grid
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Show grid -->
**Show grid** turns the grid on and off: major lines every 10 mm, minor every 1 mm.

## Snap to the grid
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Snap to grid -->
**Snap to grid**: when nothing else is near, dragged shapes land on the grid's minor lines.

## Move freely
<!-- target: canvas -->
Hold `Ctrl` while dragging to place a shape anywhere, without snapping.

## Grid settings
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Grid settings… -->
<!-- wait: window:Grid & snapping -->
Click **Grid settings…** to open the **Grid & snapping** window.

## Spacing
<!-- target: field:Major lines (mm) -->
Set **Major lines (mm)** and **Minor lines (mm)**, or click a preset such as **5 / 1 mm**.

## Snap distance
<!-- target: field:Snap distance (px) -->
How close, on screen, something must be before it snaps: 8 pixels to start with.

## Done
<!-- target: button:Done -->
<!-- wait: closed:Grid & snapping -->
Changes apply as you make them. Click **Done** to close the window.
