# Align by two points
<!-- category: Arranging -->
<!-- summary: Move and turn a design so two of its points land on two others. -->
<!-- page: arrange.md#align-2-points -->
<!-- order: 100 -->
Align 2 points moves and turns a design in one go so that two points on it land on two points
elsewhere: one part's edge along another's, or a design onto two corners of a piece.

## Select what to move
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select the shapes to move.

## Choose the tool
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Align 2 points -->
<!-- wait: tool:Align2 -->
Click **Align 2 points**.

## Two points on it
<!-- target: canvas -->
Click a point on the selection, then a second one. Clicks snap to corners.

## Where they go
<!-- target: canvas -->
Click where the first point should go, then the second. Hold `Shift` on that last click to resize
it too, so both land exactly.

## Done
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: tool:Select -->
The tool stays on: click four more points to go again, or press `Esc` to go back to **Select**.
