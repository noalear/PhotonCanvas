# Look through the close-up camera
<!-- category: Camera -->
<!-- summary: Open the close-up camera in a corner of the work area: live, or a photo at a time. -->
<!-- page: camera.md#close-up -->
<!-- order: 30 -->
The close-up camera in the laser head sees a small part of the bed, sharply. Use it to check fine
detail, a burned test, or where the red pointer lands.

## Connect
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
This needs the machine connected (see [The machine and settings](machine.md)).

## Open the close-up camera
<!-- target: ribbon:Camera/Close-up -->
Click **Close-up** on the **Camera** tab. It opens in the bottom-right corner of the work area and
takes a photo.

## Use its buttons
<!-- target: text:CLOSE-UP CAMERA -->
- **Live** keeps it updating (the overview's live view stops meanwhile).
- **Photo** takes one new photo.
- **Save** saves a full-size photo.
- **Bigger** and **Smaller** change its size.

## Close it
<!-- target: ribbon:Camera/Close-up -->
Click **Close-up** again, or the ✕ on the viewer.
