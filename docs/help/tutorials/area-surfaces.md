# Mark on a pen or other object
<!-- category: Work area -->
<!-- summary: Put a surface where a pen lies, draw on it, and the job wraps the mark round the pen and keeps it in focus. -->
<!-- page: work-area.md#surfaces -->
<!-- order: 35 -->
A mark drawn flat comes out squashed and out of focus at the sides of a pen. A surface tells the job
where the pen is and how big: marks drawn on it are wrapped round it, focused on it and evened out.
Experimental: try it on a spare pen first.

## Measure the pen
<!-- target: canvas -->
Measure the pen's diameter with calipers where the mark goes, and lay it on the bed, straight left
to right. Tape or a V-groove jig stops it rolling.

## Open Surfaces
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Surfaces -->
<!-- wait: window:Surfaces -->
Click **Surfaces** on the **Machine** tab.

## Add the pen
<!-- target: button:Add a round object -->
Click **Add a round object**. It's called Pen 1, and its rectangle shows on the left.

## Its size
<!-- target: field:Diameter (mm) -->
Type the **Diameter (mm)** you measured, and set **Lies** to the way the pen runs.

## Its height
<!-- target: button:Lying on the bed: top = diameter -->
Lying straight on the bed, its top is its diameter: click **Lying on the bed: top = diameter**. In a
jig, auto-focus on its top and use **Use the focus height now** instead.

## Where it is
<!-- target: field:Middle X (mm) -->
Set **Middle X (mm)** and **Middle Y (mm)** to the middle of the pen's top line. The camera picture
on the canvas helps line it up afterwards.

## Apply
<!-- target: button:Apply -->
<!-- wait: closed:Surfaces -->
Click **Apply**. The pen shows on the canvas as a purple rectangle, its top line dotted.

## Draw on it
<!-- target: canvas -->
Put the text or logo inside the rectangle. The rectangle is the pen's top unrolled: what you draw is
the size it comes out round the pen.

## Test on a spare
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Ship It -->
The job bar says how far round the pen the mark reaches. Ship a test on a spare pen first.
