# Picture filters
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Greyscale, saturation, sketch, emboss, comic, black and white or mosaic. -->
<!-- page: pictures.md#filters -->
<!-- order: 110 -->
Filters change the picture's pixels: a pencil sketch, a comic look, pure black and white and more.
Before and after show side by side, and `Ctrl+Z` brings the original back.

## Select a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click a picture on the work area. No picture yet? **Import → Picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Open Filters
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Filters -->
<!-- wait: window:Picture filters -->
Click **Filters**.

## Choose a filter
<!-- target: field:Filter -->
Pick one, then move the slider under it (most have one) and watch **After**.

## Apply
<!-- target: button:Apply -->
<!-- wait: closed:Picture filters -->
Click **Apply**. The picture is replaced; its tone settings stay as they were.
