# Material and saved settings
<!-- category: Layers and settings -->
<!-- summary: Save a layer's settings that worked to your material library, and put them back on any layer. -->
<!-- page: layer-settings.md#material-and-saved-settings -->
<!-- order: 230 -->
Once a layer's settings work on a material, save them to the library with how well they worked.
Next time, pick the material and its saved settings instead of finding them again. You need a
layer whose settings you've tried.

## Pick the layer
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Click the layer whose settings worked.

## Save them
<!-- target: button:Save to library… -->
<!-- wait: window:Save to material library -->
Click **Save to library…**.

## Which material
<!-- target: field:Material -->
Pick the material, or **New material…** and give it a name, category and thickness.

## What they're for and how good
<!-- target: field:How good -->
Check **Used for**, give a **Short title**, click the stars, and leave **I burned this and checked
the result** ticked if you did.

## Save
<!-- target: button:Save -->
<!-- wait: closed:Save to material library -->
Click **Save**. The layer now shows the material and the saved settings it uses.

## Use them on another layer
<!-- target: field:Saved settings -->
On another layer, pick the **Material**, then one of its **Saved settings**: everything is put on
the layer, mode included.

## The whole library
<!-- target: button:Library… -->
**Library…** opens the material library to browse, edit and share your settings.
