# Undo, redo and the history
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Take changes back one at a time, or jump to any earlier step. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#undo-redo-and-history -->
<!-- order: 110 -->
Every change can be taken back, and the history lets you jump back many steps at once. Make a few
changes first (draw or move something).

## Undo
<!-- target: header:Undo -->
Click **Undo** (`Ctrl+Z`). Its tip names the step it will undo.

## Redo
<!-- target: header:Redo -->
Click **Redo** (`Ctrl+Y`) to do it again.

## Open the history
<!-- target: ribbon:View/History -->
<!-- wait: window:Undo history -->
Click **History** on the **View** tab (`Ctrl+H`).

## Jump to a step
<!-- target: window:Undo history -->
Click any step to go back, or forward, to it. Undone steps stay below in grey until you change
something.
