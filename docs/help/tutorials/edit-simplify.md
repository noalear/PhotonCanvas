# Simplify a shape
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Redraw an imported or traced shape with far fewer lines and curves. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#simplify -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
Imported and traced shapes often have thousands of tiny pieces. Simplify redraws them with a few
lines and curves that stay within a distance you choose.

## Select the shape
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select an imported or traced shape. Text is left out.

## Open Simplify
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Simplify -->
<!-- wait: window:Simplify -->
Click **Simplify**.

## Choose how close
<!-- target: text:Stay within (mm) -->
Type how far the new outline may stray from the original, in mm. More removes more, but rounds off
small details.

## Apply
<!-- target: button:OK -->
<!-- wait: closed:Simplify -->
Click **OK**. The status bar gives the number of pieces before and after.
