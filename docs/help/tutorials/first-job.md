# Your first job, start to finish
<!-- category: Getting started -->
<!-- summary: Draw a square, make it cut, set power and speed, frame it, ship it and press the machine's button. -->
<!-- page: getting-started.md#your-first-job -->
<!-- order: 10 -->
The whole way from an empty design to a finished cut: a small square in scrap wood or card. You
draw it, check its layer cuts, set the laser, frame it on the material and send it. Nothing fires
until you press the machine's own button.

## Connect the machine
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
You need the machine on and connected. If this says **Not connected**, click **Connect**.

## Choose the rectangle
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Rectangle -->
<!-- wait: tool:Rect -->
Click **Rectangle** on the **Home** tab.

## Draw a square
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Hold `Shift` and drag on the work area. The square with the grid is the machine's 220 × 220 mm
field.

## Make it cut
<!-- target: panel:Layer settings -->
<!-- wait: layer-mode:Cut -->
These are the settings of your square's layer. Click **Cut** at the top if it isn't chosen
already.

## Set the laser
<!-- target: field:Power % -->
Type the **Power %**, **Speed mm/s** and **Passes** for your material. Not sure? Materials & Tests → **Library** has settings that worked, and Materials & Tests → Tests → **Cut test** finds them on a scrap.

## Focus on the material
<!-- target: button:Auto-focus -->
Put the material under the head, then click **Auto-focus**: the machine measures it and moves
to the right height.

## Frame it
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Frame -->
Click **Frame** (the first time, confirm with **Start framing**). A faint dot traces around the
job. Move the material until it's right, then click **Stop framing** in the job bar under the
work area (or **Frame** on the ribbon again).

## Ship it
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Ship It -->
<!-- wait: window:Ship it to the machine -->
Click **Ship It**. A summary opens: each layer's settings, the size and the time.

## Check and upload
<!-- target: button:Ship It -->
<!-- wait: closed:Ship it to the machine -->
Read the summary, then click **Ship It** in it. The job is uploaded and the machine waits.

## Press the start button
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
Close the lid and press the start button on the machine. The job bar follows the job. Stay with
the machine until it's done.
