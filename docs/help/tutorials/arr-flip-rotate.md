# Flip and turn
<!-- category: Arranging -->
<!-- summary: Mirror shapes, or turn them a quarter turn at a time. -->
<!-- page: arrange.md#flip-and-rotate -->
<!-- order: 30 -->
Flipping mirrors the selection, for engraving the back of clear acrylic or glass; turning swings
it a quarter turn about its middle.

## Select something
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select the shapes to flip or turn.

## Flip across
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Flip across -->
Click **Flip across**: it's mirrored left to right.

## Flip over
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Flip over -->
**Flip over** mirrors it top to bottom.

## Turn right
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Turn right -->
**Turn right** turns it 90° clockwise.

## Turn left
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Turn left -->
**Turn left** turns it 90° counter-clockwise.
