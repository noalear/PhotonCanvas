# Record a test grid's results
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Photograph a burned test grid, pick the best squares and save them as tested recipes. -->
<!-- page: materials-and-tests.md#record-results -->
<!-- order: 22 -->
After burning a test grid, save the squares that worked so you don't have to guess next time. Each
becomes a tested recipe with a photo of that square. You need the burned grid still in the design,
the machine connected and the camera calibrated.

## Open Record results
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Record results -->
<!-- wait: window:Record material test results -->
Click **Record results** on the **Laser** tab.

## Pick the material
<!-- target: field:Material -->
Choose it, or **New material…** and type its name, category and thickness.

## Photograph the grid
<!-- target: button:Update photo -->
Click **Update photo**. Each square shows its own piece of the fresh photo.

## Say what's best
<!-- target: field:Best is -->
**The darkest even mark**, or **The lightest clear mark (least heat)**.

## Read the squares
<!-- target: button:Read the squares -->
It measures every square against the bare material and picks the best (★). ▲ is how much darker,
~ how uneven.

## Choose the keepers
<!-- target: text:mm/s ↓ -->
Click every square you'd use again (blue border); click again to drop one.

## Rate and save
<!-- target: field:How good -->
Set the stars, add notes if you like, then click **Save chosen squares**.
