# Import a picture
<!-- category: Importing and exporting -->
<!-- summary: Bring a photo or other picture onto a picture layer and size it. -->
<!-- page: files.md#pictures -->
<!-- order: 40 -->
Photos, scans and logos saved as pictures are engraved line by line from their pixels.
Importing one puts it on a picture layer, ready to size and set up.

## Click Picture
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Picture -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click **Picture** (`Ctrl+J`) and choose a PNG, JPG or other picture in the window that opens.

## The picture layer
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
It's on the first picture layer, or on a new one called **Pictures**.

## Set its size
<!-- target: panel:Selection -->
It comes in at 0.1 mm a pixel, at most 100 mm across, ignoring the file's own dpi. Type the size you want here.

## Prepare it
<!-- target: tab:Picture -->
The **Picture** tab has the tools for a selected picture: trace, crop, filters, background removal.
