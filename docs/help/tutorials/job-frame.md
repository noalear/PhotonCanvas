# Frame the job
<!-- category: Running jobs -->
<!-- summary: Trace round the job with a faint laser dot to see where it lands on the material. -->
<!-- page: running-jobs.md#frame -->
<!-- order: 20 -->
Framing traces round the job with a faint laser dot, over and over, so you can see where it will
land before you mark anything. Move the design while it traces and the frame follows. You need the
machine connected (**Connect** at the right end of the ribbon) and the material on the bed.

## Choose what to trace
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
Click the arrow beside **Frame** in the job bar. **Rectangle around everything** traces the box;
**Snug outline (round or angled designs)** hugs the design. **Power %** and **mm/s** set the dot.

## Start framing
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Frame -->
Click **Frame**. The first time, **Start framing?** asks: click **Start framing**. The head starts
tracing straight away, without the machine's button.

## Move the design
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag the design on the work area until the dot traces where you want it on the material. The frame
follows each change.

## Stop framing
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Frame -->
Click **Frame** again (it says **Stop framing** in the job bar). The machine's button also stops
it, and so does shipping the job.
