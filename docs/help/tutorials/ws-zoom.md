# Zoom and pan
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Zoom in and out, fit the bed or the selection, and move the view around. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#zooming-and-panning -->
<!-- order: 80 -->
Zoom in for detail and out for the whole job. The mouse does most of it; the View tab has
buttons for the rest.

## Zoom with the wheel
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: zoom -->
Turn the mouse wheel over the work area: it zooms in or out where the cursor is.

## Zoom in
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Zoom in -->
<!-- wait: zoom -->
Click **Zoom in** (`Ctrl+=`). It zooms about the middle of the view.

## Zoom out
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Zoom out -->
<!-- wait: zoom -->
Click **Zoom out** (`Ctrl+-`).

## Fit the selection
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Fit selection -->
**Fit selection** (`F`) fills the view with what's selected, or with the whole design.

## Fit the bed
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Fit bed -->
**Fit bed** (`Home`) shows the whole work area again.

## Pan
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Pan -->
<!-- wait: tool:Pan -->
Choose **Pan** (`H`) on the **Home** tab, then drag the work area to move the view.

## Pan with any tool
<!-- target: canvas -->
Hold `Space` and drag, or drag with the middle mouse button, whatever tool you're using. `Esc`
leaves the Pan tool.
