# Space shapes evenly and centre them
<!-- category: Arranging -->
<!-- summary: Give shapes equal gaps, and move them to the middle of the work area. -->
<!-- page: arrange.md#spread-across-and-spread-down -->
<!-- order: 20 -->
Spread across and Spread down give three or more shapes equal gaps, keeping the outer two where
they are. Centre on bed then puts the whole set in the middle of the work area.

## Select three or more
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection:3 -->
Select three or more shapes lying roughly in a row.

## Spread across
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Spread across -->
Click **Spread across**: the gaps between them become equal, left to right.

## Spread down
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Spread down -->
For a column, **Spread down** does the same from top to bottom.

## Centre on bed
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Centre on bed -->
Click **Centre on bed**: the selection moves to the middle of the work area.
