# Tell PhotonCanvas what you have
<!-- category: Machine and settings -->
<!-- summary: Your attachments, the specialist features to show, and the exhaust fan and purifier speed. -->
<!-- page: machine.md#equipment -->
<!-- order: 70 -->
Ticking the attachments you have and the features you use keeps the ribbon to what you need. The
same page sets the exhaust fan and purifier speed sent with every job.

## Open Settings
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
Click the gear at the top right.

## Open the Equipment page
<!-- target: text:Equipment -->
Click **Equipment** in the list on the left.

## Attachments
<!-- target: text:Attachments -->
Tick the rotaries and the conveyor you have. Only those show under **Machine → Work mode**.

## Features to show
<!-- target: button:G-code console -->
Untick features you don't use, like the **G-code console**. They leave the ribbon; nothing is lost.

## Extraction
<!-- target: field:Exhaust fan gear -->
The exhaust fan gear (1–4) and **Purifier speed (%)** go with every job. Experimental: check the
fan sounds right on the first job.
