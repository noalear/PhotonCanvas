# Tell PhotonCanvas what you have
<!-- category: Machine and settings -->
<!-- summary: Your attachments, the specialist features to show, and an xTool inline fan's and purifier's speed. -->
<!-- page: machine.md#equipment -->
<!-- order: 70 -->
Ticking the attachments you have and the features you use keeps the ribbon to what you need. The
same page sets the speed of an xTool inline fan or purifier, if you have one.

## Open Settings
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
Click the gear at the top right.

## Open the Equipment page
<!-- target: text:Equipment -->
Click **Equipment** in the list on the left.

## Attachments
<!-- target: text:Attachments -->
Tick the rotaries, the conveyor, and any xTool inline fan or purifier you have. Only the rotaries
and conveyor ticked show under **Machine → Work mode**.

## Features to show
<!-- target: button:G-code console -->
Untick features you don't use, like the **G-code console**. They leave the ribbon; nothing is lost.

## Extraction
<!-- target: button:xTool inline fan -->
With **xTool inline fan** or **xTool purifier** ticked, its speed appears under Extraction and goes
with every job. The F2 Ultra's built-in exhaust fan has no speed control, so there's nothing to set
for it.
