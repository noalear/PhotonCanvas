# Find your way around the window
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: The ribbon, the panels, the job bar, the machine panel and where help is. -->
<!-- page: workspace.md#the-window-at-a-glance -->
<!-- order: 10 -->
A quick look at each part of the window, so you know where things are. Nothing is changed: press
**Next** to move on.

## The ribbon
<!-- target: tab:Home -->
The commands, in tabs: Home, Arrange, Picture, Camera, Laser and View. Hover over a button to
see what it does. Double-click a tab to fold the ribbon away; click a tab to bring it back.

## The File button
<!-- target: header:File -->
New, open, save, import and export, settings and the keyboard shortcuts.

## Save, undo and redo
<!-- target: header:Undo -->
At the top right: **Save**, **Undo** and **Redo**, then **Settings** and **Help**.

## The work area
<!-- target: canvas -->
The machine's 220 × 220 mm field. Draw here, drag files onto it, zoom with the mouse wheel.

## Layers
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Every layer: what it does, how hard and fast, and how long it takes. The top layer runs first.

## Selection
<!-- target: panel:Selection -->
The selected shapes' position, size and angle. Type exact values here.

## Layer settings
<!-- target: panel:Layer settings -->
The active layer's settings: cut, score, engrave or picture, which laser, power, speed and
passes.

## The job bar
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
**Ship It**, **Frame**, the run order and the total time, always up to date.

## The machine
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
The connection, the camera picture, the lights and the focus height.

## Help
<!-- target: header:Help -->
The guide (`F1`), the tutorials, the getting started tour and the shortcuts. `F1` in any window
opens its page in the guide.
