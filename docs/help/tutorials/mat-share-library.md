# Share materials
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Save materials to a .pcmat file for someone else, or add theirs to your library. -->
<!-- page: material-library.md#sharing-materials -->
<!-- order: 14 -->
Materials, their recipes, colour palettes and photos travel as `.pcmat` files. Share what works with
someone who has the same machine, or bring in what they found.

## Open the library
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Library -->
<!-- wait: window:Material library -->
Click **Library** on the **Laser** tab.

## Put your name on it
<!-- target: text:Your name -->
Type your name in the box at the bottom: people who import your file see your recipes as tested by
you.

## Pick what to share
Click a material in the list (`Ctrl`-click for several). With none picked, all are shared.

## Export
<!-- target: button:Export -->
Click **Export…** and choose where to save the `.pcmat` file.

## Import someone's file
<!-- target: button:Import -->
Click **Import…** and pick one or more `.pcmat` files. New materials are added; for ones you have,
only the recipes you don't have yet. Try their recipes on scrap first.
