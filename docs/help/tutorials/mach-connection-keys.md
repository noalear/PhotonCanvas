# Set up the connection keys
<!-- category: Machine and settings -->
<!-- summary: The one-time setup that lets PhotonCanvas find and talk to the machine. -->
<!-- page: machine.md#connection-keys -->
<!-- order: 10 -->
xTool machines only answer programs that know three short codes. They don't come with PhotonCanvas:
it reads them once from your own copy of xTool Studio and keeps them on this computer. You need
xTool Studio installed on this computer (you can uninstall it afterwards).

## Open Settings
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
Click the gear at the top right.

## Open the Connection page
<!-- target: text:Connection -->
Click **Connection** in the list on the left.

## Open the keys
<!-- target: button:Connection keys -->
<!-- wait: window:Connection keys -->
Click **Connection keys…**.

## Find them
<!-- target: button:Find them in my xTool Studio -->
Click **Find them in my xTool Studio**. It takes a few seconds. Installed somewhere unusual? Use
**Choose xTool Studio's program file…** instead.

## Save
<!-- target: button:Save -->
<!-- wait: closed:Connection keys -->
When all three keys are found, click **Save**. You can now connect.
