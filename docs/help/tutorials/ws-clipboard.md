# Copy, paste, duplicate and delete
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Copy shapes, paste or duplicate them, cut and delete them. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#cut-copy-paste-duplicate-and-delete -->
<!-- order: 60 -->
Copies are the quickest way to make several of the same thing, on this page, another page or in
another design. You need a shape: draw one first if the design is empty.

## Select something
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Click a shape to select it.

## Copy it
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Copy -->
Click **Copy** (`Ctrl+C`).

## Paste it
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Paste -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click **Paste** (`Ctrl+V`). The copy lands 5 mm right of and below the original; each paste goes
5 mm further.

## Duplicate
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Duplicate -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
**Duplicate** (`Ctrl+D`) copies and pastes in one go, without touching the clipboard.

## Cut
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Cut -->
**Cut** (`Ctrl+X`) copies the selection and removes it, ready to paste somewhere else.

## Delete
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Delete -->
**Delete** (`Delete` or `Backspace`) removes the selection. `Ctrl+Z` brings it back.

## More on a right-click
<!-- target: canvas -->
Right-click a shape for these and many more commands: group, align, flip, move to a layer and
so on.
