# Copy, paste, duplicate and delete
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Copy shapes, paste or duplicate them, cut and delete them. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#cut-copy-paste-duplicate-and-delete -->
<!-- order: 60 -->
Copies are the quickest way to make several of the same thing, on this page, another page or in
another design. You need a shape: draw one first if the design is empty.

## Select something
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Click a shape to select it.

## Copy it
<!-- target: canvas -->
Press `Ctrl+C`, or right-click the shape and choose **Copy**.

## Paste it
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Press `Ctrl+V`, or right-click and choose **Paste**. The copy lands 5 mm right of and below the
original; each paste goes 5 mm further.

## Duplicate
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
`Ctrl+D` (or **Duplicate** on the right-click menu) copies and pastes in one go, without touching
the clipboard.

## Cut
<!-- target: canvas -->
`Ctrl+X` (or **Cut** on the right-click menu) copies the selection and removes it, ready to paste
somewhere else.

## Delete
<!-- target: canvas -->
`Delete` or `Backspace` removes the selection (so does **Delete it** on the right-click menu).
`Ctrl+Z` brings it back.

## More on a right-click
<!-- target: canvas -->
Right-click a shape for these and many more commands: group, align, flip, move to a layer and
so on.
