# Output, show and lock
<!-- category: Layers and settings -->
<!-- summary: Keep a guide layer on screen without burning it, hide layers, and lock shapes in place. -->
<!-- page: layers.md#output-show-and-lock -->
<!-- order: 20 -->
Each layer card has three switches: the lightning (sent to the laser or not), the eye (shown or
hidden) and the padlock (locked or not). They let you keep a sketch or a jig outline on screen
without burning it, and keep finished shapes from being moved by accident.

## Output on or off
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Click the lightning on a card. Off, the layer stays on the work area, drawn faded, but is never
sent: right for guides and outlines of the material.

## Show or hide
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Click the eye. A hidden layer isn't drawn, can't be picked, and isn't sent either; the job's checks
remind you if it has shapes.

## Lock
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Click the padlock. Its shapes can't be picked or moved on the work area, but they are still sent.

## One layer at a time
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Right-click a card and choose **Show only this layer** to hide all the others. **Show all layers**
brings them back.

## Several at once
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
`Ctrl+click` cards to select several, then click a switch on one of them: all the selected layers
are set the same way.
