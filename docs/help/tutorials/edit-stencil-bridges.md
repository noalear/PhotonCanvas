# Stencil bridges
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Keep the middles of O, A, R and B in a cut-out stencil. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#stencil-bridges -->
<!-- order: 170 -->
Cut out of a sheet as a stencil, the middle of an O or an A falls out with the letter. Stencil
bridges keeps a strip of the sheet across each such letter, so the middle stays.

## Select the letters
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select a text, or shapes with holes, on a Cut layer.

## Open Stencil bridges
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/More… -->
<!-- wait: window:Stencil bridges -->
Click **More…** and choose **Stencil bridges…**.

## Set the bridge width
<!-- target: text:How wide each bridge is -->
Type how wide each bridge is, in mm. Wider is stronger but shows more.

## Apply
<!-- target: button:OK -->
<!-- wait: closed:Stencil bridges -->
Click **OK**. A strip now crosses each letter with a middle; the text has become plain outlines.
