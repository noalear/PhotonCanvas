# Clear all guides
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Remove every guideline on the page at once. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#clear-all-guides -->
<!-- order: 190 -->
Guidelines dragged from the rulers help line things up, but they pile up. This removes them all in
one go.

## Drag out a guide
<!-- target: canvas -->
Press on the ruler along the top or left of the work area and drag onto it: a guideline. Skip this
if you have some.

## Clear them
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/More… -->
Click **More…** and choose **Clear all guides**. `Ctrl+Z` brings them back.
