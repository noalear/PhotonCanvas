# Measure distances
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Measure the distance and angle between two points on the work area. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#measure -->
<!-- order: 100 -->
Check a gap, the spacing of holes or an angle without doing sums. The ends land on your shapes'
object snaps: corners and ends, middles, centres, quadrants and crossings.

## Choose Measure
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Measure -->
<!-- wait: tool:Measure -->
Click **Measure** on the **View** tab.

## Drag between two points
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag from one point to another. The label shows the length, how far across and down, and the
angle; the status bar repeats it.

## Straight lines
<!-- target: canvas -->
Hold `Shift` while dragging for 45° steps. `Ctrl` turns snapping off.

## Done
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: tool:Select -->
Press `Esc` to go back to the Select tool.
