# Match pictures to the material
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Burn grey patches, let the camera read them, and correct a picture layer so photos come out as they look. -->
<!-- page: materials-and-tests.md#tone-test -->
<!-- order: 40 -->
Most materials go dark long before black and barely show the palest greys, so photos come out too
dark or flat. The tone test measures how your material takes greys with a picture layer's settings,
and corrects that layer's pictures. You need a picture layer, the material on the bed, the machine
connected and the camera calibrated.

## Open the tone test
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Tone test -->
<!-- wait: window:Tone test -->
Put the material flat in the camera's view, then click **Tone test** on the **Laser** tab.

## Choose the layer
<!-- target: field:Settings of -->
The test burns with exactly that picture layer's settings. One pass reads far more evenly than
several.

## Choose the patches
<!-- target: field:Patches -->
How many greys, black to white, and how big.

## Add it
<!-- target: button:Add the test to the design -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
The strip goes in the middle of the bed, and the window closes.

## Burn it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
<!-- wait: window:Tone test -->
Keep the strip clear of knots and grain lines, then **Ship It**. When it's done, the window opens
again.

## Photograph it
<!-- target: button:Update photo -->
The camera must see the whole strip. If glare spoils it, lower the **Lights** and take it again.

## Read the curve
<!-- target: field:How this material takes greys -->
The further the dots are from the dashed line, the more pictures change. The summary says what to
expect.

## Correct the layer
<!-- target: button:Correct this layer's pictures -->
Pick the **Layer** under **Use it**, then click. Keep its settings as tested.

## Keep it
<!-- target: button:Save to the library -->
Pick the material and save: applying this recipe later brings the correction with it.

## See it on screen
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Pictures as burned -->
Turn on **Pictures as burned** to see corrected pictures in the material's own colours.
