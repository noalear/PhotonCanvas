# Draw with the pen
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Draw any outline point by point: corners where you click, curves where you drag. -->
<!-- page: drawing.md#pen -->
<!-- order: 20 -->
The pen draws shapes the other tools can't: straight corners where you click, smooth curves where
you drag. Use it for custom outlines, cut lines and simple drawings.

## Choose the Pen
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Pen -->
<!-- wait: tool:Pen -->
Click **Pen** on the **Home** tab (or press `P`).

## Click for corners
<!-- target: canvas -->
Click three points on the work area. Each click is a corner, joined to the last by a straight line.

## Drag for a curve
<!-- target: canvas -->
For the next point, press, drag and let go: the handle you pull out bends the line into a curve.

## Close the shape
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click the first point (drawn larger) to close the shape.

## Or leave it open
For an open line, press `Enter` or double-click instead. `Backspace` removes the last point; `Esc` throws the line away.
