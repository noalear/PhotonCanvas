# Select circles by size
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Find every circle of a size, such as all the holes, and select them in one go. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#select-circles -->
<!-- order: 55 -->
Holes for pins, magnets or screws often need their own layer or settings. This finds every circle
in the design by its diameter. You need a design with some circles in it.

## Open the window
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Select by… -->
<!-- wait: window:Select circles -->
Click **Select by…** on the **Home** tab, then **Circles by size…**.

## Pick a size or a range
<!-- target: window:Select circles -->
<!-- wait: closed:Select circles -->
Click one of the sizes listed to select those circles. Or type a range in **From Ø mm** and
**to**, and click **Select**.

## Use the selection
<!-- target: panel:Selection -->
Move the circles to another layer with **Layer** here (or `Ctrl+1` to `Ctrl+9`), to give them
their own settings.
