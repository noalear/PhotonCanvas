# Group and ungroup
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Tie shapes together so they select and move as one, and take them apart again. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#group-and-ungroup -->
<!-- order: 10 -->
A group keeps shapes together, such as a name and its border or a part and its engraving. Clicking
any of them picks them all, so they move, turn and resize as one, and Nest keeps them together.

## Select two or more shapes
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection:2 -->
Draw two shapes if you have none. With **Select**, drag a box round them, or `Shift`+click each.

## Group them
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Group -->
Click **Group** (`Ctrl+G`). Now clicking any one of them selects them all.

## Pick one out
<!-- target: canvas -->
Hold `Alt` and click one of them: it's selected on its own, still in the group.

## Ungroup
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Ungroup -->
Click one shape of the group (without `Alt`) and click **Ungroup** (`Ctrl+Shift+G`). They're
separate shapes again.
