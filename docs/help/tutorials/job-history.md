# Job history
<!-- category: Running jobs -->
<!-- summary: See every job run from this computer: when, which design, how long. -->
<!-- page: running-jobs.md#job-history -->
<!-- order: 80 -->
Every job run from this computer is kept in a list: when it ran, which design, how long it took and
whether it finished. Use it to see what a job really took, or how much marking you've done.

## Open Settings
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
Click the gear at the top right.

## Open the Tools page
<!-- target: text:Tools -->
Click **Tools** in the list on the left.

## Open the history
<!-- target: button:Job history -->
<!-- wait: window:Job history -->
Click **Job history…**.

## Read it
<!-- target: window:Job history -->
Newest first: when, the design, its layers and size, how long it took, and **finished** or
**stopped**. The top line adds up the marking time.
