# Measure the beam's slant
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Cut four bars through a board and measure them, to learn how much the galvo's beam slants. -->
<!-- page: materials-and-tests.md#bevel-test -->
<!-- order: 70 -->
Away from the middle of the field the beam comes down at a slant, so cut edges there slope. This test
measures how much, once for the machine, for bevelled edges later. You need a board at least 4 mm
thick, settings that cut through it, calipers, and the machine connected.

## Open the bevel test
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Bevel test -->
<!-- wait: window:Bevel test -->
Put the board on the bed, then click **Bevel test** on the **Laser** tab.

## Choose the cut
<!-- target: field:Cut with -->
A cut layer or library recipe that cuts right through the board cleanly.

## Add it
<!-- target: button:Add the test to the design -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Four bars go in an L along the bottom and right of the bed.

## Cut it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
<!-- wait: window:Bevel test -->
Cut it, let the bars drop out and leave the board where it is. The window opens again on
**Measurements**.

## Type the thickness
<!-- target: field:Board thickness -->
Measure the board with calipers.

## Measure bars and holes
<!-- target: text:Long bar across -->
Each bar with the outside jaws, each hole with the inside jaws from the top. Clear the ash off first.

## Keep them
<!-- target: button:Keep these for bevel cuts -->
If the window warns that the two heights don't look right, measure again first.
