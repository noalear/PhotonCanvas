# Guides from the rulers
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Drag guide lines out of the rulers, line shapes up on them, move and remove them. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#guides -->
<!-- order: 95 -->
Guides are lines you place yourself, such as the edge of your material or a margin. Shapes snap
to them, and they're never sent to the laser.

## Drag one out
<!-- target: canvas -->
Press on the ruler along the top of the work area and drag down. A guide follows, and the status
bar shows where it is. Let go to place it.

## Line shapes up
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag a shape near the guide: it snaps to it (with **Snap to shapes** on).

## Move or remove it
<!-- target: canvas -->
With the Select tool, drag the guide to move it, or drop it back on the ruler to remove it.

## Clear them all
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/More… -->
**More…** in the Outline group of the **Home** tab has **Clear all guides**.
