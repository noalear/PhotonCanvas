# The machine's own settings
<!-- category: Machine and settings -->
<!-- summary: Safety alarms, exhaust run-on, galvo timing and more, stored on the machine itself. -->
<!-- page: machine.md#device-settings -->
<!-- order: 50 -->
Some settings live on the machine itself: its name, the safety alarms, the exhaust run-on, galvo
timing and Wi-Fi. They apply whichever program sends the job. You need the machine connected (**Connect** at the right end of the ribbon). Not
yet checked against the machine.

## Open Settings
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
Click the gear at the top right.

## Open Device settings
<!-- target: button:Device settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Device settings -->
On the **Machine** page, under **STATUS**, click **Device settings…**.

## Safety
<!-- target: tabitem:Safety -->
The flame alarm, stopping when the machine is moved or the lid opens, and the MOPA overheat alarm.
Turning one off shows a full warning first; read it before you accept.

## Exhaust and sleep
<!-- target: tabitem:Exhaust & sleep -->
How long the exhaust keeps running after a job to clear the smoke, and whether the machine sleeps
when left idle.

## Galvo timing
<!-- target: tabitem:Galvo timing -->
Delays for each laser that line the beam up with the mirrors. Change them a little at a time, click
**Save timing**, and burn a test.

## Interlocks
<!-- target: tabitem:Interlocks -->
What the lid, safety key, exhaust fan and emergency stop report right now.
