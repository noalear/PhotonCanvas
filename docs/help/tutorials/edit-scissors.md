# Cut a line with scissors
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Open an outline, or split a line in two, at the point you click. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#scissors -->
<!-- order: 80 -->
Scissors cut a line or outline at one point: open a closed shape so it's cut as a line, or split a
line in two.

## Choose Scissors
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Scissors -->
<!-- wait: tool:Scissors -->
Click **Scissors**.

## Click a line
<!-- target: canvas -->
Click a shape's outline where it should be cut. A closed outline opens there; a line becomes two.

## Separate the pieces
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Break apart -->
The pieces are still one shape. Press `Esc`, select it and click **Break apart** to make them
separate shapes.
