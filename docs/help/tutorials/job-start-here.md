# Start a job from the computer
<!-- category: Running jobs -->
<!-- summary: Turn on the job bar's Start button, then start a loaded job from here after checking the machine. -->
<!-- page: running-jobs.md#starting-from-this-computer -->
<!-- order: 65 -->
Jobs normally start from the machine's own button. If you'd rather start them from the computer, you
can turn on a **Start** button in the job bar. It's off to begin with, because it fires the laser
without anyone pressing a button at the machine.

## Open Settings
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
Click the gear at the top right. Settings opens on the **Machine** page.

## Allow it
<!-- target: button:Allow starting jobs from this computer -->
<!-- wait: window:Allow starting jobs from this computer -->
Tick **Allow starting jobs from this computer** in the **STATUS** card.

## Read the warning
<!-- target: window:Allow starting jobs from this computer -->
<!-- wait: closed:Allow starting jobs from this computer -->
It says what's at stake. **I understand, allow it** turns it on; **Keep it off** leaves it off.
Then close Settings.

## Ship a job
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
Ship a job as usual (see [Ship a job](tutorial:job-ship-it)). Once it's loaded, the job bar shows
**Start** in place of **Pause**.

## Start it
<!-- target: button:Start -->
Look at the machine first: material in place, nothing in the way, the lid as you want it. Click
**Start**, then **Start job** in the question. The laser fires. **Stop** cancels it as usual.
