# Choose what a layer does
<!-- category: Layers and settings -->
<!-- summary: Make a layer cut, score, engrave or engrave pictures, and what changes with each. -->
<!-- page: layer-settings.md#mode-cut-score-engrave-picture -->
<!-- order: 60 -->
A layer's mode says what the laser does with its shapes: follow the outlines (Cut, Score), fill
them in (Engrave), or engrave pictures dot by dot (Picture). The four buttons are at the top of
**Layer settings**.

## Pick a layer
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Click the layer you want to set up. Its settings show in **Layer settings**.

## Score
<!-- target: button:Score -->
<!-- wait: layer-mode:Score -->
Click **Score**: the beam follows the outlines lightly, for fold lines and drawings. Keep score
layers above cut layers.

## Engrave
<!-- target: button:Engrave -->
<!-- wait: layer-mode:Fill -->
Click **Engrave**: the beam scans back and forth to fill closed shapes. **Lines / cm** appears:
how close the lines are.

## Picture
<!-- target: panel:Layer settings -->
<!-- wait: layer-mode:Image -->
Click **Picture** (the last of the four buttons): for photos and other pictures. Outlines on a
Picture layer are left out, and the card says so.

## Cut
<!-- target: panel:Layer settings -->
<!-- wait: layer-mode:Cut -->
Click **Cut** (the first button): the beam follows the outlines to cut through. Tabs, lead-ins and
kerf are under **More settings**.

## Set power and speed
<!-- target: field:Power % -->
Changing the mode doesn't change power or speed: set them for what the layer now does, or pick
saved settings for your material under **Saved settings**.
