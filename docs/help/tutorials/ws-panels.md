# Move, float and fold the panels
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Put the side panels where you want them, float or fold them, and put them back. -->
<!-- page: workspace.md#moving-floating-and-folding-panels -->
<!-- order: 20 -->
The Layers, Selection and Layer settings panels can go on either side, float in their own
windows or fold away, so the work area gets the room you want. Where you put them is kept
between runs.

## Fold a panel
<!-- target: panel:Selection -->
Click **▾** at the right of the panel's title bar: it folds to its title. **▸** opens it again.

## Move it to the other side
<!-- target: panel:Layer settings -->
Click **→** or **←** in a panel's title bar: it moves to the other side of the work area.

## Float it
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Click **⧉**: the panel gets a window of its own, to put anywhere. Close that window to dock it
back.

## Change the widths
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag the line between a side and the work area to make that side wider or narrower.

## Put everything back
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Reset panels -->
Click **Reset panels** on the **View** tab: every panel goes back where it started.
