# Focus the laser
<!-- category: Machine and settings -->
<!-- summary: Auto-focus on the material, nudge the focus height, and home the Z axis when it's off. -->
<!-- page: machine.md#focus-height-and-auto-focus -->
<!-- order: 30 -->
The laser marks sharpest with the head at the right height above the material. Auto-focus measures
it for you; you can also set it by hand. You need the machine connected (**Connect** at the right end of the ribbon) and *Ready*, with the
material on the bed.

## Auto-focus
<!-- target: button:Auto-focus -->
Click **Auto-focus**. The machine measures the material under the head and moves to the right
height. The machine's button stops it.

## Nudge the height
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
**▼** and **▲** move the head 1 mm (`Shift`: 0.1 mm); it moves when you stop clicking. Or type a
distance in the box and press `Enter`.

## If the height seems off
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
Open Settings with the gear at the top right.

## Open the Tools page
<!-- target: text:Tools -->
Click **Tools** in the list on the left.

## Home the Z axis
<!-- target: button:Home the Z axis -->
Click **Home the Z axis**, then **Home it**. The head goes all the way up to find its zero. Keep
your hands clear, then auto-focus again.
