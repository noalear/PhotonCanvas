# Line the slices up: numbers, marks, pins and dowels
<!-- category: 3D and slices -->
<!-- summary: Number every slice, mark where the next one sits, and add pin or dowel holes so the stack glues up straight. -->
<!-- page: 3d-slices.md#lining-the-slices-up -->
<!-- order: 20 -->
A stack of slices only looks like the model if each slice goes in the right order and the right
place. The slicer can number them, mark where the next one sits, and cut holes for pins or dowels
that hold them in line while the glue dries.

## Open 3D to slices
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/3D to slices -->
<!-- wait: window:Stacked slices -->
Click **3D to slices** on the **Home** tab.

## Open a model
<!-- target: button:Open a 3D model… -->
Click **Open a 3D model…** and double-click a model. Press **Next** when it shows in the preview.

## Number the slices
<!-- target: button:Number each slice (light mark, 1 at the bottom) -->
Leave this ticked. Each part is lightly marked with its slice number, 1 at the bottom.

## Mark where the next slice sits
<!-- target: button:Mark where the next slice sits -->
Leave this ticked. The outline of the slice above is marked on each part: glue it inside the line.

## Keep steep parts joined
<!-- target: button:Keep every slice joined -->
On steep slopes two slices can barely touch. Ticked, both get a little extra there to glue; sand it
back afterwards.

## Add pin holes
<!-- target: field:Pin holes Ø -->
Type your pin's diameter: about 2 mm for a toothpick, 3 mm for a skewer. Matching holes go wherever
a part sits on the one below.

## Or dowels right through
<!-- target: field:Dowels Ø -->
For long dowels through the whole stack, type their diameter. Two holes go through every slice, if
every slice has room.

## Look inside
<!-- target: field:From -->
Drag **From** and **to** to show only some slices, and see where the holes and marks fall.

## Make the slices
<!-- target: button:Make the slices -->
Click **Make the slices**. Set the cut layer's kerf so the pins fit snugly, then glue up from slice 1.
