# Trim lines
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Cut away the pieces of lines between the places where they cross. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#trim -->
<!-- order: 70 -->
Trim cleans up line drawings: ends that overshoot, crossing construction lines, the inner arcs of
overlapping circles. Click a piece and it's gone.

## Draw crossing lines
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Line -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+2 -->
With **Line**, draw two lines that cross each other (or use a drawing you have).

## Choose Trim
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Trim -->
<!-- wait: tool:Trim -->
Click **Trim**.

## Click a piece
<!-- target: canvas -->
Move over a line: the piece that would go is highlighted, up to where it crosses another line.
Click to cut it away.

## Finish
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Select -->
<!-- wait: tool:Select -->
Press `Esc` or click **Select** when you're done.
