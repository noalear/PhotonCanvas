# Back up your settings
<!-- category: Machine and settings -->
<!-- summary: Save your material library, shapes and templates in one file, and bring them back. -->
<!-- page: machine.md#tools -->
<!-- order: 100 -->
A backup keeps your material library, saved shapes and templates in one file: keep it somewhere
safe, or take it to another computer.

## Open Settings
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
Click the gear at the top right.

## Open the Tools page
<!-- target: text:Tools -->
Click **Tools** in the list on the left.

## Back up
<!-- target: button:Back up settings -->
Click **Back up settings…** and choose where to save the `.pcbackup` file.

## Restore
<!-- target: button:Restore settings -->
**Restore settings…** brings a backup back, replacing what's there now. Close and open the program
afterwards.
