# Measure a colour test
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Read every square's colour from a close-up photo or a scan, and save them as a palette. -->
<!-- page: materials-and-tests.md#measure-colours -->
<!-- order: 62 -->
This turns a burned colour test into numbers: each square's colour, which ones are clearly different,
and which hues the plate reaches. Saved as a palette on the material, they're what colour pictures on
metal are made from. You need the burned test still in the design.

## Open Measure colours
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Measure colours -->
<!-- wait: window:Measure colours -->
Click **Measure colours** on the **Laser** tab.

## Oxide or gratings
<!-- target: field:Measure -->
One photo for oxide colours; five views for gratings.

## Take a picture
<!-- target: button:Close-up photo -->
With a block of squares under the close-up camera. Or **Open scan or photo…** for a flatbed scan.

## Say which squares
<!-- target: button:Which squares are in this photo? -->
If the photo shows only part of the grid, click its first and last square on the chart.

## Click the corners
<!-- target: text:of 4: click -->
Click the middle of each corner square the line asks for. Check the outlines sit inside the squares.

## Correct the tint
<!-- target: button:Pick grey card -->
Optional: click two corners of a grey card in the picture.

## Check the colours
<!-- target: text:Clearly different if -->
Click a square to leave it out. The slider sets how different colours must be to count.

## Save the palette
<!-- target: button:Save palette -->
Pick the material, name the palette and save. **Export CSV…** gives a spreadsheet.
