# Power, speed and passes
<!-- category: Layers and settings -->
<!-- summary: Choose the laser, then how hard, how fast and how many times a layer is done. -->
<!-- page: layer-settings.md#power-speed-and-passes -->
<!-- order: 70 -->
Power, speed and passes decide how deep and how dark a layer burns. Find good values for a
material with a test (Laser → **Test grid** or **Cut test**), then set them here.

## Choose the laser
<!-- target: button:Diode · blue -->
**Diode · blue** (40 W) for wood, leather, acrylic and paper; **MOPA** (60 W fiber) for metals and
some plastics.

## Power
<!-- target: field:Power % -->
Percent of that laser's full power, 0–100.

## Speed
<!-- target: field:Speed mm/s -->
How fast the beam moves while firing, 2–15000 mm/s. Slower burns deeper and darker.

## Passes
<!-- target: panel:Layer settings -->
Type how many times the layer is done in **Passes**, beside **Speed mm/s**. With more than one, a
line under them says how the passes run: round by round, or each path all its passes at once
(chosen in the job bar).

## Check the card
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
The layer's card shows the power, speed and passes ("80% · 12 mm/s ×3") and about how long the
layer takes.
