# Find your way round Settings
<!-- category: Machine and settings -->
<!-- summary: The Settings window's pages: the machine, the machines you have, equipment, tools and more. -->
<!-- page: machine.md#the-settings-window -->
<!-- order: 40 -->
The Settings window holds what you set up once: the connection and the machine's options, the
machines and equipment you have, colours and units, updates and tools. Changes save as you make
them.

## Open it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Machine settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
Click **Machine settings** on the **Laser** tab (or the gear at the top right).

## The Machine page
<!-- target: window:Settings -->
It opens on the **Machine** page: the connection, the machine's status and options, the camera and
its lights.

## Options stored on the machine
<!-- target: button:Allow starting with the lid open -->
**Allow starting with the lid open** and **Beeper on** are stored on the machine. **Device
settings…** under them has the rest.

## What the machine reported
<!-- target: text:Machine events -->
Scroll down: **MACHINE EVENTS** lists everything the machine reported, with times. Look here when
something goes wrong.

## The other pages
<!-- target: text:Machines -->
The list on the left has the other pages: **Machines**, **Equipment**, **Appearance**,
**Updates**, **Workspace**, **Materials**, **Connection**, **Tools** and **Help**.

## Tools
<!-- target: text:Tools -->
**Tools** has homing the Z axis, the job history, the G-code console and backups. Close the window
when you're done: the machine stays connected.
