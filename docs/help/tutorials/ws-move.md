# Move, resize and turn shapes
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Drag, resize and turn shapes, nudge them with the arrows, or type exact numbers. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#moving-resizing-and-turning -->
<!-- order: 40 -->
Placing shapes is most of designing. Drag them for a quick fit, or type numbers in the Selection
panel for an exact one.

## Select a shape
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
You need a shape: draw one (Home → **Rectangle**) if the design is empty. Click it to select it.

## Move it
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag it. It snaps into line with other shapes and the middle of the work area (a pink line shows
it). Hold `Ctrl` to move freely, `Shift` to move straight.

## Resize it
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag a corner handle: the proportions stay. Hold `Shift` to stretch freely, `Alt` to resize
about the centre.

## Turn it
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag the round handle above the selection. Hold `Shift` for 15° steps.

## Nudge it
<!-- target: canvas -->
Press the arrow keys: 1 mm a press, 10 mm with `Shift`, 0.1 mm with `Ctrl`.

## Type exact numbers
<!-- target: panel:Selection -->
**X** and **Y** place its top-left corner; **W** and **H** set its size.

## Keep proportions
<!-- target: button:Keep proportions -->
Ticked, **W** and **H** change together. Untick it to change them separately.

## Set the angle
<!-- target: field:Angle ° -->
Type an angle, clockwise. 0 is as drawn.

## Centre it
<!-- target: button:Centre in work area -->
Click **Centre in work area** to put the selection in the middle of the field.
