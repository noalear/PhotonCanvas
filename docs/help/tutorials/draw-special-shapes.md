# Add a ready-made shape
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Add hearts, stars, badges and frames from the shape library, and keep your own there. -->
<!-- page: drawing.md#special-shapes -->
<!-- order: 50 -->
Hearts, stars, badges, frames and keychain blanks are a click away in the shape library. You can
keep your own shapes there too, to reuse in any design.

## Open the library
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Special Shapes -->
<!-- wait: window:Shapes -->
Click **Special Shapes** on the **Home** tab (or press `I`).

## Find one
<!-- target: text:Click a shape to add it -->
Pick a category on the left, or type in the search box above the shapes.

## Add it
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click a shape: it appears in the middle of the work area, 40 mm across. The window stays open for more.

## Keep your own
<!-- target: button:Save selection -->
Select shapes in your design, click **Save selection…** and name it. It's kept under **My shapes**.

## Share them
<!-- target: button:Open My shapes folder -->
Your shapes are files in this folder: copy them to friends, or put theirs in and click **Refresh**.
