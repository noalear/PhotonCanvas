# Mark a design bigger than the bed
<!-- category: Work area -->
<!-- summary: Mark a big design a section at a time, sliding the material along a fence between sections. -->
<!-- page: work-area.md#sections -->
<!-- order: 50 -->
A sign or panel bigger than 220 × 220 mm can still be marked: PhotonCanvas cuts it into sections,
marks each in the middle of the work area, and tells you how far to slide the material for the next.

## Draw the big design
<!-- target: canvas -->
The design may run off the work area. The work mode must be the flat bed.

## Open Sections
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Sections -->
<!-- wait: window:Mark in sections -->
Click **Sections** on the **Laser** tab. It says how big the design is and how many sections that
makes.

## Set the section size
<!-- target: field:Section width (mm) -->
**Section width (mm)** and **Section height (mm)**: up to 220 each.

## Place the material
<!-- target: window:Mark in sections -->
Set the material against a fence or stop. Section 1 is picked: the canvas outlines it.

## Frame it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Frame -->
Click **Frame** to see where the section lands, and move the material until it's right.

## Ship it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
Click **Ship It** and press the machine's start button.

## The next section
<!-- target: window:Mark in sections -->
Slide the material by the amount shown under the next section, click it, and ship again.

## Finish
<!-- target: button:Done -->
<!-- wait: closed:Mark in sections -->
After the last section, click **Done**.
