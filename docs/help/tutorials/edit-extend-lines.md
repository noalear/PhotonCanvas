# Extend lines to meet
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Carry open line ends straight on to the next line they meet. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#extend-lines-to-meet-the-next-shape -->
<!-- order: 140 -->
In a drawing whose lines stop just short of each other, this runs each open end straight on until
it meets a line, so the shape can be closed.

## Select the lines
<!-- target: canvas -->
Select the lines with open ends, or nothing to extend every open line in the design.

## Extend them
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/More… -->
Click **More…** and choose **Extend lines to meet the next shape**. The status bar says how many
ends were extended.

## Trim the overhangs
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Trim -->
Where a line now runs past the one it met, cut the overhang off with **Trim**, then join the pieces
with **Tidy lines**.
