# Draw centrelines and centre marks
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Mark the middle of a slot or a hole with dash-dot centrelines that aren't burned, or are when you want them. -->
<!-- page: drawing.md#centreline -->
<!-- order: 37 -->
Centrelines show where the middle of something is, to line other parts up with and to snap to.
They go on their own layer that isn't marked, unless you choose to mark them.

## Choose the Centreline
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Centreline -->
<!-- wait: tool:Centreline -->
Click the **Centreline** icon on the **Home** tab (or press `C`).

## Pick how it draws
<!-- target: field:Draw by -->
In **Draw by**: **2 points**, **Between 2 lines** (a slot's sides) or **Centre mark** (a circle or
a shape).

## Draw one
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click as the label by the pointer asks. It's drawn dash-dot on the **Centrelines** layer.

## Mark them, or not
<!-- target: field:Mark centrelines -->
Leave **Mark centrelines** off to keep them for reference. Tick it and the next ones go on the
active layer and are marked like any line.
