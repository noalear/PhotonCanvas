# Text to paths
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Turn a text into ordinary outlines you can edit point by point. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#text-to-paths -->
<!-- order: 200 -->
A text stays editable words until you convert it. Convert it to reshape letters point by point, or
to fix a serial number at the value it shows now.

## Select a text
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
With **Select**, click a text.

## Convert it
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Text to paths -->
Click **Text to paths** (`Ctrl+Shift+C`). The letters are plain outlines now; the words can't be
edited any more.

## Edit the letters
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Edit points -->
<!-- wait: tool:Nodes -->
Click **Edit points** (or double-click the shape) to move its points and curves.
