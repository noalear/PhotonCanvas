# Ship a job
<!-- category: Running jobs -->
<!-- summary: Upload the job to the machine, read the confirmation, and start it with the machine's button. -->
<!-- page: running-jobs.md#ship-it -->
<!-- order: 10 -->
**Ship It** uploads the job to the machine. Nothing moves or fires until you press the machine's
start button, so you can check everything first. You need the machine connected (**Connect** at the
right end of the ribbon) and something in the design.

## Connect the machine
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
If the status says *Not connected*, click **Connect**. Nothing can be sent until it's connected.

## Check the job
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
The job bar shows the job as it would run now. Look at the timeline and the **Checks** chip: it
should say **All clear**, or click it to see what to look at.

## Ship it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
<!-- wait: window:Ship it to the machine -->
Click **Ship It** (here or at the left of the job bar). The button says **Shipping it…** while the
job is made ready.

## Read the confirmation
<!-- target: window:Ship it to the machine -->
<!-- wait: closed:Ship it to the machine -->
It lists each layer's settings, the size and time, the focus distance and anything to check. Click
**Ship It** to upload, or **Cancel**.

## Press start on the machine
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
Once uploaded the job bar says **Loaded – press start**. Press the machine's start button to run
it; **Stop** cancels it instead.
