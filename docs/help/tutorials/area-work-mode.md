# Mark on a rotary or the conveyor
<!-- category: Work area -->
<!-- summary: Tell PhotonCanvas which attachments you have, then switch the work mode to the rotary or the conveyor. -->
<!-- page: work-area.md#work-mode -->
<!-- order: 10 -->
With a rotary attachment you can mark round things (cups, tumblers, rings); with the conveyor,
pieces up to 610 mm long. Both are experimental: try a small mark on scrap first.

## Open Settings
<!-- target: header:Settings -->
<!-- wait: window:Settings -->
The **Work mode** button only shows when you have an attachment. Click **Settings** (the gear at the
top right).

## Choose Equipment
<!-- target: text:Equipment -->
Click **Equipment** in the list on the left.

## Tick your attachment
<!-- target: text:ATTACHMENTS -->
<!-- wait: closed:Settings -->
Tick **RA2 Pro rotary (rollers and chuck)**, **RA3 rotary (rollers and chuck)**,
**My own rotary (homemade or another make)** or **Conveyor feeder**, then close Settings.

## Open Work mode
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Work mode -->
<!-- wait: window:Work mode -->
Click **Work mode** on the **Laser** tab.

## Choose the mode
<!-- target: window:Work mode -->
Click **Rotary** or **Conveyor** at the top.
- Rotary: pick the **Attachment** and type the **Object Ø (mm)** where it's marked. For
  **Your own rotary**, also say how it **Holds the object** and its scale (correct it from a test turn).
  If the laser doesn't land over your rotary, change **Laser line (mm)**.
- Conveyor: **Belt calibration %** and **Angle**, or **Read from the machine**.

## Use it
<!-- target: button:OK -->
<!-- wait: closed:Work mode -->
Click **OK**. The work area changes size: once round the object, or 610 mm of belt.

## Try it on scrap
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
Fit the attachment as xTool describes. **Frame** only traces a thin line where the laser will draw
(the **Laser line**); ship a small test mark first. **Work mode** → **Flat bed** goes back to the bed.
