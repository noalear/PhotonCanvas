# Turn shapes into a picture
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Make a picture of the selected shapes, to engrave them with picture settings. -->
<!-- page: pictures.md#shapes-to-picture -->
<!-- order: 100 -->
Shapes normally cut, score or fill. Turned into a picture, they're engraved dot by dot, so a picture
style such as halftone or line screen can be used on them.

## Select shapes
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select the shapes to turn into a picture.

## Shapes to picture
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Shapes to picture -->
<!-- wait: window:Shapes to picture -->
Click **Shapes to picture**.

## Choose the resolution
<!-- target: field:Resolution (dpi) -->
300 dpi suits most work (25 to 2540).

## Make it
<!-- target: button:OK -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click **OK**. The picture lies over the shapes on a Picture layer. The shapes stay: turn their
layer's output off if they shouldn't run too.
