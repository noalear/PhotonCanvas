# Make an assembly booklet for the slices
<!-- category: 3D and slices -->
<!-- summary: Get printable step-by-step instructions with the slices: every part, then a step per slice showing where it goes. -->
<!-- page: 3d-slices.md#the-assembly-booklet -->
<!-- order: 25 -->
A model of many slices is easier to glue up with instructions in front of you. The slicer can write
a booklet like a building-set manual: the parts, then one step per slice from the bottom up.

## Open 3D to slices
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/3D to slices -->
<!-- wait: window:Stacked slices -->
Click **3D to slices** on the **Home** tab.

## Open a model
<!-- target: button:Open a 3D model… -->
Click **Open a 3D model…** and double-click a model. Press **Next** when it shows in the preview.

## Keep the labels on
<!-- target: button:Number each slice (light mark, 1 at the bottom) -->
Leave this ticked: the booklet names each part by the label marked on it, like 3 or 3a.

## Ask for the booklet
<!-- target: button:Make an assembly booklet (PDF) -->
Leave this ticked. It only shows for **Stacked layers (solid)**.

## Make the slices
<!-- target: button:Make the slices -->
Click **Make the slices**. The summary says where the booklet was saved: next to your design once
it's saved, otherwise in the Booklets folder.

## Open it
<!-- target: button:Open the booklet -->
Click **Open the booklet** to read or print it. Build from step 1 up, each step's new parts in orange.
