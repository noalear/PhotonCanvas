# Pause, continue or stop a job
<!-- category: Running jobs -->
<!-- summary: Follow a job on the machine, pause it, carry on, or cancel it. -->
<!-- page: running-jobs.md#while-a-job-runs -->
<!-- order: 70 -->
Once a job is on the machine, the job bar shows its state and how long is left, and lets you pause,
carry on or cancel it. First ship a job (see [Ship a job](tutorial:job-ship-it)).

## Loaded
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
**Loaded – press start**: the job waits for the machine's start button. **Stop** cancels it before
it starts.

## Pause
<!-- target: button:Pause -->
While it runs, **Pause** stops it where it is. The button becomes **Continue**.

## Stop
<!-- target: button:Stop -->
**Stop** cancels the job at once. It can't be resumed.

## The machine's button
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
At the machine: a short press pauses and carries on; a long press while it's paused cancels. The
job bar follows either way.
