# Several pages in one project
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Add, rename, copy and delete pages; each page is a job of its own. -->
<!-- page: workspace.md#the-pages-bar -->
<!-- order: 30 -->
A project can hold several pages, each with its own layers, shapes and job: the parts of a box on
one page and its engraved lid on another, say, or one bed of parts per page.

## Add a page
Click **+** in the pages bar, just above the work area. A new, empty page opens, with the same
layers and settings.

## Switch pages
Click a page's tab to show it. Switching is an undo step, like any change.

## Rename it
Double-click the tab of the page on screen, type a name and click **OK**.

## More on a right-click
Right-click a tab for **Duplicate**, **Move left**, **Move right** and **Delete page**.

## One page, one job
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
The job bar, **Frame** and **Ship It** work on the page on screen. Show the next page to send
that one.
