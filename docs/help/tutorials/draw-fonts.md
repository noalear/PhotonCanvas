# Star the fonts you use
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Put your favourite fonts first in the font list and hide the rest. -->
<!-- page: drawing.md#fonts -->
<!-- order: 15 -->
A computer can have hundreds of fonts. Star the few you use so they come first in a text's
**Font** list, and hide the ones you never want to see.

## Open the Fonts window
<!-- target: header:File -->
<!-- wait: window:Fonts -->
Click **File**, then **Fonts…**. (A selected text's settings have a **Fonts…** button too.)

## Find a font
Type part of its name in **Search fonts**. The box beside it holds the sample words each font is shown in.

## Star it
<!-- target: button:★ -->
Click **★** beside a font. Starred fonts come first in the **Font** list.

## Hide the rest
<!-- target: button:Hide -->
Click **Hide** beside fonts you never use: they're left out of the **Font** list. Text already using one keeps it.

## Check your choices
<!-- target: button:Starred -->
**Starred** and **Hidden** list just those fonts. Changes are saved at once; close the window when you're done.
