# Choose where a cut starts
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Pick the point on an outline where its cut starts and ends. -->
<!-- page: editing-shapes.md#start-point -->
<!-- order: 130 -->
Where a cut starts and ends it can leave a small mark. Choose the spot yourself, a corner or the
back edge, where it shows least. Not yet tried on the machine.

## Choose Start point
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Start point -->
<!-- wait: tool:Start -->
Click **Start point**.

## Click the outline
<!-- target: canvas -->
Click a closed outline on a Cut or Score layer. A ring with a dot marks where its cut will start.

## Clear it
<!-- target: canvas -->
`Shift`+click the shape to let the layer's cut order choose again.

## Done
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Select -->
<!-- wait: tool:Select -->
Press `Esc` or click **Select**. The start point moves with the shape.
