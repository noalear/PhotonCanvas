# Export the design or the job
<!-- category: Importing and exporting -->
<!-- summary: Save the design as SVG, DXF or PNG, or the job as an .xf package or G-code. -->
<!-- page: files.md#exporting -->
<!-- order: 80 -->
Export the design to use it in another program, or to show it to someone.
Export the job to keep a copy of exactly what the machine would run, or to read its G-code.

## Choose what goes in
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
A design export takes the page on screen and leaves out hidden layers. Hide any layer you don't want in it.

## Export the design
<!-- target: header:File -->
Click **File**, choose one of these, then a name and folder.

- **Export design as SVG…**: shapes in their layer colours, a group per layer, pictures included.
- **Export design as DXF…**: outlines for CAD, a DXF layer per layer, no pictures.
- **Export design as PNG…**: a picture of the design at 300 dpi.

## Export the job
<!-- target: header:File -->
**File → Export job package (.xf)…** saves the package **Ship It** would upload. **Export G-code (.gcode)…** saves just its G-code as text.
