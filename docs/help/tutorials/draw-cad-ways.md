# Draw circles and rectangles the CAD way
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Circles through three points or touching other shapes, rectangles at any angle, and exact sizes typed between clicks. -->
<!-- page: drawing.md#draw-by-cad-style-ways-to-draw -->
<!-- order: 35 -->
Besides dragging a box, the shape tools can draw the ways a CAD program does: a circle through
three points or touching two lines, a rectangle from three points at any angle, a slot. Each click
snaps to other shapes, and a number typed between clicks gives an exact size.

## Choose the Ellipse
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Ellipse -->
<!-- wait: tool:Ellipse -->
Click the **Ellipse** icon on the **Home** tab (or press `E`).

## Pick how it draws
<!-- target: field:Draw by -->
In the bar above the work area, open **Draw by** and choose **Circle: 3 points**.

## Click three points
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click three points the circle should go through. The label by the pointer says which point is next.

## Choose the Rectangle
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Rectangle -->
<!-- wait: tool:Rect -->
Click the **Rectangle** icon (or press `R`), and choose **3 points (any angle)** in **Draw by**.

## Click two corners, then type the width
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click two corners: they set one side and its angle. Then type `20` and press `Enter` for a rectangle
20 mm wide (or click where the opposite side goes).

## Draw an arc
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Arc -->
<!-- wait: tool:Arc -->
Click **Arc** (or press `A`): click its start, a point it goes through and its end. **Draw by** also
has **Centre, start, end**.
