# Select shapes
<!-- category: The workspace -->
<!-- summary: Click, Shift-click and box selection, and selecting everything, a layer or the rest. -->
<!-- page: canvas.md#selecting -->
<!-- order: 50 -->
Every command works on the selection, so picking exactly the right shapes saves time. You need a
design with a few shapes: draw some or open one first.

## The Select tool
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Select -->
<!-- wait: tool:Select -->
Click **Select** (or press `V`). It's the tool for picking, moving and resizing.

## Click a shape
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Click on a shape's outline. A grouped shape brings its group; `Alt`+click picks just the one.

## Add more
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection:2 -->
Hold `Shift` and click another shape to add it. `Shift`+click a selected one to take it out.

## Drag a box
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag from empty space. Left to right selects what's entirely inside the box; right to left also
what it touches.

## Everything
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/All -->
Click **All** (`Ctrl+A`) to select every shape you can pick.

## The other way round
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Invert -->
Click **Invert**: what was selected is let go, and everything else is selected.

## One layer
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Active layer -->
Click **Active layer** (`Ctrl+Shift+A`) to select everything on the active layer.

## By kind
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Select by… -->
**Select by…** picks open lines, shapes the same size, everything inside a shape, everything on
the same layers, or circles by size.
