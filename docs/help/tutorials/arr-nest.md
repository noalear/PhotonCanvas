# Nest parts on the material
<!-- category: Arranging -->
<!-- summary: Pack parts onto the bed or a piece of material as tightly as they fit, with copies if you like. -->
<!-- page: arrange.md#nest -->
<!-- order: 50 -->
Nest packs parts by their real shapes, turning them where it helps, and can fill the sheet with
copies. Use it to get the most parts out of a board or an offcut.

## Outline the material (optional)
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Pick piece -->
To use the board in the machine, outline it (draw round it, or **Pick piece** on the camera
picture), then right-click the outline → **Use as material outline**.

## Select the parts
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select the parts to nest. Groups stay together.

## Open Nest
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Nest -->
<!-- wait: window:Nest parts -->
Click **Nest**.

## Choose the sheet
<!-- target: field:Sheet -->
The whole bed, your material outline, or another closed shape.

## Set the gaps
<!-- target: field:Gap between -->
The space between parts (at least the cut width), and **Edge margin** from the sheet's edge, in mm.

## Turns and copies
<!-- target: field:Rotation -->
Quarter turns is quick; finer steps pack closer but take longer. **Copies of each** or **Fill the
sheet with as many as fit** add copies.

## Nest them
<!-- target: button:Nest -->
Click **Nest**. The line above the buttons says how many were placed; `Ctrl+Z` undoes it.
