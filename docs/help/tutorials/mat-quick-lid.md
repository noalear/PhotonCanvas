# Load jobs by closing the lid
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Closing the lid focuses, finds the material, centres the design on it and loads the job. -->
<!-- page: materials-and-tests.md#quick-on-lid-close -->
<!-- order: 80 -->
For repeat jobs, put a piece in, close the lid, check, press the machine's button. Quick on lid close
only loads the job: the machine still waits for its button. You need the machine connected and a
design ready to run. Not yet tried on the machine.

## Get the design ready
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
Set up the design as for any job. Only layers with output on are moved and sent.

## Turn it on
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Quick on lid close -->
Click **Quick on lid close**. It stays on until you click it again.

## Put the material in and close the lid
It focuses, photographs the bed, centres what will be marked on the biggest piece it finds, and
loads the job.

## Check, then start
<!-- target: canvas -->
Check the placement on the work area. If it's right, press the machine's button.

## Turn it off when done
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Quick on lid close -->
Click **Quick on lid close** again.
