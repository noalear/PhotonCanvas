# Draw rectangles, ellipses and lines
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Drag out rectangles, circles and straight lines, and round a rectangle's corners. -->
<!-- page: drawing.md#drawing-tools -->
<!-- order: 30 -->
Rectangles, ellipses and lines are drawn by dragging. Rectangles and ellipses stay editable, so
their size and corner radius can be changed later without distorting them.

## Choose the Rectangle
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Rectangle -->
<!-- wait: tool:Rect -->
Click the **Rectangle** icon on the **Home** tab (or press `R`).

## Drag it
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Drag from corner to corner. Hold `Shift` for a square, `Alt` to draw from the centre.

## Round its corners
<!-- target: field:Corner radius -->
The new rectangle is selected: type a **Corner radius** in mm in the **Selection** panel.

## Choose the Ellipse
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Ellipse -->
<!-- wait: tool:Ellipse -->
Click the **Ellipse** icon (or press `E`).

## Drag a circle
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Hold `Shift` while you drag to make a circle.

## Choose the Line
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Line -->
<!-- wait: tool:Line -->
Click the **Line** icon (or press `L`).

## Drag the line
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Drag from one end to the other. `Shift` keeps it to 45° steps.
