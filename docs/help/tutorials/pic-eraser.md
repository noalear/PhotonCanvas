# Rub out parts of a picture
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Erase stray marks or clutter from a picture so they aren't engraved. -->
<!-- page: pictures.md#eraser -->
<!-- order: 120 -->
The eraser turns what you drag over white, so it isn't engraved: a stray mark, clutter in the
background, a corner you don't want.

## Have a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
You need a picture on the work area. No picture yet? **Picture → Import picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Pick the eraser
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Eraser -->
<!-- wait: tool:Eraser -->
Click **Eraser**. A 3 mm circle follows the pointer.

## Rub out
<!-- target: canvas -->
Drag over the part to remove. Each stroke can be undone with `Ctrl+Z`.

## Stop erasing
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Select -->
<!-- wait: tool:Select -->
Press `Esc` or click **Select** to go back to selecting.
