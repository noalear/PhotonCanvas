# Carry on after the piece was knocked
<!-- category: Camera -->
<!-- summary: The material moved halfway through a job: find it again by its marks and finish the job lined up. -->
<!-- page: knocked-piece.md -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
When the material is bumped halfway through a job, Knocked piece finds it again from a camera
photo, by what's already burnt on it, and puts the rest of the job on a new page, moved to line up.

## Stop and leave it
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
Pause or stop the job on the machine. Leave the piece where it is now. This needs the machine
connected and the camera calibrated.

## Open Knocked piece
<!-- target: ribbon:Placement/Knocked piece -->
<!-- wait: window:Knocked piece -->
Click **Knocked piece** on the **Placement** tab.

## Take a photo
<!-- target: button:Take a photo -->
Click **Take a photo**. Nothing moves.

## Find the piece
<!-- target: button:Find the piece -->
Click **Find the piece**. The line under it says how far it moved and turned; on the canvas, grey
lines should sit on the burnt marks.

## Check the list
<!-- target: text:WHAT'S ALREADY MARKED -->
Ticked shapes are marked again; unticked ones are left out. Change any the camera got wrong.

## Make the rest of the job
<!-- target: button:Make the rest of the job -->
<!-- wait: closed:Knocked piece -->
Click **Make the rest of the job**. A new page holds what's left, lined up; your page stays as it
was. Check it, then **Ship It**.
