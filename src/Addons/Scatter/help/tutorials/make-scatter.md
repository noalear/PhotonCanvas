# Make a village's scatter terrain
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make barrels, fences, a cart, a market stall, a well and gravestones for tabletop games that slot together, with wood grain, stonework and lettering etched on. -->
<!-- page: scatter.md -->
<!-- order: 77 -->
Scatter terrain cut from thin sheet slots together without tools, so a whole village square of it is
one job on the laser.

## Open the scatter terrain maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Mini-Designer/Scatter -->
<!-- wait: window:Scatter terrain -->
Click **Scatter** on the **Mini-Designer** tab (in the **Furniture & props** group).

## Set the sheet
<!-- target: tabitem:Sheet -->
On the **Sheet** tab, type your sheet's **Thickness** (2 mm suits 28 mm figures) and pick the
**Figures** size. Picking your material in **Cut with** fills the thickness and slot fit in for you.

## Add scenery to the list
<!-- target: button:Add -->
Back on the **Scenery** tab, pick a **Market stall** under the list and press **Add**. Add a **Well**
and a **Fence** the same way.

## Make several of them
<!-- target: field:How many -->
With the fence picked on the list, type 4 in **How many**. Change its sizes and style underneath:
the preview follows.

## Look at it put together
<!-- target: tabitem:Put together -->
**Put together** shows the piece picked in 3D: drag it round to check it from every side.
**Pieces** shows what will be cut.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. Every piece is nested onto the bed, the detail on layers that run
before the cut. Cut one fence first and change **Slot fit** if the tabs are too tight or too loose.
