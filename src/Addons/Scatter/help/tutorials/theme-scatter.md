# Fill a cave with stalagmites, mushrooms and boulders
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Start the scatter list from a theme: clumps of stalagmites, giant mushrooms and boulders for a cave, or consoles and drums for a sci-fi base, stacked from layers on a hidden core. -->
<!-- page: scatter.md -->
<!-- order: 78 -->
Each theme has scenery of its own. Rock and mushrooms are stacked from layers of thin sheet, so they
come out round and lumpy, and every Variation number grows them differently.

## Open the scatter terrain maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Mini-Designer/Scatter -->
<!-- wait: window:Scatter terrain -->
Click **Scatter** on the **Mini-Designer** tab (in the **Furniture & props** group).

## Pick the cave theme
<!-- target: field:Theme -->
At the top of the **Scenery** tab, pick **Cave** in **Theme**. The note under it says what the theme
brings.

## Start the list from it
<!-- target: button:Start the list from this theme -->
Press **Start the list from this theme**. The list fills with clumps of stalagmites, giant mushrooms
and boulders.

## Grow a bigger clump
<!-- target: field:In the clump -->
Pick the first stalagmite on the list and type 4 in **In the clump**: four spires grow from one base,
each a different size.

## Change their shapes
<!-- target: tabitem:Sheet -->
On the **Sheet** tab, press **Shuffle** beside **Variation** for a different set of shapes.

## Look at it put together
<!-- target: tabitem:Put together -->
**Put together** shows the layers stacked: drag it round. The notes say how many layers there are;
each has the outline of the one above scored on it, so they go together the right way round.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. Glue each stack up on its core, smallest layers at the top.
