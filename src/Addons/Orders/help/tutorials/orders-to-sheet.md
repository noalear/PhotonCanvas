# Turn your orders into a sheet
<!-- category: Running jobs -->
<!-- summary: Bring in Etsy or Shopify orders and lay out a sheet with each customer's name on its piece, with a packing list. -->
<!-- page: orders.md -->
<!-- order: 96 -->
Orders lays out a sheet of personalized pieces from your shop's orders: a copy of the product's
design for each piece, with each customer's words written in. This walks through it with made-up
orders, so you don't need a shop to try it.

## Open Orders
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/Orders -->
<!-- wait: window:Orders to sheet -->
Click **Orders** on the **Import** tab.

## Bring in some orders
<!-- target: button:Sample orders -->
Click **Sample orders** to bring in made-up Etsy and Shopify orders, with two sample products. With
your own shop, use **Import CSV…** for its export, or **Get Etsy orders** and **Get Shopify orders**
once it's set up in **Shops…**.

## Look at a line
<!-- target: text:THE SELECTED LINE -->
Click a line in the list. On the right is the piece as it will burn, with the customer's words in
it. **Save proof…** saves it as a picture to send them.

## Your products
<!-- target: button:Products… -->
**Products…** says which design each thing you sell is burned with. A design is your own drawing
with `{Personalization}` typed where the customer's words go. The samples are set up already.

## Where the pieces go
<!-- target: field:Sheet -->
Pick the whole bed, or the material you outlined on the page. **Gap between** and **Edge margin**
keep the pieces apart and off the edge.

## Make the sheets
<!-- target: button:Make sheets -->
Click **Make sheets**. Each material gets its own sheets, as new pages, with every piece numbered on
a layer that isn't burned.

## The packing list
<!-- target: button:Packing list… -->
**Packing list…** saves a PDF with a map of each sheet and a slip for each order. Close the window,
check each sheet's layers for your material, and use **Ship It**. When the sheet finishes, its
orders are marked burned.
