# Carve a house sign
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Start from a ready-made sign, type your house's name, choose how it's carved and its border, and put its depth map in the design. -->
<!-- page: signs.md -->
<!-- order: 82 -->
The sign maker lays your words out inside a carved border, as big as they fit, and makes the sign
a depth map to carve, with cut lines round it. Carving depth slices hasn't been tried on the
machine yet: try a small sign on scrap first.

## Open the sign maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/Signs -->
<!-- wait: window:Sign maker -->
Click **Signs** on the **Import** tab (in the **Generate** group).

## Pick a sign to start from
<!-- target: tabitem:Signs -->
On the **Signs** tab, click **House name**: an oval sign with the name V-carved inside a rope
border. The picture on the right shows it as carved wood.

## Type your words
<!-- target: tabitem:Words -->
On the **Words** tab, type your house's name in the first box. Use the next box for a second line
(a number, or a road) and give it a smaller **Size %**. A **Curve °** above 0 arches a line to
follow the top of the oval.

## Choose how they're carved
<!-- target: field:Letters -->
On the **Lettering** tab, **Letters** picks **V-carved**, **Sunk**, or letters that stand up:
**Raised**, **Domed** or **Chiselled**. **Depth** is how deep they're carved.

## Choose the shape and border
<!-- target: tabitem:Shape -->
On the **Shape** tab, pick the sign's **Shape** and size, and a border **Design** to carve round
its edge. The words grow or shrink to fit inside it.

## Add screw holes
<!-- target: field:Screw holes -->
On the **Extras** tab, **Screw holes** puts countersunk holes inside the border to fix the sign up.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
<!-- wait: closed:Sign maker -->
Click **Put in the design**. The depth map goes on a new Picture layer in **Depth slices (relief)**
mode, with a cut line round the sign on the Cut layer: set the slices, power and passes, carve a
test on scrap first, then carve the sign before cutting it out.
