# Make a topo map stack
<!-- category: 3D and slices -->
<!-- summary: Turn any place on Earth into stacked contour layers to cut and glue up. -->
<!-- page: topo-map.md -->
<!-- order: 60 -->
A layered relief map of a place you love: a mountain, a lake, an island, your town. You find the
place, choose the area and how many layers, and the slicer cuts the terrain into numbered contour
layers. Finding a place needs the internet.

## Open Topo map
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/Topo map -->
<!-- wait: window:Topo map -->
Click **Topo map** in the **3D & maps** group of the **Import** tab.

## Find the place
<!-- target: button:Find -->
Type a place's name, coordinates, or a link copied from a web map into the box, and click **Find**.
The ground round it appears, with the map's outline on it.

## Choose the area
<!-- target: field:Area across (km) -->
Drag the outline in the picture to move the map, and turn the mouse wheel over it to take in more
or less ground. **Area across** shows how much, west to east.

## Size and shape
<!-- target: field:Shape -->
Pick a **Shape** (a circle looks good on a wall), then set the **Width** and your sheets'
**Material** thickness.

## How many layers
<!-- target: field:Land layers -->
More **Land layers** make the hills taller and the steps finer. The text below says how much taller
than true they come out: 1.5 to 4 times usually looks best.

## Make the layers
<!-- target: button:Make the layers -->
<!-- wait: window:Stacked slices -->
Click **Make the layers**. 3D to slices opens with the map, ready to number, mark and lay out the
layers: click **Make the slices** there to put them on the work area.
