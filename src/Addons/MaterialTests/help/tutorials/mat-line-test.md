# Hide the rows in pictures
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Burn the same piece of a picture with one setting changed each time, and keep the one where rows don't show. -->
<!-- page: material-tests.md -->
<!-- order: 42 -->
Pictures are burned in rows, and on some materials the rows show. The line test burns a piece of your
picture several times side by side, each with one change. You need a picture layer with a picture on
it, the material on the bed and the machine connected.

## Open the line test
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Line test -->
<!-- wait: window:Line test -->
Click **Line test** on the **Laser** tab.

## Choose the layer
<!-- target: field:Settings of -->
Every patch starts from exactly that picture layer's settings.

## Choose what the patches show
<!-- target: field:Patch shows -->
A piece of your picture shows the rows as they'd really be. Click the picture below to move the
square.

## See what changes
<!-- target: field:The patches, left to right -->
Each patch changes one thing from the first. Finer lines get less power, for the same energy.

## Add it
<!-- target: button:Add the test to the design -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
The patches go in the middle of the bed, and the window closes.

## Burn it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
<!-- wait: window:Line test -->
Frame it, **Ship It** and press the machine's button. When it's done, the window opens again.

## Compare
<!-- target: tabitem:Compare -->
Look at the burned piece itself, close up, in light from the side. The photos show which patch is
which.

## Use the best
<!-- target: field:Layer -->
Pick the picture layer, then click **Use these settings** on the best patch. Make the test again from
there to fine-tune.

## Tidy up
<!-- target: button:Remove the test from the design -->
Deletes the patches and their layers.
