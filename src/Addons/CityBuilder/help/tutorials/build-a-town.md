# Build a town
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a town for you, or read one from a battle map picture, lay roads and fill them with buildings, change and find its buildings, see it in 3D, save it as a battle map picture and cut its buildings, ground, bridges, walls, trees and scenery. -->
<!-- page: citybuilder.md -->
<!-- order: 79 -->
The city builder lays out a whole town: the ground, the buildings, walls and trees, in a light 3D
view, each building a building editor building to cut.

## Open the city builder
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/City -->
<!-- wait: window:City builder -->
Click **City** on the **Home** tab. It opens with the last map (the first time, a sample village).

## Make a town for me
<!-- target: button:Make a town -->
On the **Map** tab, set the size, pick how the **Streets** run and tick **A town wall with gates**,
then press **Make a town**: roads, a market square, every building facing its road and trees round
it. Press it again for another. (Or read one from a picture, below.)

## Read a battle map
<!-- target: tabitem:Battle map -->
On the **Battle map** tab, press **Open a picture…** and pick a top-down town map (or **Try a sample
battle map**). Its grid is found by itself.

## Make the town from it
<!-- target: button:Read the map -->
Press **Read the map**: the ground, the buildings (each roof as its own building, square to the grid
or at its angle) and the trees are read from the picture. It's a best guess, so check it over.

## Paint the ground
<!-- target: button:Paint -->
Click **Paint**, pick the **Ground** on the **Tools** tab, and drag over the squares it got wrong.
The right button paints grass.

## Lay a road
<!-- target: button:Road -->
Click **Road** and drag from one point to another: a street two squares wide runs there, round one
corner (hold Ctrl for a straight one), with a name of its own written along it. Make it 4 wide on
the **Tools** tab for a high road, or a dirt track. It stops at a building in its way and crosses
water on a bridge.

## Put up a wall
<!-- target: button:Wall -->
Click **Wall**, pick a town wall under **Walls and fences** on the **Tools** tab (**A rectangle** to go
all the way round), and drag. Its ends are capped, and where a road goes through it there's a gate.

## Fill it with buildings
<!-- target: button:Fill with buildings -->
With **Select**, drag a box round the new road, pick a **District** under **Fill with buildings** on
the **Map** tab and press **Fill with buildings**: buildings of that kind go up along it.

## Add a building
<!-- target: button:Building -->
Click **Building** and drag out a rectangle where a building should stand: it gets walls, a door
towards the road, windows and a roof.

## Change a building
<!-- target: button:Select -->
Click **Select**, then click a building: drag it to move it, press `R` to turn it, and change its
name, type, district and roof on the **Picked** tab. Click a road or wall to drag its handles and
stretch or bend it. Drag a box round several things to move, turn or copy them all at once.

## Find a building
<!-- target: tabitem:Town -->
The **Town** tab lists every building: type to find one by name, type, district or tag, and click it to
go to it. Tick **Colour the plan by district** to see the town's parts.

## Open it in the building editor
<!-- target: button:Edit in the building editor… -->
Press **Edit in the building editor…** (or double-click the building on the plan) to change its
rooms, doors, furniture and roof. Close the editor and the building on the map changes to match.

## Save it to use again
<!-- target: button:Save as a building to reuse -->
Press **Save as a building to reuse**. It's then on the **Tools** tab under **Saved buildings**:
click it and click the map to put down another.

## See it in 3D
<!-- target: tabitem:3D -->
Click **3D** to see the town put together. Drag to turn it round and scroll to zoom in; untick
**Roofs** to look inside.

## Cut a building
<!-- target: button:Put this building in the design -->
With a building picked, press **Put this building in the design**: its pieces go into the design,
nested onto the bed, a page per bed.

## Cut the ground, walls and scenery
<!-- target: tabitem:Cut -->
On the **Cut** tab, press **Put the ground in the design** for the ground as tiles that lock together,
each square etched with its ground, **Put the bridges in the design** for a bridge wherever a road
crosses water, **Put the town walls in the design** for the wall with its gatehouses and guard towers,
**Put the trees in the design**, and **Put the scenery in the design** for the wells, carts, stalls, fountains,
stages and fences.

## Save it as a picture
<!-- target: button:Save as a picture… -->
On the **Map** tab, press **Save as a picture…** to save the town drawn from above as a battle map,
for a virtual table or to print.
