# Draw your own building
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Drag out rooms on a grid, drop in doors and windows, add a floor on top, furnish it, put a roof on and see it in 3D with the walls cut away, then cut every wall, column, floor and roof. -->
<!-- page: houseeditor.md -->
<!-- order: 78 -->
The building editor lets you draw a building floor by floor, like building a house in a life-sim
game, and turns it into pieces to cut.

## Open the building editor
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Build -->
<!-- wait: window:Building editor -->
Click **Build** on the **Home** tab.

## Drag out a room
<!-- target: button:Room -->
Click **Room**, then drag from one grid point to another on the plan: four walls go round it. Drag
another next to it and they share a wall.

## Draw a wall
<!-- target: button:Wall -->
Click **Wall** and drag from point to point: straight along, across or corner to corner. Walls that
close a space in make a room.

## Put in a door and windows
<!-- target: button:Window -->
Click **Door**, **Doorway** or **Window**, then click a wall. Keep the button down to slide it along.
Click **Select** and pick one to change its shape and size on the **Picked** tab.

## Give a wall a different inside
<!-- target: button:Select -->
Click **Select** and click a wall. On the **Picked** tab, **Other side** puts another texture on its
inside, wood panelling in a stone house, say. With single-panel walls that side is engraved after
the pieces are cut and turned over: the backs get a page of their own, lined up with **Fit to piece**.

## Add a floor above
<!-- target: button:Add a floor above -->
Click **Add a floor above**. The floor below shows faintly: draw this floor's rooms over it. It can be
smaller, with a door out onto the floor over the rooms below.

## Put a roof on
<!-- target: button:Roof -->
Click **Roof** and drag a rectangle over the top floor's rooms (or click **Roof it**). Click the roof
to change its shape, pitch and covering on the **Picked** tab. Railings go round any floor left open
by themselves.

## Furnish it
<!-- target: button:Furniture -->
Click **Furniture**, pick a table, a bed or a chest on the right and click in a room to stand it
there. Drag it about and turn it with the mouse wheel as you drag. Click **Stairs** and click in a
room for a way up to the floor above: an opening is cut in that floor over it.

## Look inside
<!-- target: tabitem:Put together -->
**Put together** shows it in 3D. Pick **Walls cut away** under **Show** to see into the rooms, and
drag to turn it round. **Engraving** shows the textures sunk in as deep as you'll etch them, and
**Doors open** swings the doors that open out on their hinges.

## Cut it
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Pick your material on the **Look** tab under **Cut with**, then click **Put in the design**: every wall,
column, floor and tile goes onto the bed, a page per bed.

## Build it step by step
<!-- target: button:Building booklet -->
Click **Building booklet**: it makes a PDF with every piece numbered and a page for each step, from
the ground up, with the new pieces picked out. The same numbers are scored small on the pieces, so
you can find each one as you go.
