# Write in your handwriting
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Type words and get them as single lines in your own handwriting, ready to score. -->
<!-- page: handwriting.md#write-with-it -->
<!-- order: 61 -->
Once you've made a handwriting font, anything you type comes out in your writing, as single lines
that engrave quickly. No font yet? Do [Make a font from your handwriting](tutorial:handwriting-font) first.

## Open Handwriting
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/Handwriting -->
<!-- wait: window:Handwriting -->
Click **Handwriting** on the **Import** tab.

## Type the words
<!-- target: field:Words -->
Type what to write. The preview shows it in your handwriting as you type.

## Set the size
<!-- target: field:Capital height (mm) -->
Set how tall capital letters should be. **Natural variation** makes the letters a little uneven, like
real writing.

## Add it
<!-- target: button:Add to the design -->
<!-- wait: closed:Handwriting -->
Click **Add to the design**. The writing goes on a Score layer in the middle of the work area.
