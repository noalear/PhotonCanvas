# Make a font from your handwriting
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Print a sheet, write your letters on it, photograph it: a single-line font of your own handwriting. -->
<!-- page: handwriting.md#make-a-font-from-your-handwriting -->
<!-- order: 60 -->
Turn your handwriting into a font that engraves as single lines, fast. You'll need a printer, a dark
pen and a phone camera (or the machine's camera).

## Open Handwriting
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Handwriting -->
<!-- wait: window:Handwriting -->
Click **Handwriting** on the **Home** tab.

## Start a font
<!-- target: button:Make a font -->
<!-- wait: window:Make a handwriting font -->
Click **Make a font…**.

## Print the sheet
<!-- target: button:Save the sheet as PDF -->
Pick the **Paper**, click **Save the sheet as PDF…** and print it at actual size (100%).

## Write your letters
<!-- target: text:Write with a dark pen -->
One character per box, standing on the solid line, with a dark pen. Write them separately, not
joined up.

## Read the photo
<!-- target: button:Open a photo or scan -->
Photograph the whole sheet, flat and evenly lit, then click **Open a photo or scan…**. Each box
shows on the right with the lines the laser will follow in red.

## Check the letters
<!-- target: field:Ink threshold -->
Lower **Ink threshold** if box lines creep into letters, raise it if pale strokes go missing. Click
a letter to leave it out.

## Save it
<!-- target: button:Save font -->
<!-- wait: closed:Make a handwriting font -->
Type a **Font name** and click **Save font**. It's ready to write with.
