# Touch up an engraving
<!-- category: Camera -->
<!-- summary: Photograph a burned engraving, see where it came out too light, and send a touch-up that goes over only those parts. -->
<!-- page: touch-up.md -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
After a picture or engraved area is burned, the camera compares it with the design and finds the
parts that came out too light. A touch-up page goes over just those. You need a burned engraving
still where it was burned, and the machine connected (the close-up camera's position known, for
close-up photos).

## Open Touch up
<!-- target: ribbon:Placement/Touch up -->
<!-- wait: window:Touch up -->
Leave the piece where it is, then click **Touch up** on the **Placement** tab.

## Choose what to check
<!-- target: text:What to check -->
Every engraving and picture layer is ticked. Untick any you don't want compared.

## Take the photo
<!-- target: button:Close-up photo -->
The close-up is sharpest; use **Overview photo** if the engraving is bigger than the close-up
camera sees. Keep glare off the burn.

## Look at the result
<!-- target: text:Too light (red) -->
Red shows what came out too light, redder where more is missing. The line under the pictures says
how much.

## Adjust if needed
<!-- target: field:Strength (%) -->
**Strength** sets how much of the missing darkness goes back; below 100% is safer. Raise **Ignore
differences under** if wood grain shows up as red.

## Add the touch-up
<!-- target: button:Add the touch-up page -->
<!-- wait: closed:Touch up -->
The touch-up goes on a page of its own after the design, and is shown.

## Burn it
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Ship It -->
**Ship It** sends just the touch-up page. Start it on the machine, then open **Touch up** again to
look once more.
