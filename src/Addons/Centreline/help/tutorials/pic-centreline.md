# Centre-line trace
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: One line down the middle of each stroke of handwriting or line art. -->
<!-- page: centre-line-trace.md -->
<!-- order: 30 -->
An ordinary trace goes round both sides of a stroke. Centre-line trace draws one line down its
middle instead: quick, clean scoring of signatures, handwriting and line art.

## Select a line-art picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click a picture of dark strokes on a light background. No picture yet? **Import → Picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Trace the centre lines
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Centre-line trace -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+1 -->
Click **Centre-line trace**. The status bar says how many lines it found.

## Check the result
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
The lines are one shape on a Score layer (a new **Centre lines** layer if there was none). Turn the
picture's layer output off if it shouldn't be engraved too.
