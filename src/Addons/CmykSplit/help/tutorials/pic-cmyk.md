# CMYK split for screen printing
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Separate a colour picture into four ink plates, each on its own halftone layer. -->
<!-- page: cmyk-split.md -->
<!-- order: 140 -->
For four-colour screen printing each ink needs its own screen. CMYK split makes the four plates
(cyan, magenta, yellow, black) as halftone pictures on their own layers, to mark one at a time.

## Select a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click a colour picture on the work area. No picture yet? **Import → Picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Split it
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/CMYK split -->
<!-- wait: shapes:+4 -->
Click **CMYK split**.

## Check the layers
<!-- target: panel:Layers -->
There are four new Picture layers, one per ink, with halftone dots at the usual screen angles. Turn
the original picture's layer output off.

## Mark one layer at a time
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Layer by layer -->
**Layer by layer** marks one layer, then waits while you change the screen.
