# Wrap a pattern round a pen or table leg
<!-- category: Work area -->
<!-- summary: Wrap a carved border round a round part in the rotary, as rings, flutes or a spiral that meets itself round the back. -->
<!-- page: borders.md -->
<!-- order: 82 -->
The border maker can wrap any of its designs (rope, Greek key, egg and dart, barley twist and the
rest) round a round part turning in the rotary: rings round a pen, flutes along a handle, a spiral
up a table leg. The pattern goes once round exactly, so it meets itself round the back without a
seam. It hasn't been carved on the machine yet: try a short ring on a scrap dowel first.

## Open it for the rotary
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Wrap a pattern -->
<!-- wait: window:Border maker -->
Click **Wrap a pattern** on the **Machine** tab (in the **Work area** group). The Border maker opens
on **A round part on the rotary**.

## Say how big the part is
<!-- target: field:Part Ø (mm) -->
Type the part's diameter in **Part Ø (mm)**, or click **Use Work mode's** for the **Object Ø** your
rotary is set to. The pattern is made to go once round exactly this size.

## Choose how it wraps
<!-- target: field:Wraps -->
**Round it (rings)** puts rings round the part, **Along it (flutes)** runs copies along it side by
side, and **A spiral along it** winds it along the part like a barley twist.

## Pick a design
<!-- target: tabitem:Design -->
On the **Design** tab choose **Ornament bands** under **Show** and click one, such as **Rope** or
**Barley twist**. Any frame or edge works too.

## Look at it on the part
<!-- target: button:On the part -->
With **On the part** ticked the picture shows the part as the laser sees it: drag up and down to
turn it and check the back. Untick it to see it unrolled.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
<!-- wait: closed:Border maker -->
Click **Put in the design**. It goes once round the rotary start line on a new **Rotary wrap**
layer in **Depth slices (relief)** mode. Set **Machine → Work mode** to **Rotary** with the same
**Object Ø**, set the slices, power and passes, and carve a test first.
