# Carve a picture frame
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Pick a carved frame design, size it to your picture, and put its depth map in the design to carve as a relief. -->
<!-- page: borders.md -->
<!-- order: 81 -->
The border maker draws a carved moulding (egg and dart, rope, Greek key, acanthus and many more) to
fit your frame, with mitred corners and whole repeats along every side, and makes it a depth map to
carve. Carving depth slices hasn't been tried on the machine yet: try a short length on scrap first.

## Open the border maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/Borders -->
<!-- wait: window:Border maker -->
Click **Borders** on the **Import** tab (in the **Generate** group).

## Pick a design
<!-- target: field:Show -->
Choose **Picture frames** under **Show**, then click a design in the list, such as **Classic gilt**.
The picture on the right shows the whole frame.

## Say how big it is
<!-- target: tabitem:Size -->
On the **Size** tab, set **Size is** to **The opening** to give your picture's size, or **The
outside** for the frame's. Then type the **Width (mm)** and **Height (mm)**.

## Choose the corners
<!-- target: field:Corners -->
**Mitred** corners turn the pattern on the diagonal; **Corner blocks** put a rosette in each corner;
**Rounded** bends it round.

## Set the width and depth
<!-- target: field:Border width -->
**Border width** is how wide the border is across, and **Depth** how deep its deepest part is carved.
The summary under the picture says how many motifs fit along each side.

## Look closely
<!-- target: field:Show as -->
Scroll on the picture to zoom in on a corner, and change **Show as** to see it as gilt, metal or the
depth map itself.

## Too big for the bed?
<!-- target: button:Four separate mitred sticks -->
If the summary warns it's bigger than the work area, tick **Four separate mitred sticks**: each side
becomes its own picture, to carve one at a time and glue up.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
<!-- wait: closed:Border maker -->
Click **Put in the design**. The depth map goes on a new Picture layer in **Depth slices (relief)**
mode: set its slices, power and passes there, and carve a test on scrap first.
