# Cut a model's profiles out of rotary stock
<!-- category: Work area -->
<!-- summary: Before carving, cut the waste off a part in the rotary round the model's outline from the top, the side, the 45s and the 22.5s, checking each cut with the camera. -->
<!-- page: stock-carve.md#profile-cuts-take-the-bulk-off-first -->
<!-- order: 41 -->
Profile cuts take the bulk of the waste off a part in the rotary before the carving: the part is turned to each view and cut straight down through round the model's outline.

## Open Carve to model
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Carve to model -->
<!-- wait: window:Carve stock to a model -->
Click **Carve to model** on the **Machine** tab and place the model in the stock (or click **Best fit**).

## Open the profile cuts
<!-- target: button:Profile cuts first… -->
<!-- wait: window:Profile cuts -->
Click **Profile cuts first…**. The window shows the first view's cut from above: green is kept, the lines are the cut.

## Choose the cut settings
<!-- target: field:Cut with -->
Choose cut settings from your library under **Cut with**, and set how deep one pass of them cuts.

## Turn to the view
<!-- target: text:Turn to this view -->
Click the turn button to turn the part to the view (the laser stays off). Focus on the top of the part at the X the window names.

## Send the cut
<!-- target: button:Send the cut -->
Click **Send the cut** and press the machine's start button. The head comes down a focus step at a time as the cut goes deeper.

## Check it went through
<!-- target: button:Check with the camera -->
When a view is done the camera compares the part with how it was before the cut. Click **More passes** if it isn't through, or **It's through: next** for the next view.

## Carve the rest
<!-- target: button:Carve what's left -->
After the views, click **Carve what's left**: Carve to model then only engraves what the cuts couldn't take off.
