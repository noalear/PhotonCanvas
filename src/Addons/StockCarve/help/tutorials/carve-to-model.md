# Carve a scanned part to a model
<!-- category: Work area -->
<!-- summary: Line a 3D model up inside a part scanned in the rotary, see where it sticks out, and engrave the part down to the model. -->
<!-- page: stock-carve.md#carve-a-scanned-part-to-a-model -->
<!-- order: 40 -->
A rough part in the rotary's chuck, scanned, can be engraved down to a 3D model's shape as it turns.

## Open Carve to model
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Carve to model -->
<!-- wait: window:Carve stock to a model -->
Click **Carve to model** on the **Laser** tab. The last scan of the part is the stock.

## Open the model
<!-- target: button:Open a 3D model… -->
Click **Open a 3D model…** and choose the shape to carve. It's laid along the axis in the middle of the stock.

## Fit it in the stock
<!-- target: button:Best fit -->
Click **Best fit**: the model turns and moves to where it fits the stock best. Red shows where it still sticks out.

## Keep its size
<!-- target: text:Keep the model's size -->
With **Keep the model's size** ticked, Best fit never changes the model's size. Untick it to make the model as big as fits.

## Make the engraving
<!-- target: button:Put the engraving in the design -->
Click **Put the engraving in the design**. A page with a layer for each focus height is added; the summary says the Object Ø to set and where to focus.
