# Unfold a 3D model into papercraft
<!-- category: 3D and slices -->
<!-- summary: Turn an STL, OBJ or STEP model into pieces of card with cut lines, folds, glue tabs and numbered edges. -->
<!-- page: papercraft.md#open-the-papercraft-window -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
A 3D model can be built from card: unfolded into flat pieces, cut and scored on the laser, then
folded and glued. You need a model file (STL, OBJ or STEP); simple, faceted models work best.

## Open 3D to papercraft
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/3D to papercraft -->
<!-- wait: window:Fold-up papercraft -->
Click **3D to papercraft** on the **Home** tab. The **Fold-up papercraft** window opens.

## Open a model
<!-- target: button:Open a 3D model… -->
Click **Open a 3D model…** and pick the model file. Its pieces appear on the left.

## Set the size
<!-- target: field:Size (mm) -->
Type how big the finished model should be, along its longest side.

## Choose how simple
<!-- target: field:Faces at most -->
Fewer faces give fewer, bigger pieces that are quicker to fold; more give a rounder model. A few
hundred is a good start.

## Check the summary
<!-- target: text:edges to glue -->
Read how many pieces, folds and glued edges you'll get, and any warnings. Red lines on the preview
are mountain folds, dashed blue lines valley folds.

## Make the papercraft
<!-- target: button:Make the papercraft -->
Click **Make the papercraft**. Every piece goes on the work area with its numbers, folds and cut
outline, on as many pages as it takes.

## Close the window
<!-- target: button:Close -->
<!-- wait: closed:Fold-up papercraft -->
Click **Close**. Set the **Papercraft** layers for your card (folds only score it) before you send
the job.
