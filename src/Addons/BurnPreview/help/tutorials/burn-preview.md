# See the finished piece before you burn it
<!-- category: Camera -->
<!-- summary: Draw your design as it will come out, on the camera's picture of the material. -->
<!-- page: burn-preview.md -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
Before you send a job, see the finished piece: your design drawn on the real material on the bed,
each layer as dark and in the colour your own tests say.

## Show the material
<!-- target: ribbon:Camera/Show picture -->
Put your material on the bed, focus on it, and click **Show picture** on the **Camera** tab. (No
machine? You can still go on: the piece is drawn on a stand-in for the material.)

## Open the preview
<!-- target: ribbon:View/See it burned -->
<!-- wait: window:See it before you burn it -->
Click **See it burned** on the **View** tab.

## Compare before and after
<!-- target: button:Hold to see before -->
Hold **Hold to see before** down to see the bare material; let go to see it burned.

## Check where each look came from
<!-- target: text:LAYERS -->
Each layer says **✓ measured** (from your colour or tone test) or **? guess**. A guess means there's
no test for those settings on that material yet.

## See it on the work area
<!-- target: button:Show on the canvas -->
Tick **Show on the canvas** to draw the burned look on the work area, under your outlines, while
you keep editing.

## Or from the ribbon
<!-- target: ribbon:View/Burned on canvas -->
**Burned on canvas** on the **View** tab does the same, with or without the window open. Click it
again to see the camera picture as it is.
