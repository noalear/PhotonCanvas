# A copy on every piece
<!-- category: Camera -->
<!-- summary: Photograph the bed, find each piece on it and put a copy of the design on every one. -->
<!-- page: camera-placement.md -->
<!-- order: 60 -->
For a batch of tags, coasters or keyrings scattered on the bed: one click puts a copy of the design
on each piece, turned to match, with the next serial number or spreadsheet row on each.

## Connect and lay out the pieces
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
This needs the machine connected and the camera calibrated (the
[Calibrate the camera](tutorial:cam-calibrate) tutorial). Lay the pieces apart, away from the edges
of the bed.

## Select the design
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select the design to put on each piece.

## Open Every piece
<!-- target: ribbon:Placement/Every piece -->
<!-- wait: window:Place on every piece -->
Click **Every piece** on the **Placement** tab.

## Set the height
<!-- target: field:Distance (mm) -->
Type the distance to the top of the pieces (as the machine's focus uses it). The line underneath
must show a green ✓.

## Find the pieces
<!-- target: button:Take photo and find pieces -->
Click **Take photo and find pieces**. Each piece gets a green copy and a number: the order serial
numbers count up in.

## Adjust if needed
<!-- target: field:Tolerance -->
Bed pattern found as pieces? Raise **Tolerance**. Pale pieces missed? Lower it. Then find again.

## Apply
<!-- target: button:Apply -->
<!-- wait: closed:Place on every piece -->
Click **Apply**: the design is replaced by one copy per piece.
