# Cut round printed stickers
<!-- category: Camera -->
<!-- summary: Print-then-cut: the camera finds printed art on paper and a cut goes round each one. -->
<!-- page: camera-placement.md -->
<!-- order: 70 -->
Print stickers or labels on any printer, lay the sheet on the bed, and PhotonCanvas finds each
printed piece and cuts round it with a white border. Not yet tried on real prints.

## Connect and lay out the sheet
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
This needs the machine connected and the camera calibrated (the
[Calibrate the camera](tutorial:cam-calibrate) tutorial). Lay the printed sheet flat and focus on it.

## Limit the search (optional)
<!-- target: canvas -->
To look at only part of the bed, select a shape drawn round that part. With nothing selected, the
whole bed is searched.

## Find the prints
<!-- target: ribbon:Camera/Cut round prints -->
<!-- wait: window:Cut round prints -->
Click **Cut round prints** on the **Camera** tab.

## Set the margin
<!-- target: window:Cut round prints -->
<!-- wait: closed:Cut round prints -->
Type how far outside the art the cut goes (mm), and click **OK**.

## Set the cut
<!-- target: panel:Layer settings -->
The cuts are on the "Print cut" layer (made if there isn't one). Set its power for the paper:
just through the sticker layer for a kiss cut.

## Frame and ship
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
**Frame** to check, then **Ship It** and press the machine's start button.
