# Tune a recipe in rounds
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Let the tuner suggest a few settings, burn and rate them, and repeat until a recipe is just right. -->
<!-- page: recipe-tuner.md -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
The recipe tuner learns from the stars on your recipes and suggests the next settings worth
trying. You need the material in the library (a rated recipe or two helps, from **Record results**
or the library) and a scrap of it in the machine.

## Open the tuner
<!-- target: ribbon:Tests/Recipe tuner -->
<!-- wait: window:Recipe tuner -->
Click **Tests** on the **Materials & Tests** tab, pick **Recipe tuner** in the list and click **Open**.

## Choose the material
<!-- target: field:Material -->
Pick the material, and under **What for**, the kind of work: cutting, scoring, engraving, marking or a picture.

## See what it knows
<!-- target: text:What it knows -->
How many rated recipes it found, and which similar materials it borrows from.

## Choose what may change
<!-- target: field:Change -->
Tick the settings the tuner may change. Fewer settings get there sooner; the rest stay as in your best recipe.

## Read the suggestions
<!-- target: button:Suggest -->
Each suggestion says what it expects and why. The best guess comes first; the others look for something better.

## Add the round
<!-- target: button:Add the tries to the design -->
<!-- wait: closed:Recipe tuner -->
Click **Add the tries to the design**: one small square per try, numbered, each on its own layer.

## Burn the tries
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Ship It -->
Send them with **Ship It** and press the machine's button. When the job finishes, the tuner opens to rate them.

## Rate each try
<!-- target: tabitem:Rate the tries -->
Open **Recipe tuner** again if it didn't open by itself. Give each try stars, and say whether its heat was too little or too much: that shows the tuner which way to go.

## Save and go again
<!-- target: button:Save the ratings -->
Click **Save the ratings** (good tries go into the library), then **Suggest the next round**. Stop when a try gets five stars.
