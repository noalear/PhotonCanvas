# Make a whole cottage
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Pick a cottage, tavern, watchtower or octagonal tower, in any shape, and get every wall, column, floor tile, floor, stair, roof, door and piece of furniture at once, ready to cut and slot together. -->
<!-- page: buildings.md -->
<!-- order: 77 -->
One window makes every piece of a building: textured walls, the columns they slide into, the
floors, the door and the furniture for every room.

## Open the building maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Mini-Designer/Buildings -->
<!-- wait: window:Whole buildings -->
Click **Buildings** on the **Mini-Designer** tab (in the **Buildings** group).

## Pick a building
<!-- target: field:Building -->
Pick a **Cottage**, **Tavern** or **Watchtower** under **Building**, and the size of your figures
under **Figures**. Change its size, storeys, roof, the way up, floor tiles, door and windows underneath.

## Pick a shape
<!-- target: field:Building shape -->
**Building shape** makes it an L, a T or a U, or a hexagon, an octagon or a round tower; **Column
shape** picks octagonal, round, fluted or other columns for its corners. Or pick the **Octagonal
tower** as the building: eight walls under a pointed roof.

## Pick a theme
<!-- target: field:Theme -->
**Theme** under **Building** changes the whole building's look at once: walls, door, windows, roof,
floor tiles, the furniture's faces and the way up. Try *Dungeon* on the cottage, or pick the
**Sci-fi outpost** or **Gothic chapel** as the building.

## Set the wall sheet and texture
<!-- target: tabitem:Walls -->
On the **Walls** tab, **Walls** is *Boxes*: thick walls that stand up on their own, with their own
door that swings open on pins. Pick your material in **Cut with** (or type the sheet's
**Panel thickness**), then the texture for the ground floor and the storeys above. **Etched as**
*Relief* carves the texture in depth instead of etching it flat: the most real, and much slower.

## Furnish the rooms
<!-- target: tabitem:Furniture -->
The **Furniture** tab lists what's in each room. Pick one to change how many and its size, or add
more under the list.

## Look inside
<!-- target: field:Show -->
Pick **With the front cut away** under **Show** to see the rooms, the stairs and the furniture
standing in them. Drag the building to turn it round, and scroll to zoom in on a room; press **F** to see it all again.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. The walls' pieces go on their own pages, nested onto the bed, and the
furniture's on pages of their own. Cut a column side and the end of a wall first and change **Slot fit**
if the wall's tabs are too tight or too loose in the slots.
