# Cut the alignment pins from a dowel
<!-- category: 3D and slices -->
<!-- summary: Lay a long dowel in the machine and cut every pin the slices' holes need, with a few spares. -->
<!-- page: 3d-slices.md#cutting-the-pins-from-a-dowel -->
<!-- order: 21 -->
Pin holes line the slices up, but every pair of holes needs a pin. The slicer can make a second job
that cuts them all from one long dowel lying on the bed.

## Open 3D to slices
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/3D to slices -->
<!-- wait: window:Stacked slices -->
Click **3D to slices** on the **Import** tab.

## Open a model
<!-- target: button:Open a 3D model… -->
Click **Open a 3D model…** and double-click a model. Press **Next** when it shows in the preview.

## Set the pin holes
<!-- target: field:Pin holes Ø -->
Type the dowel's diameter here, so the holes and the pins match.

## Ask for the pins too
<!-- target: button:Cut the pins from a dowel too -->
Tick this. The dowel's settings and a picture of the cuts appear under it.

## Measure the dowel
<!-- target: field:Dowel Ø (mm) -->
Measure the dowel with calipers and type it here: bought dowels are often a little under their size.

## How long it is
<!-- target: field:Dowel length (mm) -->
Type the dowel's length. The note under the picture warns if it's too short for every pin.

## How long the pins are
<!-- target: field:Pins shorter by (%) -->
A pin goes through two slices. 15 % shorter leaves room for a drop of glue, so the slices still close up.

## A few spares
<!-- target: field:Spare pins (%) -->
10 % more pins than holes, rounded up, for the ones that roll away.

## Settings for the dowel
<!-- target: field:Cut the dowel with -->
Choose cut settings from your library that go through the dowel's whole thickness, or set the
**Pins · cut** layer afterwards.

## Make them
<!-- target: button:Make the slices -->
Click **Make the slices**. The pins go on a page of their own after the slices: tape the dowel down
along the bed's long side, set the distance to its top, and move the cuts onto it in the camera picture.
