# Build a round model with radial fins
<!-- category: 3D and slices -->
<!-- summary: Cut a round model as fins round a centre axis, slotted onto flat rings. -->
<!-- page: 3d-slices.md#radial -->
<!-- order: 60 -->
Round things, like heads, vases and pumpkins, can be built from upright fins standing round an axis,
held by flat rings at a few heights. Not yet tried on the machine.

## Open 3D to slices
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/3D to slices -->
<!-- wait: window:Stacked slices -->
Click **3D to slices** on the **Import** tab.

## Open a model
<!-- target: button:Open a 3D model… -->
Click **Open a 3D model…** and double-click a model. Press **Next** when it shows in the preview.

## Build it radially
<!-- target: field:Build as -->
Choose **Radial (fins on rings)**. The axis runs up through the middle of the model.

## Choose the fins
<!-- target: field:Fins -->
Choose how many fins stand round the axis, evenly spaced.

## Choose the rings
<!-- target: field:Rings -->
Choose how many flat rings hold the fins, at even heights.

## Add a centre hole
<!-- target: field:Centre hole Ø -->
Type the diameter of a dowel to run up the middle, or 0 for none. The fins start at the hole's
edge.

## Set the slot fit
<!-- target: field:Slot fit (mm) -->
Slots are the material's thickness plus this. Raise it if the parts won't go together.

## Make the slices
<!-- target: button:Make the slices -->
Click **Make the slices**. To build it, put the rings on the dowel (if you made a centre
hole), then push each fin in towards the middle.
