# Cut a panel bigger than the bed in pieces
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Split an outline bigger than the bed into pieces that join back together with dovetails or jigsaw knobs. -->
<!-- page: boxes.md#split-with-joints -->
<!-- order: 79 -->
A shelf, a sign or a panel longer than the bed can be cut in pieces that lock back together.

## Select the outline
<!-- target: canvas -->
Draw the outline, or click it to select it. It has to be closed.

## Open Split with joints
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Split with joints -->
<!-- wait: window:Split with joints -->
Click **Split with joints** on the **Home** tab (in the **Cut lines** group).

## Pick the join
<!-- target: field:Join -->
Choose **Dovetails** or **Jigsaw knobs** to lock the pieces together, and set their **Size**. The
picture shows the pieces spread apart.

## Split it
<!-- target: button:Split -->
Click **Split**. The outline is replaced by its pieces: cut them, then glue them up.
