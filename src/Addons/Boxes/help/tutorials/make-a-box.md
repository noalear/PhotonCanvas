# Make a box that slots together
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a finger-jointed box with a lid, put it together in 3D, and put its pieces in the design to cut. -->
<!-- page: boxes.md -->
<!-- order: 78 -->
The box maker draws every piece of a box with its joints, so they press together. Cut a small one
from scrap first, to check your kerf and the fit.

## Open the box maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/Boxes -->
<!-- wait: window:Box maker -->
Click **Boxes** on the **Import** tab (in the **Generate** group).

## Pick a style
<!-- target: field:Style -->
Choose **Finger-jointed box** under **Style**. A **Rounded box** bends its wall round the corners,
and a **Box with a bending lid** bends its back over the top.

## Say how big it is
<!-- target: field:Width -->
Type the **Width**, **Depth** and **Height** in mm. **Sizes are** says whether that's the inside
or the outside.

## Choose your sheet
<!-- target: field:Cut with -->
Pick the cut settings for your sheet under **Cut with**, ideally ones with the kerf measured, and
check **Sheet thickness** is what your sheet measures.

## Choose a lid
<!-- target: tabitem:Lid -->
On the **Lid** tab, choose a **Lift-off lid**, a **Sliding lid** or a **Hinged lid**, or none.
Tick **Carve a border on the lid** for a carved frame round it.

## Look it over
<!-- target: text:Open the lid -->
Drag the box round to see it from every side, and tick **Open the lid** to see the lid open. The
**Pieces** tab shows each piece as it will be cut.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Click **Put in the design**. The pieces go on the **Box · cut** layer, nested on the bed, ready to cut.
