# Price a job and send a quote
<!-- category: Running jobs -->
<!-- summary: Work out what a job costs you and what to charge, then save a PDF quote or copy it as text. -->
<!-- page: quote.md -->
<!-- order: 95 -->
Quote adds up the material, laser time, electricity and your own time for the design on screen,
puts your markup on top and makes a quote to send. Set your rates once; after that each quote takes
a minute. Nothing is sent to the machine.

## Open Quote
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Quote -->
<!-- wait: window:Quote and cost -->
With your design open, click **Quote**.

## Who and how many
<!-- target: field:Customer -->
Type who it's for. Set **Runs** (how many times the job runs) and **Pieces a run** (how many
pieces each run makes) to get the price of one piece.

## The time
<!-- target: field:Time from -->
The estimate is corrected by how long your past jobs really took. Once this design has run to the
end, you can pick **Last real run** instead.

## The material
<!-- target: field:Sheet price -->
Enter what a whole sheet costs and how big it is. It's remembered for that material next time.
Tick **The customer brings the material** when they do.

## Your rates
<!-- target: field:Machine / hour -->
What an hour of the machine costs you, its power draw and your electricity price, your time, and
the set-up and handling minutes. Set them once; they're kept for every quote.

## The price
<!-- target: field:Markup (%) -->
Your markup, a minimum, rounding and tax. The price on the right updates as you type.

## Send it
<!-- target: button:Save quote… -->
**Save quote…** makes a PDF to print or send, or **Copy as text** for an email or a message.
