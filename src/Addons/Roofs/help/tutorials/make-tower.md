# Make a round castle tower
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a round tower of stacked rings with stonework, battlements and slots for walls. -->
<!-- page: roofs.md#towers -->
<!-- order: 78 -->
A round tower is stacked from rings of the same sheet: the stonework is cut into their edges, and
its storeys lift apart so figures can go inside. Walls from Walls slide into slots up its side.

## Open the tower maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Towers -->
<!-- wait: window:Round towers -->
Click **Towers** on the **Home** tab.

## Size the tower
<!-- target: field:Diameter -->
Type its **Diameter** and **Height**, your sheet's **Thickness** and how many **Storeys** lift apart.

## Choose the stonework
<!-- target: tabitem:Stonework -->
On the **Stonework** tab, pick stone blocks, brick or fieldstone and the size of the blocks.

## Choose the top
<!-- target: tabitem:Top -->
On the **Top** tab, choose battlements round a deck, or a cone roof that lifts off.

## Add a door and windows
<!-- target: tabitem:Doors & windows -->
On **Doors & windows**, choose the door and how many windows each storey has.

## Join it to walls
<!-- target: tabitem:Walls -->
On **Walls**, pick **Corner (90°)** for a corner tower and press **Match the walls in the design** so
the slots fit your walls.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. Each ring has its number scored on top: stack them in order and glue
them, with a locator ring under each floor above the ground.
