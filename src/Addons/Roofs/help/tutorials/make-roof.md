# Make a roof for a room of walls
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a shingled gable roof that lifts off a room built with Walls. -->
<!-- page: roofs.md -->
<!-- order: 77 -->
A roof with shingles etched on finishes a building made with Walls, and lifts off so figures can go
inside. Make the walls first: the roof is made to fit them.

## Open the roof maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Designer/Roofs -->
<!-- wait: window:Roofs -->
Click **Roofs** on the **Designer** tab (in the **Buildings** group).

## Pick a theme
<!-- target: field:Theme -->
**Theme** at the top sets the shingles, the slope and the gable ends for a look: slate for a dungeon,
corrugated metal for sci-fi, clay tiles for the desert, a steep slate roof for gothic. Leave it on
*Medieval* here; change anything it sets afterwards.

## Choose the shape
<!-- target: field:Roof -->
Pick **Gable** for two slopes, **Hipped** for four (a pyramid on a square room) or **Lean-to** for one.

## Fit it to the walls
<!-- target: button:Match the walls in the design -->
Press **Match the walls in the design**: the size, the sheet and the walls' texture for the gables
are read from your walls.

## Set the slope
<!-- target: field:Pitch (°) -->
Type the **Pitch**: 45° for a cottage, 30° for a barn, 60° for something steep.

## Choose the shingles
<!-- target: tabitem:Shingles -->
On the **Shingles** tab, pick slate, wooden shingles, fish scales, clay tiles or thatch.

## Add a gable window
<!-- target: tabitem:Gables -->
On **Gables**, choose a **Window** for the attic.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. Cut a gable and the base first and check the tabs fit their slots;
change **Slot fit** on the **Fit** tab if they don't.
