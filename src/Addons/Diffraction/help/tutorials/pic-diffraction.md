# A diffraction picture
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: A picture of tiny gratings on polished stainless that shimmers with rainbow colours. -->
<!-- page: diffraction.md -->
<!-- order: 60 -->
On polished stainless the MOPA's pulses can form tiny diffraction gratings that split light into
rainbows. This turns a picture into grating pixels that light up as the plate tilts. Run a grating
test first (Materials & Tests → Tests → Colour test, Diffraction gratings) to find your power and green pitch.

## Select a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click a picture on the work area. No picture yet? **Import → Picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Open Diffraction
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Diffraction -->
<!-- wait: window:Diffraction picture -->
Click **Diffraction**. The preview shows the grating pixels, coloured by direction.

## Choose the mode
<!-- target: field:Mode -->
**Shimmer** turns brightness into grating direction; **Colour** turns hue into pitch; **3D** is for
depth maps (experimental).

## Set the pixel size
<!-- target: field:Pixel (mm) -->
Smaller pixels show more detail but take longer.

## Enter your green pitch
<!-- target: field:Pitch for green µm -->
The pitch that showed green on your grating test.

## Set the marking
<!-- target: field:Power % -->
Keep the power low. Use the speed and pulse width from your test.

## Add the layers
<!-- target: button:Add grating layers -->
<!-- wait: closed:Diffraction picture -->
Click **Add grating layers**: one MOPA layer per direction or pitch, over the picture.
