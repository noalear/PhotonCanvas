# Build an automaton box
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a box with a handle, cams on a shaft and platforms on top that lift your figures, checked moving before you cut. -->
<!-- page: mechanisms.md#automaton-box -->
<!-- order: 72 -->
An automaton box makes little figures move when you turn its handle: each figure stands on a
platform that its own cam lifts. You need your material's real thickness, a square dowel for the
shaft and a round one for the handle.

## Open the mechanism maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Mechanisms -->
<!-- wait: window:Mechanism maker -->
Click **Mechanisms** on the **Home** tab.

## Choose Automaton box
<!-- target: tabitem:Automaton box -->
Click the **Automaton box** tab. It starts with two cams: one rises and waits, the other bobs twice.

## Set each cam's motion
<!-- target: button:Add a cam -->
Open a cam's section to change its steps, or **Add a cam** for another figure (up to 6).

## Put them out of step
<!-- target: field:Starts -->
**Starts** sets how far into its motion each cam is when the handle starts, so the figures don't all
move together.

## Fit the box to your sheet
<!-- target: field:Thickness -->
Set your sheet's **Thickness**, or pick your material under **Cut with**: the box's tabs and slots
are made for it.

## Check it from every side
<!-- target: text:Drag a part round -->
Watch it from the front and each cam from the side. The check makes sure the followers follow, the
handle clears the base and the platforms never touch.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Click **Put in the design**. The parts go on as many beds as they need, a page each.
