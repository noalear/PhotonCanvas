# Make gears and watch them turn
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a pair of gears on a plate, watch them turn and check they mesh, then put them in the design to cut. -->
<!-- page: mechanisms.md#gears -->
<!-- order: 70 -->
Gears change how fast and which way things turn. The mechanism maker draws real gear teeth for your
material, turns them on screen to check they mesh without jamming, and lays the parts out to cut.

## Open the mechanism maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Mechanisms -->
<!-- wait: window:Mechanism maker -->
Click **Mechanisms** on the **Home** tab.

## Choose Gears
<!-- target: tabitem:Gears -->
Click the **Gears** tab. To start with, a big gear A with a handle drives a small gear B.

## Size the teeth
<!-- target: field:Tooth size -->
**Tooth size** is mm across the gear for each tooth: 2 to 3 is sturdy in 3 mm wood. Bigger teeth
make bigger gears.

## Set the teeth
<!-- target: button:Add a gear -->
Type each gear's **Teeth**: with 36 on A and 12 on B, B turns 3 times for each turn of A. **Add a
gear** puts another in the train.

## Watch it turn
<!-- target: text:Drag a part round -->
Drag a gear round to turn it by hand, or click along the strip under the picture. Green all the
way along means nothing jams.

## Read the check
<!-- target: text:Also needed -->
Under the picture: what the check found (red is a problem to fix, and says what to change), the
parts to cut and the dowels you need.

## Choose your material
<!-- target: field:Cut with -->
Pick your material's cut settings: its kerf goes on the cut layer, so the gears come out the size
they're drawn and the play stays as set.

## Put them in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Click **Put in the design**. The parts go on the layer **Mechanism · cut**, nested on the bed, with
their names on **Mechanism · marks**.
