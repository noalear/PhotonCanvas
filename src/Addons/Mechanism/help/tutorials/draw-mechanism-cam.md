# Make a cam that lifts and drops
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Shape a cam from steps (rise, wait, fall, drop), check the follower really follows it, and cut it. -->
<!-- page: mechanisms.md#cam-and-follower -->
<!-- order: 71 -->
A cam is a shaped wheel that lifts a follower as it turns. You say what the follower should do, step
by step; the mechanism maker shapes the cam and checks the follower really does it.

## Open the mechanism maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Mechanisms -->
<!-- wait: window:Mechanism maker -->
Click **Mechanisms** on the **Home** tab.

## Choose Cam and follower
<!-- target: tabitem:Cam and follower -->
Click the **Cam and follower** tab.

## Start from a motion
<!-- target: field:Start from -->
Pick a ready-made motion, such as **Slow rise, sudden drop**. Its steps fill the list under it.

## Change the steps
<!-- target: button:Add a step -->
Each step is a **Rise**, **Wait**, **Fall** or **Drop**, over so many degrees of the turn and so many
mm. Change any of them, or **Add a step**.

## Size the cam
<!-- target: field:Base radius -->
A bigger **Base radius** pushes the follower more gently. If the check says the cam pushes too far
off straight up, make it bigger.

## Pick the foot
<!-- target: field:Foot -->
A round foot follows dips and drops; a flat one can't get into dips. The chart shows **Planned**
against **On the cut cam**: where they part, the cam can't make that motion.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Click **Put in the design**. The cam, the follower and the backboard go on **Mechanism · cut**, the
arrow showing which way to turn it on **Mechanism · marks**.
