# Bend a panel to a shape
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a living hinge that bends a flat panel into a curve you draw. -->
<!-- page: bend-to-shape.md -->
<!-- order: 65 -->
Bend to shape makes a panel with slits only where it has to bend, closer where it bends tighter.
Draw the curve seen from above first, a rounded rectangle for example, and select it.

## Open Bend to shape
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/Bend to shape -->
<!-- wait: window:Bend to shape -->
Click **Bend to shape** on the **Import** tab.

## Choose the curve
<!-- target: field:Curve -->
**The selected shape** uses your selection. Or pick **Rounded rectangle** or **Oval** and type its size.

## Set the panel
<!-- target: field:Panel height -->
Set how tall the panel stands, the sheet's **Thickness** and its **Material**.

## Check the preview
<!-- target: text:lines of slits -->
The preview shows the curve from above: blue parts get slits, grey parts stay solid. Red parts bend too tight: round those corners, or make the **Slit length** longer.

## Make it
<!-- target: button:Make the panel -->
<!-- wait: closed:Bend to shape -->
Click **Make the panel**. The panel, its slits and two formers to glue it round go on your Cut layer.
