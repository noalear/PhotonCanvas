# Make a music box strip
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Turn a tune you type, or a MIDI file, into a punched strip for a paper-strip music box. -->
<!-- page: automata.md#music-box-strip -->
<!-- order: 75 -->
You need a hand-crank paper-strip music box movement (15 or 30 notes) and thin card the thickness of
the strips that came with it.

## Open the toy maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Mini-Designer/Automata -->
<!-- wait: window:Toys and automata -->
Click **Automata** on the **Mini-Designer** tab (in the **Mechanical** group).

## Choose Music box strip
<!-- target: tabitem:Music box strip -->
Click the **Music box strip** tab.

## Pick your box
<!-- target: field:Box -->
Choose your music box under **Box**, or **Another box** to give its strip's sizes.

## Cut a test strip first
<!-- target: field:Start from -->
**Every note, lowest first** plays each note once: cut it to check the holes line up with your box.

## Write the tune
<!-- target: button:Open a MIDI file -->
Type the notes in the box above (like `C5 D5 E5:2`), or **Open a MIDI file…** to read one in.

## Make it fit
<!-- target: button:Fit it to the box -->
**Fit it to the box** moves the tune to the key that leaves the fewest notes out.

## Check the strip
<!-- target: field:Beat -->
Watch the strip pass the comb. If repeated notes are too close, give each **Beat** more strip.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Click **Put in the design**. A long strip comes in pieces that lock together: tape over each join.
