# Make a music box comb
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Cut a music box comb from spring steel, each tine as long as its note needs, to play the strips you punch. -->
<!-- page: automata.md#music-box-comb -->
<!-- order: 76 -->
You need thin spring steel (blue-tempered 1095 shim, 0.5 mm is a good start), 3 mm steel plate for
the clamp bars, a few bolts and a fiber laser to cut them.

## Open the toy maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Designer/Automata -->
<!-- wait: window:Toys and automata -->
Click **Automata** on the **Designer** tab (in the **Toys** group).

## Choose Music box comb
<!-- target: tabitem:Music box comb -->
Click the **Music box comb** tab.

## Pick the strips it plays
<!-- target: field:Box -->
Choose the same box as your strips under **Box**: the tines are as far apart as the strip's lanes.

## Pick the metal
<!-- target: field:Metal -->
Choose your metal. Blue-tempered spring steel is already hardened; annealed steel must be hardened after cutting.

## Set the thickness
<!-- target: button:Suggest -->
Type your stock's thickness, or click **Suggest** for the shim size that gives tines a good length.

## Check the tines
<!-- target: field:Gap -->
The check lists every tine's note and length. Each tine is the lane spacing less the **Gap** wide.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Click **Put in the design**. The comb goes on one page and the two clamp bars, for thicker plate, on the next.
