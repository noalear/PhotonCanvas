# Make a box of moving figures
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a crank box whose cut-out figures bob, peck and spin when you turn the handle, checked moving before you cut. -->
<!-- page: automata.md#moving-figures -->
<!-- order: 74 -->
Turn the handle and the figures on top move: each one bobs, nods, pecks or spins. You need your
material's real thickness, a square dowel for the shaft and a few round ones.

## Open the toy maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Mini-Designer/Automata -->
<!-- wait: window:Toys and automata -->
Click **Automata** on the **Mini-Designer** tab (in the **Mechanical** group).

## Choose Moving figures
<!-- target: tabitem:Moving figures -->
Click the **Moving figures** tab. It starts with a fish that bobs, a bird that pecks and a dancer
that spins.

## Start from a ready-made box
<!-- target: field:Start from -->
**Start from** fills the box with figures: pecking birds, boats on the waves, a nodding cat and more.

## Change a figure
<!-- target: field:Moves -->
Open a figure's section to choose how it **Moves**, which **Figure** it is and how tall, and its cam,
lever or turntable.

## Add another
<!-- target: button:Add a figure -->
**Add a figure** puts another on the box (up to 6). Each gets its own space, so they never meet.

## Fit the box to your sheet
<!-- target: field:Thickness -->
Set your sheet's **Thickness**, or pick your material under **Cut with**: the slots are made for it.

## Watch it move
<!-- target: text:Drag a cam round -->
The box turns on the right, from the front, each cam from the side and the turntables from above.
Problems show red on the strip under it and in the list.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Click **Put in the design**. The parts go on as many beds as they need, a page each.
