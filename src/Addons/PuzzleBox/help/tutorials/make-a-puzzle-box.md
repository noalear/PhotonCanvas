# Make a puzzle box with a secret drawer
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a box whose sliding panels only come out in the right order, with a drawer hidden in its layers that a secret button unlocks. -->
<!-- page: puzzle-boxes.md -->
<!-- order: 78 -->
A puzzle box is glued up from layers of sheet. Its top is sliding panels hooked together
underneath, and a drawer hides in its bottom layers until you find the button inside.

## Open the puzzle box maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Designer/Puzzle box -->
<!-- wait: window:Puzzle box -->
Click **Puzzle box** on the **Designer** tab (in the **Toys** group).

## Pick the kind of box
<!-- target: text:Kind of box -->
In **Kind of box**, pick **Puzzle box with a secret drawer**. Set its **Width**, **Depth** and
**Height** under **Size**.

## Make the puzzle harder
<!-- target: field:Difficulty -->
Under **Top**, choose how many **Panels** and the **Difficulty**. **Shuffle** next to
**Variation** makes a different puzzle the same size.

## Step through opening it
<!-- target: tabitem:Put together -->
Press **▶** under the 3D view to see each move, the piece that moves picked out in orange. **How
it opens** lists them all: keep it to open the box later.

## Set the sheet
<!-- target: tabitem:Sheet -->
On the **Sheet** tab, type your sheet's **Thickness** and pick your material in **Cut with** so
the kerf is allowed for. Turn **Sliding fit** up if the panels come out stiff.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. Every layer is nested onto the bed, the letters that show the order
on a layer that runs before the cut.
