# Make a tavern's furniture
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make tables, chairs, benches and a chest for tabletop games that slot together, with wood grain etched on, and a door that hangs in a wall from the wall maker. -->
<!-- page: furniture.md -->
<!-- order: 76 -->
Flat-pack furniture cut from thin sheet slots together without tools, so a whole room of it is one
job on the laser.

## Open the furniture maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Furniture -->
<!-- wait: window:Mini furniture -->
Click **Furniture** on the **Home** tab.

## Set the sheet
<!-- target: tabitem:Sheet -->
On the **Sheet** tab, type your sheet's **Thickness** (2 mm suits 28 mm figures) and pick the
**Figures** size. Picking your material in **Cut with** fills the thickness and slot fit in for you.

## Or start from a theme
<!-- target: button:Start the list from this theme -->
Pick a **Theme** at the top and press **Start the list from this theme** for a room's worth that suits
it: bunks and lockers for a sci-fi ship, a cook fire with a roast turning on a spit, a torch on splayed legs and a tattered war banner, a treasure chest thrown open and heaped with gold, a straw training dummy and an anvil on a stump for a dungeon, tree-stump stools round a fire on a spit for a cave, a carved settle and a hooded great fireplace for a medieval hall. The faces change to
match (metal panels for sci-fi).

The ready-made **Town** pieces dress the square outside the tavern: the theme's town wall, a guard
tower, trees, a horse trough, a players' stage, a market stall, an outdoor bar and a notice board.

## Add furniture to the list
<!-- target: button:Add -->
Back on the **Furniture** tab, pick a **Chair** under the list and press **Add**. Add a **Bench** and
a **Chest** the same way.

## Make four of them
<!-- target: field:How many -->
With the chair picked on the list, type 4 in **How many**. Change its sizes and style underneath:
the preview follows.

## Look at it put together
<!-- target: tabitem:Put together -->
**Put together** shows the furniture picked in 3D: drag it round to check it from every side.
**Pieces** shows what will be cut. Some pieces are cut to glue on, so they stand proud the way real
ones do: a cupboard's doors and drawer fronts, the bosses on a candelabra, a clock's bezel and trunk door,
the arcade of arches across a wooden chest and the handles on its ends, the corbels under a stone
fireplace's mantel, the tracery across the top of a bookcase, the book on a lectern, the cloth on an altar
the frames, braces and rope handles on the sides of a crate, and the ring of stones, roast and cauldron round a cook fire.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. Every piece is nested onto the bed, the grain on layers that run
before the cut. Cut one chair first and change **Slot fit** if the tabs are too tight or too loose.
