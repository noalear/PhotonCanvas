# Turn a map into terrain
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Draw a floor plan or read a battle map, and get every wall, column, door and floor tile for it at once, ready to cut and stand up. -->
<!-- page: mapterrain.md -->
<!-- order: 78 -->
One window turns a floor plan into tabletop terrain: textured walls with doorways, the columns
they slide into, doors and jigsaw floor tiles.

## Open the map maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Designer/Map to terrain -->
<!-- wait: window:Map to terrain -->
Click **Map to terrain** on the **Designer** tab (in the **Town** group). It opens with a small dungeon to start from.

## Pick a look
<!-- target: field:Look -->
Pick a **Look**: dungeon, castle, ruins, an inn, a temple or sci-fi. It sets the walls' and the
floor's textures and the doors' shape.

## Draw the rooms
<!-- target: button:Room -->
With the **Room** tool, drag out rooms on the plan; the walls go round them by themselves. Use
**Wall** to add a wall between two rooms (or drag along one to take it away), and **Door** to click
a door into a wall.

## Or read a battle map
<!-- target: button:Open a picture… -->
To start from a battle map instead, press **Open a picture…** and pick it. The grid is found and
the floor read from it; check the plan and fix it with the tools.

## Set the sheet
<!-- target: tabitem:Walls -->
On the **Walls** tab, pick your material in **Cut with** (or type the sheet's **Thickness**), and
the walls' height, texture and doors.

## See it put together
<!-- target: tabitem:Put together -->
**Put together** shows the terrain in 3D: drag it to turn it round, and scroll to zoom in on a part (press **F** to see it all again).

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. Every piece goes in, nested onto the bed a page at a time. Cut a column
and the end of a wall first and change **Slot fit** if the wall is too tight or too loose in the slot.
