# Hollow a stacked model
<!-- category: 3D and slices -->
<!-- summary: Cut the middle out of each slice to save material and glue, with a channel for a wire and pockets for magnets. -->
<!-- page: hollow-models.md#hollow-a-model -->
<!-- order: 25 -->
A solid stack uses a lot of material that nobody sees. Hollowing keeps a shell round the outside and
cuts the middles out, and can leave room inside for a light or magnets.

## Open 3D to slices
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/3D to slices -->
<!-- wait: window:Stacked slices -->
Click **3D to slices** on the **Home** tab.

## Open a model
<!-- target: button:Open a 3D model… -->
Click **Open a 3D model…** and double-click a model. Press **Next** when it shows in the preview.

## Hollow it
<!-- target: button:Hollow it out -->
Tick **Hollow it out**. The preview shows the slices with their middles cut out.

## Choose the wall
<!-- target: field:Wall (mm) -->
How thick the shell is, sideways and at the top and bottom. 5 mm is a good start; thicker leaves more room for the pins.

## A way in for a wire
<!-- target: field:Wire / LED channel Ø -->
Type a size to get a hole up through the base into the hollow, for a wire or an LED. 0 = none.

## Magnets in the base
<!-- target: field:Magnet Ø -->
Type your magnets' size to get pockets for them in the base. Glue them in before the slice above goes on.

## See what it saves
<!-- target: text:Hollowed with -->
The summary says how many slices are hollow and how much material that saves.
