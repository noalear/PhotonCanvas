# Make leather or fabric pieces
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Seam allowance, stitch holes, notches and mirrored pairs for sewn goods. -->
<!-- page: leather-and-fabric.md -->
<!-- order: 80 -->
Turn the outlines of a wallet, pouch or sewing pattern into pieces ready to cut: the cut edge, the
stitch line, holes for sewing by hand, notches and a left and a right.

## Select the pieces
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Draw or import the outline of each piece, then select them all.

## Open Leather & fabric
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Leather & fabric -->
<!-- wait: window:Leather and fabric -->
Click **Leather & fabric** on the **Home** tab.

## Choose the material
<!-- target: field:Material -->
Pick what you're cutting. It sets the usual settings and says what to watch out for. PVC and vinyl must never be lasered.

## Which line did you draw
<!-- target: field:You drew -->
The cut edge (stitching goes inside it) or the sewing line (the cut edge goes outside it). Set how far apart under **Seam allowance**.

## Stitch holes
<!-- target: field:Stitch holes -->
Round holes, slanted slots like a pricking iron, or diamonds, with the **Stitch length** between them. Every corner gets a hole.

## A left and a right
<!-- target: button:Also make the mirror image -->
Tick it when you need each piece flipped as well, like the two sides of a bag.

## Make them
<!-- target: button:Make pieces -->
<!-- wait: closed:Leather and fabric -->
Click **Make pieces**. Set the power and speed on the new cut and hole layers before sending.
