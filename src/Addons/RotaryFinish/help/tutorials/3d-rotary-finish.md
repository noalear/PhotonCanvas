# Finish a stacked model on the rotary
<!-- category: 3D and slices -->
<!-- summary: Put a dowel up the middle of stacked slices, then engrave the glued stack down to the model's shape on the rotary. -->
<!-- page: rotary-finish.md#finish-a-stacked-model-on-the-rotary -->
<!-- order: 26 -->
A stack of slices has steps all over. Glued up on a dowel and turned in the rotary's chuck, the
laser can take the steps off, down to the model's real shape.

## Open 3D to slices
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/3D to slices -->
<!-- wait: window:Stacked slices -->
Click **3D to slices** on the **Import** tab.

## Open a model
<!-- target: button:Open a 3D model… -->
Click **Open a 3D model…** and double-click a model. Press **Next** when it shows in the preview.

## Keep the steps on the outside
<!-- target: text:Outer: sand the steps back to the shape -->
Choose **Outer: sand the steps back to the shape** for the outline, so the slices hold all of the model and the steps stick out.

## Turn it on
<!-- target: button:Finish it on the rotary -->
Tick **Finish it on the rotary**. Every slice gets a hole up the middle for the dowel.

## Choose the dowel
<!-- target: field:Centre dowel Ø -->
The dowel the chuck holds. 6 mm is a good start; a notch in its hole points to the side that faces the laser first.

## Choose the engrave settings
<!-- target: field:Engrave with -->
Pick settings with a measured depth a pass (Materials & Tests → Tests → Etch rate test). The depth fills in.

## The focus band
<!-- target: field:Focus band (mm) -->
How far the surface may be from the focus. The stack isn't round, so each band gets its own focus height.

## Read the summary
<!-- target: text:Rotary finish: -->
It says the Object Ø to set on the rotary, how much comes off, and at how many focus heights.

## Make it
<!-- target: button:Make the slices -->
Click **Make the slices**. A page "… on the rotary" comes after the slices' pages. Glue the stack up on the dowel, fit it in the chuck, set Work mode to the rotary, and focus with the notch pointing up.
