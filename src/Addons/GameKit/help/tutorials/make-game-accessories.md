# Make a dice tower and a movement gauge
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a dice tower with a tray, a movement gauge and other accessories for tabletop games that slot together. -->
<!-- page: game-accessories.md -->
<!-- order: 77 -->
Dice towers, card stands, initiative trackers and gauges all slot together from one sheet, so a
whole table's worth is one job on the laser.

## Open the game accessories maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Game kit -->
<!-- wait: window:Game accessories -->
Click **Game kit** on the **Home** tab.

## Set the sheet
<!-- target: tabitem:Sheet -->
On the **Sheet** tab, type your sheet's **Thickness** (3 mm plywood suits a dice tower) and pick
the **Faces** to etch on it. Picking your material in **Cut with** fills the thickness and slot fit
in for you.

## Size the tower for your dice
<!-- target: field:Biggest die -->
Back on the **Accessories** tab, the dice tower is on the list. Type the size of your biggest die in
**Biggest die**: the ramps and the gate are sized from it.

## Add a movement gauge
<!-- target: button:Add -->
Pick **Movement gauge** under the list and press **Add**. Choose what it's **Marked in** and how
many **Segments** it has.

## Look at it put together
<!-- target: tabitem:Put together -->
**Put together** shows the accessory picked in 3D: drag it round to check it from every side.
**Pieces** shows what will be cut.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. Every piece is nested onto the bed, the etching on layers that run
before the cut. Cut a small piece first and change **Slot fit** if the tabs are too tight or too
loose.
