# Make a time-lapse
<!-- category: Running jobs -->
<!-- summary: Photograph a job every few seconds while it runs and turn the photos into a video. -->
<!-- page: time-lapse.md -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
A time-lapse takes an overview camera photo every 5 seconds while a job runs and makes a video of
them at the end. You need the machine connected (**Connect** at the right end of the ribbon); the video needs ffmpeg installed (otherwise the
photos are kept).

## Turn it on
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Time-lapse -->
Click **Time-lapse**. It stays on for every job until you click it again.

## Run a job
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Ship It -->
Ship the job and start it on the machine as usual. The photos start when the job does.

## Find the video
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
When the job ends, the caption under the timeline says where it was saved: in your Pictures folder,
under *PhotonCanvas time-lapses*.
