# Base a unit and make its movement tray
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make ten 25 mm round bases with cracked earth etched on and magnet holes, and a movement tray that carries them in two rows. -->
<!-- page: bases.md -->
<!-- order: 78 -->
Laser-cut bases with the ground already etched on take a few minutes to paint, and a movement tray
cut to fit them moves the whole unit in one go.

## Open the base maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Bases -->
<!-- wait: window:Miniature bases -->
Click **Bases** on the **Home** tab.

## Choose the bases
<!-- target: tabitem:Bases -->
On the **Bases** tab, leave the shape on **Round**, pick **25 mm** in **Size** and type 10 in **How
many**. **Add a size** adds a line for a leader on a bigger base.

## Add magnets
<!-- target: field:Magnet size -->
Type your magnets' diameter in **Magnet size**. Each base gets a bottom layer with a hole for one.

## Choose the ground
<!-- target: tabitem:Texture -->
On the **Texture** tab, pick **Cracked earth**, or rocks, grass, flagstones or deck plates. Every
base gets its own layout.

## Make the tray
<!-- target: tabitem:Tray -->
On the **Tray** tab, tick **Make a movement tray**, press **Same as the first size** and set five
bases **Across** in two **Rows**.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. The pieces are nested onto the bed, the texture on an etch layer that
runs before the cut. Cut one base and the tray first and change **Clearance** if the bases are too
tight or too loose in their pockets.
