# Carve a portrait from a photo
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Turn a photo of a face or a pet into a relief on an oval plaque, and put its depth map in the design to carve. -->
<!-- page: photorelief.md -->
<!-- order: 82 -->
Photo relief raises the subject of a photo off its background and rounds it, keeps its features as
carved detail, and puts it on a plaque. A photo against a plain wall or sheet works best. Carving
depth slices hasn't been tried on the machine yet: try a small one on scrap first.

## Open Photo relief
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/Photo relief -->
<!-- wait: window:Photo to relief -->
Select the photo in your design (or nothing), then click **Photo relief** on the **Import** tab (in
the **3D & maps** group).

## Pick the photo
<!-- target: button:Open photo -->
If the photo isn't already showing, click **Open photo…** and pick it.

## Choose a style
<!-- target: field:Style -->
Choose **Portrait** for a face, **Pet** for an animal, or **Coin or medal** for a small round one.
The picture on the right shows it as carved wood.

## Check the subject
<!-- target: field:Show as -->
Set **Show as** to **Depth map**: the subject should be lighter than the flat grey field round it.
If bits of background are left, switch on **Click the background** and click them, or raise the
**Tolerance**.

## Place the photo
<!-- target: tabitem:Shape -->
On the **Shape** tab, pick the **Plaque** and its **Width (mm)**. Drag the picture to move the photo
on the plaque, and scroll on it to zoom in on the face.

## Tune the carving
<!-- target: tabitem:Carving -->
On the **Carving** tab, raise **Fine detail** for crisper hair and features, lower **Broad shading**
if one side of the face is in shadow, and set **Depth (mm)** to how deep you mean to carve.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
<!-- wait: closed:Photo to relief -->
Click **Put in the design**. The depth map goes on a new Picture layer in **Depth slices (relief)**
mode: set its slices, power and passes there, and carve a test on scrap first.
