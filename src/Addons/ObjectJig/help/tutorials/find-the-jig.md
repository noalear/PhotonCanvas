# Use a jig again
<!-- category: Camera -->
<!-- summary: Lay a jig anywhere on the bed: the camera finds it and lines the design up with it. -->
<!-- page: object-jig.md#find-the-jig -->
<!-- order: 81 -->
A jig picked up and put back, or one made of stacked sheets, isn't where it was cut. The camera
finds it, any way round, and moves the design onto it.

## Show the objects page
<!-- target: canvas -->
Show the jig's objects page (the one with the jig drawn in grey). For a new design on a saved jig,
make one with **Object jig** and **Open a saved jig…**.

## Lay the jig down
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
Lay the jig on the bed with the objects in it and nothing touching it, and measure the focus on its
top.

## Open Find the jig
<!-- target: ribbon:Camera/Find the jig -->
<!-- wait: window:Find the jig -->
Click **Find the jig** on the **Camera** tab.

## Find it
<!-- target: button:Take photo and find the jig -->
Click **Take photo and find the jig**. Check the green outline sits on the jig in the photo.

## Apply
<!-- target: button:Apply -->
<!-- wait: closed:Find the jig -->
Click **Apply**: the design moves onto the jig. Measure the focus on an object, then **Ship It**.
