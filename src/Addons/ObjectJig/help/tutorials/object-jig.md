# Make a jig for an odd-shaped object
<!-- category: Camera -->
<!-- summary: Photograph an object, cut a jig that holds it, and mark the design in the same spot on every one. -->
<!-- page: object-jig.md#object-jig -->
<!-- order: 80 -->
For pens, phone cases, knife handles and other odd shapes: the camera outlines the object and a
sheet is cut with a pocket it drops into, so the design lands in the same place every time. Not yet
tried on real objects.

## Connect and lay the object down
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
This needs the machine connected and the camera calibrated (the
[Calibrate the camera](tutorial:cam-calibrate) tutorial). Lay the object flat on the bed, on its own.

## Place the design (optional)
<!-- target: canvas -->
Put the design over the object's camera picture where you want it marked, and select it.

## Open Object jig
<!-- target: ribbon:Camera/Object jig -->
<!-- wait: window:Object jig -->
Click **Object jig** on the **Camera** tab.

## Find the object
<!-- target: button:Take photo and find the object -->
Check **Distance (mm)** and **Height (mm)**, then click **Take photo and find the object**.
Its outline shows in green in the preview.

## Choose how the jig is made
<!-- target: field:Made as -->
**One sheet, cut through** is quickest. Set **Sheet (mm)**, the **Gap (mm)** round the object, and
**Across** and **Down** for a batch.

## Make it
<!-- target: button:Make the jig -->
<!-- wait: closed:Object jig -->
Click **Make the jig**. New pages are added: one to cut the jig, and one with the design on every
object.

## Cut, fill, mark
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
Ship the cut page and leave the sheet where it is. Put the objects in, show the objects page,
measure the focus on an object, and **Ship It** again.
