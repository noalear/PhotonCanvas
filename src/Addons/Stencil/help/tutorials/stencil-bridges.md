# Stencil bridges
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Keep the middles of O, A, R and B in a cut-out stencil. -->
<!-- page: stencil-bridges.md -->
<!-- order: 170 -->
Cut out of a sheet as a stencil, the middle of an O or an A falls out with the letter. Stencil
bridges keeps short strips of the sheet across those letters, so the middles stay.

## Select the letters
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select a text, or shapes with holes, on a Cut layer.

## Open Stencil bridges
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Stencil bridges -->
<!-- wait: window:Stencil bridges -->
Click **Stencil bridges**. The bridges are placed for you: two across each letter with a middle.

## Set the bridge width
<!-- target: field:Bridge width -->
Type how wide each bridge is, in mm. Wider is stronger but shows more.

## Choose the direction
<!-- target: field:Direction -->
The shortest way across each letter, or up and down for the classic stencil look.

## Change any by hand
<!-- target: window:Stencil bridges -->
Click in a letter to add a bridge there, or click a bridge to take it away. Middles shown in red
would still fall out.

## Keep them
<!-- target: button:Keep bridges -->
<!-- wait: closed:Stencil bridges -->
Click **Keep bridges**. Open Stencil bridges again on the same letters to change the bridges or
take them out.
