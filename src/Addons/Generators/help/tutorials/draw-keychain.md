# Make a keychain or tag
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Draw a keychain or gift tag with its ring hole and engraved words. -->
<!-- page: generators.md#keychain-and-tag -->
<!-- order: 62 -->
A keychain or gift tag with its ring hole and, if you like, engraved words, ready to cut. The words
can hold a serial number or a name from a spreadsheet, so each tag can be different.

## Open the generators
<!-- target: ribbon:Import/Generators -->
<!-- wait: window:Generators -->
Click **Generators** on the **Import** tab.

## Choose Keychain / tag
<!-- target: tabitem:Keychain / tag -->
Click the **Keychain / tag** tab.

## Pick a shape
<!-- target: field:Shape -->
Choose **Rounded rectangle**, **Circle** or **Tag** (a luggage tag with a pointed end), and set its **Width** and **Height**.

## Place the hole
<!-- target: field:Hole Ø -->
Set **Hole Ø** for the key ring (0 = no hole) and how far it is **from the edge**.

## Add words
Type words in the box below (optional). They're fitted into the tag beside the hole, on an Engrave layer.

## Make it
<!-- target: button:Make it -->
<!-- wait: closed:Generators -->
Click **Make it**: the tag appears in the middle of the work area.
