# Make a pair of earrings
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Draw a matching pair of earring blanks with holes for the hooks. -->
<!-- page: generators.md#earrings -->
<!-- order: 66 -->
A matching pair of earring blanks with holes for the hooks: teardrops, leaves, circles, hexagons or
arches. Cut them from wood or acrylic, then engrave or paint them.

## Open the generators
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Generators -->
<!-- wait: window:Generators -->
Click **Generators** on the **Home** tab.

## Choose Earrings
<!-- target: tabitem:Earrings -->
Click the **Earrings** tab.

## Pick a shape
<!-- target: field:Shape -->
Choose a **Shape** and set each earring's **Width** and **Height**.

## Size the hook hole
<!-- target: field:Hook hole Ø -->
Set **Hook hole Ø** and its distance **from the top**. On a pointed top, keep the hole low enough not to break through.

## Make them
<!-- target: button:Make a pair -->
<!-- wait: closed:Generators -->
Click **Make a pair**: two earrings appear side by side, the right one mirrored.
