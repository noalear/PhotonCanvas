# Find the gap for your woods
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Cut star holes at different gaps and see which star goes in snug. -->
<!-- page: inlay-marquetry.md -->
<!-- order: 51 -->
How tight an inlay fits depends on the woods, the glue and the cut. The fit test cuts a row of
star holes, each a different gap bigger, and a star for each: the snug one is your gap.

## Open Inlay and marquetry
<!-- target: ribbon:Tests/Inlay and marquetry -->
<!-- wait: window:Inlay and marquetry -->
Click **Tests** on the **Materials & Tests** tab, pick **Inlay and marquetry** and click **Open**.
Nothing needs to be selected.

## Choose what they go into
<!-- target: field:Goes into -->
A pocket or holes, with the board's or sheet's settings, as for the real inlay.

## The wood's kerf
<!-- target: field:Kerf (mm) -->
Pick the wood's cut recipe or type its kerf: the stars are cut with it allowed for.

## Make the test
<!-- target: button:Fit test -->
<!-- wait: closed:Inlay and marquetry -->
Two pages are added: the holes (or pockets) with their gaps marked, and the stars.

## Try them
<!-- target: canvas -->
Burn and cut both. Push a star into each hole, and type the snuggest gap into **Gap (mm)** next
time: the window remembers it.
