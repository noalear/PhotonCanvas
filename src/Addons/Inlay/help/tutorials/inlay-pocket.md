# Inlay veneer into a board
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Engrave a pocket in a board and cut veneer pieces that sit in it flush. -->
<!-- page: inlay-marquetry.md -->
<!-- order: 50 -->
An inlay needs a pocket as deep as the veneer and pieces exactly the pocket's size. Run the
**Etch rate** test on the board and measure the veneer's kerf first: then both come out right.

## Select the shapes
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select the closed shapes to inlay. Put the shapes of each wood on a layer of their own.

## Open Inlay and marquetry
<!-- target: ribbon:Tests/Inlay and marquetry -->
<!-- wait: window:Inlay and marquetry -->
Click **Tests** on the **Materials & Tests** tab, pick **Inlay and marquetry** and click **Open**.

## Into a pocket
<!-- target: field:Goes into -->
Choose **A pocket engraved in a board**.

## How thick the veneer is
<!-- target: field:Pieces (mm) -->
Measure the veneer with calipers: the pocket goes this deep.

## The board's settings
<!-- target: field:Engrave with -->
Pick your etch-rate recipe for the board: its depth a pass sets how many passes.

## Each wood's kerf
<!-- target: field:Kerf (mm) -->
Pick each wood's cut recipe under **Cut with**, or type its kerf: the pieces come out the drawn size.

## The gap
<!-- target: field:Gap (mm) -->
0.05 mm leaves room for glue. The fit test finds the best gap for your woods.

## Make them
<!-- target: button:Make the pieces -->
<!-- wait: closed:Inlay and marquetry -->
The pocket and each wood's pieces go on pages of their own after your design.

## Burn, then cut
<!-- target: canvas -->
Burn the **Inlay · pocket** page into the board. Then cut each wood's page from that wood, put
the pieces in by their numbers, glue and sand flush.
