# Cut a marquetry picture
<!-- category: Editing shapes -->
<!-- summary: Cut a picture of several woods into a background veneer, every piece to fit. -->
<!-- page: inlay-marquetry.md -->
<!-- order: 175 -->
In marquetry each wood is cut separately and the pieces go into a background veneer like a
jigsaw. Draw each wood on its own layer: shapes on top cut away what's under them.

## Select the picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select all the shapes of the picture.

## Open Inlay
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Inlay -->
<!-- wait: window:Inlay and marquetry -->
Click **Inlay** on the **Home** tab. The preview shows each piece in its wood's colour, numbered.

## Into a background veneer
<!-- target: field:Goes into -->
Choose **Holes cut through a sheet**, and name the background veneer under **Sheet**.

## Name the woods
<!-- target: field:Wood -->
Each layer is one wood: type its name and pick how it's cut. Each wood gets a page of its own.

## Pack the pieces
<!-- target: text:Pack the pieces close together -->
Tick it to cut each wood's pieces in rows close together. A key page shows where each goes.

## Turn them over
<!-- target: text:Turn the pieces over -->
Ticked, the pieces are cut mirrored: turn each over and the joints close up on the face.

## Make them
<!-- target: button:Make the pieces -->
<!-- wait: closed:Inlay and marquetry -->
The background and each wood go on pages of their own after your picture.
