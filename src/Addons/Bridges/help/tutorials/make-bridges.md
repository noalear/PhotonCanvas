# Make a footbridge and a stone bridge
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make an arched plank footbridge with a railing, then a stone arch bridge with stone etched on, both cut from flat sheet and slotted together. -->
<!-- page: bridges.md -->
<!-- order: 77 -->
A bridge cut from thin sheet slots together without tools: the planks drop into notches in its
sides, a stone bridge's road and inside walls tab into its side walls.

## Open the bridge maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Mini-Designer/Bridges -->
<!-- wait: window:Miniature bridges -->
Click **Bridges** on the **Mini-Designer** tab (in the **Terrain** group).

## Pick a plank bridge
<!-- target: field:Kind -->
Choose **Plank bridge** in **Kind**. It starts at a size to suit 28 mm figures.

## Pick a theme
<!-- target: field:Theme -->
**Theme** under **Kind** gives the bridge a look: *Sci-fi* turns a plank bridge into a metal gantry
with grating and rivets, *Cave* roughens its planks. Leave it on *Medieval* for now.

## Make it arch
<!-- target: field:Arch rise -->
Type 10 in **Arch rise** to lift its middle 10 mm above its ends (0 makes it level). The preview
follows every change.

## Look at it put together
<!-- target: tabitem:Put together -->
**Put together** shows the bridge in 3D: drag it round to check it from every side. **Pieces**
shows what will be cut: two sides and the planks.

## Set the sheet
<!-- target: tabitem:Sheet -->
On the **Sheet** tab, type your sheet's **Thickness**, or pick your material in **Cut with** to fill
in the thickness and slot fit from your cut test.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. Every piece is nested onto the bed, the grain on a layer that runs
before the cut. Stand the sides up, slide each plank through the railing and drop it into its
notches.

## Now a stone bridge
<!-- target: tabitem:Bridge -->
Back on the **Bridge** tab, choose **Stone arch bridge** in **Kind**. Set the **Arches** and their
**Arch width**, pick a **Road** (level, or humped with ramps) and a **Texture** for the stone. If
there are walls from the wall maker in the design, **Match the walls in the design** uses their
stone. Tick **Carve as a relief** on the **Sheet** tab to carve the stones in depth instead of
etching them flat (much slower). **Update in the design** swaps the footbridge for it; to keep both, close the window and
click **Bridges** again for a new bridge.
