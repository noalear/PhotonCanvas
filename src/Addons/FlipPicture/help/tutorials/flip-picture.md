# A flip picture
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Two to four pictures in one polished stainless plate, each showing from its own tilt. -->
<!-- page: flip-picture.md -->
<!-- order: 61 -->
Weave two to four pictures into one mirror stainless plate as diffraction gratings with different
directions: tilt it one way and you see one picture, the other way another. Run a grating test first
(Laser → Colour test, Diffraction gratings) to find your power and pitch.

## Select the pictures
<!-- target: canvas -->
Shift-click two to four pictures of the same shape. The one you added first is picture A: the plate
goes where it is. No pictures yet? **Picture → Import picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Open Flip picture
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Flip picture -->
<!-- wait: window:Flip picture -->
Click **Flip picture**. The preview shows the woven patches, one colour per picture.

## Set the patch size
<!-- target: field:Patch (mm) -->
Smaller patches keep more detail but take longer.

## Use your grating test
<!-- target: field:Settings from test -->
If you measured a grating test from the five views, pick it: each picture gets the pitch and power
that showed best from its side.

## Set the directions
<!-- target: field:Direction ° -->
Each picture's scan direction. The line under it says which way to tilt. Keep them far apart.

## Check each tilt
<!-- target: field:Preview -->
Choose **Tilted to show A** (B, …) to see roughly how the plate looks from that side.

## Add the layers
<!-- target: button:Add flip layers -->
<!-- wait: closed:Flip picture -->
Click **Add flip layers**: one MOPA layer per picture, over picture A.
