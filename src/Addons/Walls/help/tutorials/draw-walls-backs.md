# Engrave both sides of a single-panel wall
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Give a single-panel wall another texture on its back, cut it, turn the pieces over and engrave the backs, lined up on each piece with the camera. -->
<!-- page: walls.md -->
<!-- order: 76 -->
A single sheet wall can have a different look on each side: stone outside, wood panelling inside.
The front is engraved and cut as usual; then you turn the pieces over and run a second job that
only engraves their backs.

## Open the wall maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Mini-Designer/Walls -->
<!-- wait: window:Textured walls -->
Click **Walls** on the **Mini-Designer** tab (in the **Buildings** group).

## Make it a single panel
<!-- target: field:Build it as -->
Set **Build it as** to **A flat panel**, and leave **Two panels back to back** off.

## Pick the front's texture
<!-- target: tabitem:Texture -->
On the **Texture** tab, pick the **Texture** for the front.

## Turn on the back
<!-- target: button:Engrave the back too -->
Under **Back of the wall**, tick **Engrave the back too**.

## Pick the back's texture
<!-- target: field:Back texture -->
Pick the **Back texture** and its **Back wear %**.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. The sheet's page gets the front and the cut as usual, and a page after
it, "backs (turn the pieces over)", holds each piece's back, mirrored, with its outline on a guide
layer that isn't burned.

## Cut, turn over and line up
<!-- target: ribbon:Placement/Fit to piece -->
Run the sheet. Turn the pieces over left to right and lay them on the bed, then show the backs page.
Drag each piece's group onto its piece in the camera picture, select it and click **Fit to piece**:
only the outline is matched, and the engraving moves with it. Then run the backs page.
