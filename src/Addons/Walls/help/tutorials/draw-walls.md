# Make a brick wall with a door and columns
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a brick wall for a tabletop game with battlements, a door and a window, and the corner columns that join walls into a room, and pick a theme (dungeon, cave, sci-fi, desert, gothic) for other looks. -->
<!-- page: walls.md -->
<!-- order: 75 -->
Walls cut from sheet with a texture etched on make quick, sturdy scenery for tabletop games. Each
wall is a box that stands up on its own, and columns join the walls into rooms.

## Open the wall maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Mini-Designer/Walls -->
<!-- wait: window:Textured walls -->
Click **Walls** on the **Mini-Designer** tab (in the **Buildings** group).

## Pick a theme
<!-- target: field:Theme -->
**Theme** at the top sets the texture, door, windows and accessories for a look: *Dungeon*, *Cave*,
*Sci-fi*, *Desert* or *Gothic*. Leave it on *Medieval* for this brick wall; the other terrain makers
have the same themes, so a set made in one theme matches.

## Size the wall
<!-- target: field:Length -->
Type the wall's **Length** and **Height**. Leave **Build it as** on a box.

## Set the thicknesses
<!-- target: field:Wall thickness -->
**Wall thickness** is the whole wall, front to back: 12 mm suits 28 mm figures. **Panel thickness**
is your sheet's, and every slot and tab is made to fit it. Picking your material in **Cut with**
fills it in for you.

## Choose a texture
<!-- target: tabitem:Texture -->
On the **Texture** tab, pick **Brick** (or stone, wood, metal), or make your own from shapes you've
drawn. **Wear %** sets how weathered it looks (0 new and crisp, 100 ancient and battered) and
**Detail size** how fine its pits, grain and bolts are. The preview shows it on the wall. For the
most real look, set **Etch as** to **Relief**: the texture is carved in depth from a greyscale depth
map instead of etched flat (much slower; set its depth on the **Walls · relief** layer).

## Add battlements
<!-- target: tabitem:Top -->
On the **Top** tab, choose **Battlements (parapet)** for gaps along the top, and tick **Walkway
behind the battlements** for somewhere to stand figures. **Put together** on the right shows the
wall built; drag it round to see the walkway. **Merlon heights** can make every other merlon taller.

## Break it for a ruin (if you like)
<!-- target: field:Broken -->
Still on **Top**, **Broken** turns the wall into a ruin: *Crumbled*, a *Breach*, a *Fallen end* and
more, with or without battlements. On brick the break follows the joints, taking whole bricks away.
Leave it on *Not broken* for a whole wall.

## Add a door and a window
<!-- target: tabitem:Doors & windows -->
On **Doors & windows**, choose a **Door** and its shape, and type how many **Windows**. They keep
clear of each other and of the wall's ends.

## Make the door open
<!-- target: field:In the windows -->
Choose *Doorway and a door to fit it* and the door is cut out too: it hangs on pins inside the wall
and swings open. Under **Inside the openings**, pick which side it's hinged on, and what goes in the
windows: a frame with its bars, a shutter, or *Shutters that open*.

## Hang a torch or a shelf
<!-- target: button:Add a mount point -->
On **Mounts**, press **Add a mount point** and pick its **Accessory**: a torch sconce, a peg for a
shield, or a shelf on brackets. Drag it about on the wall in the **Pieces** view to put it where you
want it.

## Join walls with columns
<!-- target: tabitem:Connectors -->
On **Connectors**, type how many columns of each kind you need: four **Corner (90°)** make a square
room. Each column is a box with slots for the walls' tabs; pick its **Top** and **Bottom**.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. The pieces are nested onto the bed, the texture on an etch layer that
runs before the cut. Cut one wall and a column first, and change **Slot fit** if the tabs are too
tight or too loose.
