# Warp a design
<!-- category: Arranging -->
<!-- summary: Taper, arch, bulge or wave a design, or correct it for a round object. -->
<!-- page: warp.md -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
Warp bends a design: a taper, an arch for a name, a wave, or a correction so it comes out true on a
mug or a tapered tumbler marked without a rotary. You see it change as you go.

## Select the design
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Select the shapes or text to warp. Pictures aren't warped.

## Open Warp
<!-- target: ribbon:Arrange/Warp -->
<!-- wait: window:Warp -->
Click **Warp**.

## Choose the kind
<!-- target: field:Warp -->
Perspective, arch, bulge, wave, a round object, a cone, or four corners moved by hand.

## Set the amount
<!-- target: window:Warp -->
Drag the slider, or type the sizes, under it. The design on the work area changes as you go.

## Keep it
<!-- target: button:Warp -->
<!-- wait: closed:Warp -->
Click **Warp**. Text becomes plain outlines.
