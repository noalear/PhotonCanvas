# Even out an engraving on wood grain
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Map the board's grain from the camera picture so a photo or fill engraves evenly across it. -->
<!-- page: grain.md -->
<!-- order: 90 -->
Wood grain burns unevenly, so engravings come out striped. Map the grain with the camera and the
power follows it: less where the grain would come out darker.

## Put the board on the bed
<!-- target: panel:Machine -->
This needs the machine connected and the camera calibrated. Put the board on the bed under where
you'll engrave, and close the lid.

## Open Grain
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Grain -->
<!-- wait: window:Grain-aware engraving -->
Click **Grain** on the **Picture** tab.

## Take a camera picture
<!-- target: button:Take a camera picture -->
Click **Take a camera picture**. Nothing moves and the laser stays off.

## Map the grain
<!-- target: button:Map the grain -->
Leave **Map over** at **Around the design** and click **Map the grain**. The line above says how
strongly the grain shows and which way it runs.

## Choose the layer
<!-- target: field:Layer -->
Pick the Engrave or Picture layer to even out.

## Even it out
<!-- target: field:Grain -->
Set **Grain** to **Even it out**. **Strength** is how much less power at most: 25 % is a fair start.
Set **Show** to the layer's power to see where it's lowered.

## Measure it (optional)
<!-- target: button:Add a grain test -->
For your wood's own strength, click **Add a grain test**, burn the two strips on the new page, then
**Read the test** and **Use the test's settings**.

## Ship It
<!-- target: panel:Job -->
Ship the job as usual. The power follows the grain on every pass.
