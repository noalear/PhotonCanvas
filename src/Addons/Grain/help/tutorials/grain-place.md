# Place a picture on the grain
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Move a picture to where its dark parts fall on dark grain, so the wood's figure becomes part of it. -->
<!-- page: grain.md#place-a-picture-on-the-grain -->
<!-- order: 91 -->
An artistic use of the grain map: the picture goes where the board's own pattern matches it best,
and the grain becomes part of the engraving.

## Pick the board's outline
<!-- target: ribbon:Placement/Pick piece -->
<!-- wait: tool:Wand -->
With the board on the bed and the camera picture showing (**Show** in the machine panel), click **Pick piece** on the **Placement** tab (or press `W`), then click inside the board. Keep its outline selected.

## Open Grain
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Grain -->
<!-- wait: window:Grain-aware engraving -->
Click **Grain** on the **Picture** tab.

## Map the board
<!-- target: field:Map over -->
Set **Map over** to **The selected shapes (the board's outline)**, then click **Map the grain**.

## Select the picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click the picture on the canvas.

## Find the best place
<!-- target: button:Find the best place -->
Tick **Allow quarter turns** if it may be turned, then click **Find the best place**. The match is
from −1 to 1; above about 0.3 the grain clearly helps. `Ctrl+Z` puts it back.

## Bring out the grain
<!-- target: field:Grain -->
Set the picture's layer to **Bring out the grain** to make the most of it.
