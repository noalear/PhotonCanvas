# A colour picture on metal
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: A picture in the colours your stainless or titanium plate really made, one layer per colour. -->
<!-- page: colour-on-metal.md -->
<!-- order: 50 -->
The MOPA laser colours stainless steel and titanium. This turns a picture into the colours your own
plate made, one layer per colour. You need a measured palette first (Materials & Tests → Tests → Colour test, then
Measure colours), and **Colour on metal** ticked in Settings → Equipment (it is at first).

## Select a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click a picture on the work area. No picture yet? **Import → Picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Open Colour on metal
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Colour on metal -->
<!-- wait: window:Colour picture on metal -->
Click **Colour on metal**. The picture and a simulation in your measured colours show side by side.

## Choose the palette
<!-- target: field:Palette -->
Pick the palette measured on the same kind of plate.

## Set the dot size
<!-- target: field:Dot size (mm) -->
Smaller dots show finer detail, but heat makes neighbouring colours bleed: 0.2–0.5 mm is a good
start.

## Fewer colours
<!-- target: text:Merge colours closer than -->
Raise it for fewer layers. **Colours used** and the summary show the colours and the time.

## Add the layers
<!-- target: button:Add colour layers -->
<!-- wait: closed:Colour picture on metal -->
Click **Add colour layers**: one Engrave layer per colour, with the settings that made it.
