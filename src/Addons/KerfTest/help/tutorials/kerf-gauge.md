# Measure the kerf with a gauge
<!-- category: Materials and tests -->
<!-- summary: Cut a tiny tapered-slot gauge and read the kerf and slot fit from a photo, so joints fit first time. -->
<!-- page: kerf-gauge.md -->
<!-- order: 25 -->
Parts drawn to fit come out loose because the beam takes a kerf away. The kerf gauge measures it
with two keys pushed into tapered slots and a photo, then saves it on your material for joints,
slices and inlays.

## Open the kerf gauge
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Kerf gauge -->
<!-- wait: window:Kerf gauge -->
Click **Kerf gauge** on the **Machine** tab.

## Choose the cut settings
<!-- target: field:Cut with -->
Pick the cut recipe you'll build with under **Cut with**. The kerf goes back onto it.

## Set the thickness
<!-- target: field:Thickness (mm) -->
Type what the sheet is sold as. The bottom slot is drawn around it.

## Add it and cut it
<!-- target: button:Add the gauge to the design -->
<!-- wait: closed:Kerf gauge -->
Click **Add the gauge to the design**, then frame and send it as usual.

## Push the keys in
<!-- target: ribbon:Machine/Kerf gauge -->
<!-- wait: window:Kerf gauge -->
Lift out the window piece and the keys without moving the sheet. Push one key lying flat into the top slot, and slide the other, standing on its edge, along the bottom slot until each stops. Then open **Kerf gauge** again.

## Take a photo
<!-- target: button:Close-up photo -->
Click **Close-up photo**, or **Open a photo…** for a phone photo and click the top slot's mouth and closed end.

## Check the markers
<!-- target: field:Top scale: kerf (mm) -->
Each slot gets a marker where its key stops: drag one if it isn't at the tip. The kerf and slot fit are shown below.

## Save it
<!-- target: button:Save to the library -->
Choose the material and click **Save to the library**. The box generator, slicer, dowel pins and inlay now use it.
