# One design across several pieces
<!-- category: Work area -->
<!-- summary: Split a picture or sign across boards that go together, then mark each one lined up by the camera. -->
<!-- page: split-pieces.md -->
<!-- order: 55 -->
A design can run across several separate pieces, such as four 200 × 75 mm boards making one
400 × 150 mm picture. Each piece gets a page with its share, and the camera lines each one up with
the pieces done before it. You need the pieces, their sizes, and the machine connected with the
camera calibrated.

## Draw the pieces
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Rectangle -->
<!-- wait: tool:Rect -->
Draw a rectangle the size of each piece (type its **W** and **H** in the Selection panel).

## Make them material
<!-- target: canvas -->
Select the rectangles, right-click → **Use as material outline**. Lay them out edge to edge as the
pieces will go together; they may run off the bed.

## Put the design on them
<!-- target: canvas -->
Place the design over the pieces where you want it, on the layers you'll mark with.

## Select it all
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: selection -->
Drag a box round the design and the pieces.

## Split
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Split onto pieces -->
<!-- wait: window:Line up the pieces -->
Click **Split onto pieces** on the **Laser** tab. A page for each piece appears above the work area,
and **Line up the pieces** opens.

## Check a page
<!-- target: tab:Home -->
Piece 1's page shows its share of the design; the rest is greyed on **Other pieces**, which is never
sent. The page has been moved so the piece is in the work area.

## Set the height
<!-- target: field:Distance (mm) -->
Type the distance to the top of the pieces, or click **Auto-focus now** with a piece under the head.

## Lay the piece down
<!-- target: window:Line up the pieces -->
Put piece 1 on the bed under its outline. While it's following, the camera moves the design onto it.
Wait for "✓ Lined up and steady".

## Ship it
<!-- target: ribbon:Laser/Ship It -->
Click **Ship It** and press the machine's start button.

## The next piece
<!-- target: button:Next piece -->
Click **Next piece**. Lay piece 2 under its outline with piece 1 beside it as they'll go together,
wait for steady, take piece 1 away if it's in the laser's way, and ship. Do the same for each piece.

## Line up later
<!-- target: ribbon:Camera/Line up pieces -->
Closed the window? **Line up pieces** on the **Camera** tab opens it again on the page shown.
