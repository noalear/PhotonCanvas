# Make an upper floor with stairs
<!-- category: Drawing -->
<!-- summary: Make a floor that sits on a room of textured walls, with an opening and a flight of stairs up to it. -->
<!-- page: stairs.md -->
<!-- order: 77 -->
A floor plate on top of a room of walls makes a second storey; stairs or a ladder through an
opening in it take figures up. Make the walls first with **Walls**, so the floor can be fitted to them.

## Open the floor and stairs maker
<!-- target: ribbon:Home/Stairs & floors -->
<!-- wait: window:Floors and stairs -->
Click **Stairs & floors** on the **Home** tab.

## Fit it to your walls
<!-- target: button:Match the walls in the design -->
Press **Match the walls in the design**: the walls' height, length, sheet and columns are filled in
from the walls you made. Or type them in.

## Choose the floor
<!-- target: tabitem:Floor -->
On the **Floor** tab, pick how far the **Edge** reaches and the **Texture**: planks for floorboards,
stone blocks for flagstones. A floor bigger than the bed can be cut in pieces.

## Choose the way up
<!-- target: tabitem:Way up -->
On **Way up**, pick **Open stairs**, solid or spiral stairs, or a ladder. The stairs climb exactly to
the floor's top; the opening is sized to fit them.

## Look at it put together
<!-- target: tabitem:Put together -->
The picture shows the floor and stairs in the room. Drag to turn it, and tick **Lift the floor off**
to see the stairs under it.

## Put it in the design
<!-- target: button:Put in the design -->
Press **Put in the design**. The pieces are nested onto the bed, the texture on an etch layer that
runs before the cut. Cut a stair side and one step first, and change **Slot fit** if the fit is wrong.
