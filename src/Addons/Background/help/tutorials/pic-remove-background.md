# Remove a background
<!-- category: Pictures -->
<!-- summary: Make a picture's backdrop see-through so it isn't engraved. -->
<!-- page: remove-background.md -->
<!-- order: 150 -->
A photo of an object or a scanned logo often has a plain backdrop you don't want burned. Remove
background makes it see-through, with no AI and nothing sent online.

## Select a picture
<!-- target: canvas -->
<!-- wait: picture-selected -->
Click a picture on the work area. No picture yet? **Import → Picture** (`Ctrl+J`).

## Open Remove background
<!-- target: ribbon:Picture/Remove background -->
<!-- wait: window:Remove background -->
Click **Remove background**. The checkerboard shows what will be see-through.

## Set the tolerance
<!-- target: field:Tolerance -->
Raise it if bits of backdrop remain; lower it if the subject's edges get eaten.

## Click to remove more
<!-- target: field:Clicks remove -->
Click the preview on a colour still left: just that patch, or that colour everywhere.

## Clean the edge
<!-- target: field:Shrink edge -->
**Shrink edge** drops a halo of backdrop colour; **Soften edge** feathers the cut.

## Apply
<!-- target: button:Apply -->
<!-- wait: closed:Remove background -->
Click **Apply**. `Ctrl+Z` undoes it.
